# Hanulas (The Merchant of Riga)

**True Name**: Hanulas (Hanul) of Riga [Record]; the Shadow-Seller's **first mask** in the saga [Saga invention, D2]
**Title**: Riga-born merchant of Vilnius; Jogaila's man in the city
**Era**: Book I (1382), Part I
**Archetype**: The Honest-Seeming Broker / The Open Gate
**Pronunciation**: HA-nu-las · /ˈɣaːnʊɫɐs/ · English sound-alike "HAH-noo-lahs" *(verify)*
**Power**: Not a mortal practitioner. A **mask of a Force** (a shard of Velnias), outside the tier ladder. As Hanulas he never casts: he trades. Every coin he gives is warm; every gift carries a thread.

## The Record [Record]
*   A merchant from **Riga** settled in Vilnius, he **opened the city to Jogaila** in spring–June 1382 while Kęstutis was away campaigning against Kaributas.
*   He was rewarded by Jogaila and appears in the Vilnius embassy to Kraków in **1385** *(verify both: his reward and the embassy)*.

## The Mask [Saga invention]
*   **Hanulas is Henė.** The names echo on purpose. The merchant who opens the gate in 1382 and the jester who sits at Jogaila's table are the same hunger in two coats.
*   **What he sells**: Order and Riga **silver**, not Polish gold (review H2). The **golden chain** that kills Kęstutis is a Riga-made "gift" that passes through his hands (ch. 1, ch. 9: "Hanulas said it was worthy of your honour"). It carries the **Breath of the Void**.
*   **The feast-day iron**: The Order crossbow bolts that kill the Iron Wolves in ch. 7 arrive with his silver.
*   **The Hanseatic face**: The Gold-Glutton's envoys at Trakai (ch. 28) are his kind. Hanseatic merchants carry boxes of dead amber and never know.

## Appearance
*   A prosperous burgher in good German furs; ink-stained fingers; a merchant's ring.
*   A pleasant, forgettable face. People remember the voice, which sounds like their own.

## Personality
*   **Courteous**: He never threatens. He offers.
*   **Exact**: He keeps every bargain to the letter, and drafts every letter.
*   **Patient**: He will wait thirty years for a thread to come due.

## Fate
Once the city is Jogaila's, Henė the jester takes the foreground at court, yet the merchant goes on living his recorded life (the 1385 embassy). The Shadow-Seller can wear two coats at once. **Open question for the author:** is the historical Hanul wholly the mask, or a real man whom the shard rode for a season and then let go?
