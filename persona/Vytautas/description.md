# Vytautas (The Exile)

**True Name**: Vytautas; baptised Wigand (Tapiau, 21 Oct 1383) and Aleksandras (Kraków, 1386)
**Title**: Duke of Grodno (in 1382; also Brest and Podlasie from 1384), the Wolf's Son. Trakai becomes his only in **1392**; Grand Duke of Lithuania 1392–1430
**Age**: c. 32 in 1382 (born c. 1350)
**Archetype**: The Honourable Traitor / The Prodigal Prince
**Pronunciation**: VY-tau-tas · /ˈviːtɐʊtɐs/ · English sound-alike "VEE-tow-tahs"
**Power**: Bound, **tier 2** (officially tier 0). Bound into Birutė's *Senovės Sutartis* (ch. 3), with her as surety until 1384; can use the Five-Voice and the amber pebble and nothing else. Claimed by Perkūnas at Krėva (ch. 10): an instrument, not a caster. His power is political, not magical (`world_rules/power_systems.md` §4).
**Note on names**: "Vytis" is the coat of arms, never the man. "Vyti" is used only by Birutė and Kęstutis.

## Appearance
*   Dark hair, kept short in the warrior style.
*   Piercing grey eyes, like a winter storm.
*   Strong build, scarred hands from sword training.
*   Often wears a wolf-fur cloak over simple mail, eschewing the finery of his cousin Jogaila.

## Personality
*   **Honourable but Desperate**: Vytautas values his word, but Jogaila's betrayal forces him to break his own code to survive.
*   **Intense**: He rarely smiles. He carries the weight of his father's legacy and the safety of his people.
*   **Pragmatic Warrior**: Unlike his father, who was stubborn, Vytautas is willing to adapt (even using enemy weapons) to win, but he struggles with the moral cost.

## Motivation
*   **Revenge**: Avenging the murder of his father, Kęstutis.
*   **Restoration**: Reclaiming Trakai, his father's seat, which goes to Skirgaila in 1384.
*   **Survival**: Protecting his wife, Ona, and his remaining loyalists.

## Arc
Seven forms across Books I–IV (Son, Survivor, Tool, Traitor of Traitors, Grand Duke, Iron Wolf, Keeper of the Flame): see `books/character_arcs.md` §1.
