# The Krivis of Šventaragis (The Keeper of the Pact)

**True Name**: **To be decided by the author.** Suggested: **Sirvydas** (`world_rules/religion_and_clergy.md` §2.2) [Saga invention]
**Title**: *Krivis* (*Krivių Krivaitis*), high priest of the Šventaragis valley, Vilnius; keeper of the eternal flame and of the Silent Pact
**Era**: Book VI (the Pact, 1345); Book I (offstage in 1382; the extinguishing, Feb–Mar 1387); Book V (flashbacks)
**Age**: Old in 1382: he bound the Pact thirty-seven years earlier
**Archetype**: The Broker-Priest / The Keeper of Terrible Secrets
**Pronunciation**: (see pronunciation guide)
**Power**: **Tier 4** (Krivis: all five elements; can bind a *Senovės Sutartis*; speaks with Forces; cannot command one). He rarely casts. His power is **binding** and the oath-memory of the flame. He carries the ***krivulė***, the crooked staff that "remembers" every oath sworn while it was held.

## Record or invention
*   **[Record, debated]** Peter of Dusburg describes a Prussian high priest (*Criwe*) at Romuva; whether Lithuania had a single high priest is debated. The 16th-century chronicles make **Lizdeika** high priest under Gediminas. Długosz describes the Vilnius sacred fire, grove and snakes destroyed in 1387. Ethnography records the *krivulė* staff that summoned village assemblies.
*   **[Saga invention]** The office moved from Romuva to Vilnius when Prussia fell (1283). This Krivis witnessed and bound the Silent Pact (1345) and is its last keeper.

## Appearance [Saga invention]
*   Very old; white hair to the waist; a linen robe with a belt of woven *perkūnėliai*.
*   Leans on the *krivulė*. His eyes are smoke-reddened from sixty years at the fire.

## Personality
*   **Grey**: An honest broker who keeps a dishonest silence. He bound two princes to a Pact whose price (royal children's blood) the people were never told.
*   **Afraid**: In 1382, when Jogaila's captain came for a prophecy, he sent his youngest *vaidilutė*, Eglė, to give it instead of facing the duke.
*   **Dignified at the end**: In 1387 he does not resist.
