# Birutė: Timeline

1.  **The Vow**: Becomes a *vaidilutė* at Palanga, guarding the shore-fire; the sea speaks to her through amber.
2.  **The "Abduction" (c. 1350)**: Refuses Kęstutis by her vow, then lets herself be carried off so the vow's letter is kept.
3.  **The Matriarch**: Rules Trakai alongside Kęstutis for three decades; bears Vytautas c. 1350.
4.  **The Sutartis (Book I, ch. 3)**: Binds Vytautas, stands surety for him, extracts the flame promise.
5.  **The Widow (Aug 1382)**: Kęstutis is murdered. Jogaila's men try to drown her; the sea refuses her three times. Her eyes go sea-dark. She hides at Palanga.
6.  **The Fog (autumn 1382)**: Covers Vytautas's escape from Krėva through the pebble, at the cost of her voice.
7.  **The Amber Sacrifice (1384)**: At the full moon after the July reconciliation she pays the surety in full at Palanga and becomes the Amber Queen, leaving the amber kneeling woman.
8.  **The Narrator**: Tells the series; in Book V, the last thing she remembers before the sea takes her statue.
