# Jonas (The Pack-Elder)

**True Name**: Lost. "Jonas" is his ***kailio vardas*** (skin-name): the baptismal name of an Order sergeant he killed in 1361 during Kęstutis's escape from Marienburg, worn as a trophy. That is why a pagan wolf has a saint's name (ch. 25:7) [Saga invention]
**Title**: Kęstutis's armour-bearer; ***vilkų vyresnysis*** (pack-elder) of the **hidden nine**, the Iron Wolves of Kęstutis's guard; leader of the ch. 25 band
**Era**: Book I (ch. 2, 7, 15, 25)
**Age**: About 47 in 1382 (c. 1335–1384)
**Archetype**: The Loyal Hound / The Old Soldier
**Pronunciation**: JO-nas · /ˈjoːnɐs/ · English sound-alike "YOH-nahs"
**Power**: **Granted**, the **Wolf-Change** (*Vilkų krikštas*) from the Root-Mother via the Dykra: night-sight, scent, fast healing, the berserk "iron hour". Vilkatai are not casters and sit **outside the tier ladder** (`world_rules/iron_wolves.md` §1). Part of his soul stays wolf.

## Appearance [Saga invention]
*   Grey-bearded, broad, quiet; wolf-hide over quilted wool, no plate.
*   His **burned palm**, the pack-elder's mark, from carrying the pack's single **feast-day iron** blade, wrapped in linen.

## Personality
*   **Silent and literal**: One of "the men who don't drink Jogaila's mead" (ch. 2). He will not drink from a hand that has not given bread.
*   **Loyal to the dead**: He serves the son because he swore to the father.
*   **Lawful**: He lives by the Pack's Word, and dies by it.

## Relationships
*   **Kęstutis**: His prince and his oath. Jonas carried his armour for thirty years.
*   **Vytautas**: The son he follows though Vytautas is not a wolf ("Vilkų kunigaikštis", half in irony).
*   **Šešiapirštis**: His *Vilkų Tėvas*. **Kirmėlė**: the fellow survivor who sold the nine (Jonas does not know).

## Timeline
1.  **1361**: Kills an Order sergeant in Kęstutis's escape from Marienburg and takes his name.
2.  **Ch. 2 (1382)**: Among the hidden nine at Kęstutis's side; the silver wolf is given to Vytautas.
3.  **Ch. 7 (Aug 1382)**: Survives the slaughter at Jogaila's camp near Trakai, with Kirmėlė: his men stayed in Kęstutis's own camp, and Kirmėlė was not in the tent (D10; Book I ch. 7).
4.  **Ch. 15 (autumn 1382)**: Leaves a horse for the fleeing Vytautas at a named oak a night's walk from Krėva.
5.  **Ch. 25 (1384)**: Leads the remnant of Kęstutis's packs as Vytautas's band; dies under the oak. Vytautas does not burn him but carries him to a bog: the first time he keeps a wolf's law. The folk see the band and call the whole thing "the Iron Wolf".
