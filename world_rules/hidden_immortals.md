# The Hidden Immortals: Avatars of the Saga

In the Geležinio Vilko Saga, the gods do not just watch—they walk. Certain characters are more than they seem.

## 1. Birutė: Vessel of the Amber Queen *(revised per canon D1)*
*   **The Secret**: Birutė is born mortal, the last of the Palanga amber-priestess line. The **Amber Queen** is an *office* of the sea's grief, first held by Jūratė (cosmology §3.4), and it needs a living vessel. Birutė **becomes** the Queen over Book I.
*   **The Clue**: The Queen's voice speaks to her from amber (ch. 3). After widowhood her eyes turn sea-dark (ch. 11). The sea returns her ring three times, and in 1382 it refuses to drown her (D3).
*   **The Reveal**: Book I ch. 27 (1384). At the full moon she pays Vytautas's surety in full and dissolves into light, leaving the amber kneeling woman on Palanga shore. From then on she acts only through amber she touched, and she narrates the series (`00_pradzia.qmd`).

## 2. Jester Henne: The Shadow-Seller (Velnias)
*   **The Secret**: The jester who serves Jogaila (1382), Skirgaila (1387–97), Vytautas (from 1392) and Sigismund of Luxembourg (1429–30) is actually the **Shadow-Seller** in a manageable human form.
*   **The Clue**: He is seen in the background of every major betrayal, often juggling **three black stones** (the cousins). One drops each time a bargain is collected (D2). His first mask is **Hanulas**, the Riga merchant who opened Vilnius to Jogaila in 1382.
*   **The Reveal**: At the Congress of Lutsk (Book IV), he is the one who "steals" the crown, revealing that the gods never intended for a mortal king to rise above their influence.

## 3. Dievas Senelis: The Wandering Beggar
*   **The Secret**: A recurring old man with a wooden staff who appears to Vytis and Jogaila at their lowest moments.
*   **The Clue**: He asks for a piece of bread, a coin or a story. Those who give are not rescued but **seen**: their road becomes clear to them, even if it leads to death (Kęstutis, ch. 4). Those who refuse are left blind to their own road (cosmology §5).
*   **The Reveal**: He never reveals himself fully, but his footprints in the snow glow with a faint blue light.

## 4. Galminas: The Host of the Root-Mother
*   **The Secret**: Galminas died years ago in a Teutonic raid. The Root-Mother reanimated his body with vines and earth. He is a "Living Alkas" (Sacred Space).
*   **The Clue**: He does not eat or sleep, and his wounds bleed green sap instead of red blood.
*   **The Reveal**: During the Battle of Grunwald, he transforms into a massive, wooden-skinned beast, showing the Order the true "Wild" they tried to conquer.

## 5. Jadwiga: The Relic of the White Silence
*   **The Secret**: She is a "Saint" not by church decree, but because the **White Silence** uses her as a conduit for its cold, purifying light.
*   **The Clue**: Her touch can heal, but it also leaves a faint layer of frost.
*   **The Reveal**: At her death, her body does not decay, but turns into a statue of salt and silver, a permanent anchor for the White Silence in the Polish heartland.
