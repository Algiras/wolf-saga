# Kęstutis (The Old Boar)

**True Name**: Kęstutis
**Title**: Duke of Trakai, Defender of Samogitia; co-ruler of the Grand Duchy (1345–82)
**Age**: About 85 in 1382 (born c. 1297)
**Archetype**: The Old Guard / The Tragic Hero
**Pronunciation**: kęs-TU-tis · /kʲæːsˈtʊtʲɪs/ · English sound-alike "kess-TOO-tis"
**Power**: **Tier 0** (no gift). A sworn oath-taker of the Silent Pact (1345) and **claimed by Perkūnas** (justice) since Book VI. He gave the Beggar his coin (ch. 4) and so walks to Krėva *seeing* his road. His sword's *perkūnėlis* ward fails as the family's oaths break.
**Note on names**: calls his son "Vyti" in private; never "Kestus" or "Kęsto" in prose.

## Appearance
*   White hair and huge white beard, braided in the old style.
*   Massive frame, though stooped with age.
*   Covered in scars from a lifetime of fighting Crusaders.
*   Wears heavy bear furs and iron amulets of Perkūnas.

## Personality
*   **Stubborn**: Refuses to abandon the Old Gods. "I was born under the oak, and I will die under the oak."
*   **Blunt**: Has no patience for politics or lies.
*   **Merciful, fatally**: Loved his brother Algirdas and spared his nephew Jogaila in 1381 after reading the Dovydiškės treaty. That mercy is his tragic flaw.

## Motivation
*   **Defence**: To keep the Teutonic Knights out of Samogitia.
*   **Tradition**: To keep the sacred flames and the old faith against the encroaching Christian tide.
