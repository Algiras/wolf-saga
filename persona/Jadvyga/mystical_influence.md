# Jadvyga: Mystical Influence

**Patron Force**: The White Silence (*Baltoji Tyla*), which uses her as a conduit
**Conflict**: The Amber Queen (memories of lost love)

## The Meddling
-   **The Burning Cross**: In her prayers, Jadvyga sees a cross of fire that points north. She believes it is a holy command; the Forces know it is a beacon for destruction.
-   **The Stillness**: In diplomatic meetings a cold calm spreads from her and people find it hard to lie. It is not illusion (the White Silence grants calm and fearlessness, not false faces), but it is a terrifying political weapon.
-   **The Frost-Touch**: Her touch heals, but leaves a faint layer of frost.
-   **The Salt-Tears**: Her grief for her lost love is taken up by the Amber Queen, and amber "pearls" appear on the Baltic shore, linking the Polish queen to the pagan sea.

## Interaction
Jadvyga is a saint in the making, but she does not realise that the White Silence that guides her is as cold and ruthless as the Root-Mother she fears. Eglė warns her in a dream: "The White Silence will freeze your heart before it saves your soul."
