Source: books/1/continuity_bible.md

Compiled 2026-10-10 from a full read of books/1/prologue.qmd, 00_pradzia.qmd and 01–30 in _quarto.yml order, checked against world_rules/canon_decisions.md (D1–D9), cosmology.md, magic_rules.md, master_chronology.md, books/1/senoves_kalba_magic_system.md and books/1/story_review.md.

How to read references. 12:13 means books/1/12_gedulo_skraiste.qmd, line 13 (each paragraph is one line). P is the prologue and 00 is 00_pradzia.qmd. C/H/M codes point to items already in story_review.md. N codes are new findings from this pass, indexed in §0. Line numbers are from the 2026-10-10 text and will drift after edits.

N Where Finding Sev.
N1 17:25 Vytautas calls himself “Senojo Šerno vienintelis likęs gyvas sūnus”. Kęstutis’s sons Tautvilas (d. 1390) and Žygimantas Kęstutaitis (d. 1440, a Book V lead in master_chronology.md) were alive. High
N2 8:3, 14:17, 17:9, 17:11, 18:5 The seasons flip back and forth: “prasidėjo tikroji pilka žiema” (8:3), then “įprasta rudeninė migla” (14:17), then “gruodu sustingusios žemės” next to “šaltoje rudeninėje saulės šviesoje” (17:9/17:11), then “žiemos šviesa” (18:5). High
N3 10:7, 16:13, 17:27, 26:19 The amulet has no origin and no return. Vytautas “nelietė tėvo atšalusio kūno” (10:7), so he could not have taken it then. He gives up his “paskutinius bajoriškus daiktus” (16:13). The border guard takes it (“paėmė amuletą”, 17:27) and it is never handed back, yet in 26:19 he has kept it “per visą savo bėgimą”. High
N4 24:23 → 25:7 He runs into the forest “galutinai … vienas” (24:23), and the next morning “Su juo liko nedidelis būrys žmonių” including Jonas (25:7). The bridge sentence “Jis bėgo vienas, bet jautėsi vienišas” (25:7) is garbled. High
N5 15:27, 23:9, 23:13 Eglė’s silence breaks, and so does D5. In 15:27 she nods to “Tu esi Gintaro Šventikė” (“Mergina lėtai linktelėjo galva”), confirming the identity D5 says she must correct. In 23:9 a voice speaks to Vytautas and 23:13 says she spoke “daugiau nė vieno … žodžio”, although she is mute in ch. 15–16. High
N6 3:69 vs 28:13 Birutė gives him the pebble as “prisiminimas apie jūrą, kurios tu dar nematei”, but Ona remembers him loving her “prie Palangos jūrų”. Med
N7 28:3, 30:3 Internal chronology fails. 23:3 dates the siege to 1383 and ch. 26–28 follow straight on, yet 28:3 says “dar prieš kelerius trumpus metus” (about one year by the text’s own dates). 30:3 says only “metai” have passed since the division, but Jogaila already holds the Polish crown (≥1386) and Skirgaila is vicegerent (1387). Med
N8 30:11 vs ch. 28 Henė tells Skirgaila that Vytautas has “viltį visiškai atstatyti Trakus”, although ch. 28 shows Vytautas already rebuilding Trakai. On the record, Trakai went to Skirgaila (H8), so the taunt is aimed at the wrong man. Med
N9 7:11, 17:33, 18:11 Cosmology §2.2 is broken (“the saga never passes judgement on the Christian God”): “tam tamsiam, negailestingam pavidalui, kurį kantriai maitino Dievas” (7:11). “krikščioniškojo Geležinio Šventojo kvėpavimas” (17:33). Knights “aklai meldėsi” to the Iron Saint “Marienburgo katedroje” (18:11), and Marienburg had no cathedral. Med
N10 21:23, 13:7, 13:35 Slips that invert the meaning: “netikėtai pamatė neapykantą, o gailestį” (21:23; it needs ne). “Jos rankos nė akimirkos netvirtėjo” (13:7) says her hands shook, the opposite of the intent. “Laukiau tavęs prie Jūros” (13:35) is past tense and should be future (Lauksiu). Med
N11 9:19–21 Kęstutis crushes one killer’s skull (“kaukolės sutriuškinimo garsas”), yet “jų buvo trys” and all three still pin him down. Med
N12 9:27 = 9:39 Duplicated sentence: “Pasaulis pradėjo blėsti, virsti į begalinį pilką rūką.” Low
N13 29:5 vs 29:17 He stands “iki pat kulniukų” (ankle-deep), then “išniro iš vandens”. 29:5 treats this as his second baptism, but by 1387 he had been baptised Catholic in 1383, Orthodox (Jurgis) in 1384 (verify) and Catholic (Aleksandras) in 1386. Med
N14 22:3 Vilnius is called Vytautas’s “gimtąjį miestą”. He was born at (Senieji) Trakai. Low
N15 6:9 “Net paukščiai virš katedros nutilo”. There was no cathedral in pagan Vilnius in 1382 (it was founded in 1387). Low
N16 19:27 “pirmą kartą per visus šiuos metus”: only months have passed since ch. 3. “savo karius, dabar vilkinčius baltus apsiaustus”: he arrived alone, and only professed knight-brothers wore white. Med
N17 13:11 vs 13:17; 13:25 The maid’s dress is “pilką” and then “ruda lininę” eleven sentences later. The maid lies “nusukusi veidą į sieną”, yet Vytautas looks “į jas” (her eyes). Low
N18 12:13 The sleeping draught is called “mirtina dovana”. A lethal sleeping draught is a contradiction, and it is never used (C5). Low
N19 ch. 2, 6, 9 Kęstutis’s rusted sword, the big plant of ch. 2, disappears: it is never mentioned at the gate, at the arrest or at Krėva. Its metal also changes: “Žalvaris jaučia pavojų” (2:51), “ryjant sidabrinį blizgesį” (2:75), against iron rust. Med
N20 3 → 11 Birutė is at Trakai in ch. 3 and on the Palanga shore (about 300 km) in ch. 11, days later, while Jogaila holds her husband. The journey is never accounted for. Med
N21 9:29 Birutė appears to the dying Kęstutis. He carries no amber anchor, so under magic_rules.md §3.4 her range is line of sight. Attribute it to the Amber Queen (a Force) or to Giltinė, not to Kalba. Med
N22 8:5 vs 14:17 The White Silence sits over Krėva (8:5). Under magic_rules.md §9 it smothers Wind, so Birutė’s Vėjopatis fog should fail or cost double. The text does not address this. Med
N23 3:61–63 A “tau” working means the caster pays (magic_rules.md §3.1), but the only cost shown (a burn scar) lands on Vytautas, the recipient. Med
N24 15:21, 15:33, 15:39 The swamp invocation is called “Senovės Sutartis” three times. Under magic_rules.md it is the Five-Voice invocation, and the Sutartis is the ch. 3 binding. Low
N25 27:15 “jos Gyvybės Versmės palaiminimas”: Life Spring is the Water healing boon, not a self-sacrifice. Low
N26 27:13 The pebble is missing at the moment of transformation. cosmology.md §3.4 says the Queen now acts only through amber Birutė touched, so the touch Vytautas feels in Vilnius should come through the pebble. Med
N27 23:25 vs 22:9 “„Geležis dega,“ — prisiminė jis inžinieriaus žodžius”: the engineer never said it (22:9 says “Geležis ir siera … nugali akmenį ir medį”). 24:15 then spells the arrow message “Geležis degia”. Low
N28 20:19 The copy-paste breaks the logic: “bėgančią su tėvu Kęstučiu” and then “Kęstutis stovėjo šalia … jūs abu esate man sūnūs!” Kęstutis is now both a runner and the onlooker. Med
N29 4:55 vs 6:3/6:5; 5:47 vs 6:25 They arrive “Vėlai vakare” (4:55), but enter the gate “rudens pavakarę” in “popietės oras” (6:3/6:5). Jogaila walks to the “derybų stalo” (5:47), then watches from the top of the Gediminas tower (6:25). Low
N30 23:15 vs 10:5 “su kuriais Vytis jį matė paskutinį kartą prie Vilniaus vartų”: he last saw his father as a corpse at Krėva (10:5). Resolved: the arrest is at the camp near Trakai, so the gate is the camp gate, and the line no longer appears in ch. 23. Low
N31 21:25 “Tavo tėvas apsiverstų kape” depends on the ch. 10 burial. If the H4 cremation fix goes in, this line must change with it. Low
N32 6:15, 12:19, 1:25 Kinship errors beyond C12: “savo dėdėms” (plural; Skirgaila has one uncle present, 6:15). Ona says “tėvo Kęstučio” (D9: uošvio, 12:19). The envoys bring “broliškus sveikinimus” from a nephew to an uncle (1:25). Low
N33 4:51, 21:25; 14:19, 28:13 D4: “Vyti” is used by the beggar and by Gediminas, not only Birutė and Kęstutis. “Vyties” is a non-existent genitive (Vyčio). Low
N34 P:17, P:23 From “Romuva” Eglė looks “tiesiai link sostinės Vilniaus akmeninių sienų”, which supports D5’s move to Šventaragis. Jogaila’s captain wears “Gediminaičių stulpais”, the emblem later tied to the Kęstutaičiai (Vytautas, Žygimantas) (verify), so they belong to the wrong house. Low
N35 P:19 Plant: “po tūkstančio naktų” from August 1382 lands in spring to summer 1385, which is the Krėva Union (14 Aug 1385). It pays off H10 if that scene is added. Note
N36 ch. 15–17; ch. 19–21 History: the escape route through Masovia (his sister Danutė, Duke Janusz I) is missing. The joint campaign in ch. 20–21 is set in spring, but on the record it was autumn 1383, and the baptism (21 Oct 1383) came after it. The 1383 Order Marshal was Kuno von Hattenstein (verify). Med
N37 1:5, 1:11 master_chronology.md places the Order and Jogaila besieging Trakai in Jul–Aug 1382. Ch. 1 shows a peaceful harvest feast inside Trakai (this extends H3). Med
N38 25:19 “atsisېdo” contains an Arabic-script letter (U+06D0). It will break the LuaLaTeX/DejaVu PDF build or render as a box. Med (build)
N39 24:17 “paties Birutės balsas” is a gender error (pačios). Low
N40 17:11 vs 17:35/17:39 “masyvi medinė tvirtovė”, then “juodus kryžius ant mūrų sienų” and “sušalusių vokiškų akmenų”. Low
N41 30:19 The Vorskla prophecy has the cousins “vėl klastos vienas kitą”. Vorskla (1399) was a defeat by Edigu, not kin betrayal, and Skirgaila died in 1397. Low
N42 12:5; 15:27 Back-to-back repeats: two veil sentences (12:5) and two “ištiesė savo ranką” sentences (15:27). Low
N43 00 00_pradzia ends “pirmiausia … apie tą, kas nutiko Trakuose”, so in the build it must sit after the prologue (Šventaragis) and immediately before ch. 1. Note
N44 13:5, 15:3, 28:11; 2:75, 26:27, 26:31 “vito” is not a word (virto). “klausėsi” is misused for “aidėjo / skambės” (“žingsnių, kurie klausėsi koridoriuje”, “tavo vardas klausėsi Europos žemėse”, “baimė klausėsi”). Low
N45 17:15 “Trakų ir visos Lietuvos kunigaikštį”: Vytautas held no grand-ducal title in 1382. Low
N46 18:21 His answer to the terms, “Priešingai nei jūs norėtumėte, magistre”, never says yes, yet ch. 19 assumes acceptance. Low
N47 25:7 Jonas, “senasis Kęstučio ginklanešys”: a Christian name for a pagan duke’s squire in 1383, and his presence in the Order camp is never explained. Low
N48 15:5 The horse that appears from nowhere (C4) is mourned as his “ištikimu palydovu”, which implies an old horse of his own that a prisoner could not have. Low
N49 29:25 His double accuses him of acting “kad tavo siela liktų amžinai švaria”: the accusation contradicts itself. Low
N50 27:5 vs 27:13 Birutė knows the union is “ką tik formaliai pasirašyta”, while in 27:13 Vytautas is still about to sign it. Low
N51 14:17, 24:17, 25:15 Three “last gifts”: “paskutinė Birutės dovana” (14:17), the wind (24:17), and “galutinė jo motinos dovana” (25:15). Low
N52 ch. 3 → 29 The scar from ch. 3 appears only once more (15:27). It is absent when he undresses (13:17), is bathed (18:5) and at both baptisms (19, 29), where it should react. Opp.
N53 3:11, 3:33, 3:81 There are two ambers in ch. 3: Birutė’s large bee-amber (left glowing blue on the floor) and the thumb-sized pebble. The bee-amber is never seen again, and readers may conflate the two. Low
N54 27:13 “skubotai kylančių naujų bažnyčių pamatų” in Vilnius in 1383–84, before the 1387 baptism. Low
N55 9:19 Kęstutis was about 85 by tradition (b. c. 1297) and wrestles three young men. Fine for legend, but one line acknowledging his age would help. Note
