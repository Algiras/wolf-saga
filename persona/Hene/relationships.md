# Henė: Relationships (Masters and Marks)

*He serves the deal, never the man (D2). "Master" means the court he sits in.*

## Masters
| Years | Master | Mask | The deal |
|---|---|---|---|
| before 1377 | Algirdas's court (haunting, Book VI) | — | Watches the Pact's oath-takers |
| 1382 | **Jogaila** | Hanulas, then Henė the jester | The "easy way" of the trap. Price: his line inherits the crown and loses the land |
| 1387–97 | **Skirgaila** | Henė | Bought at Krėva. Price: to be used and discarded. **Collected 1397** (the first stone drops) |
| from 1392 | **Vytautas** | Henė | Joins him after Ostrów. Half-bought at the **Vorskla** (the vision of empire); **refused** at Lutsk |
| 1399–1411 | both cousins | Henė | Advises both sides in Book III |
| 1429–30 | **Sigismund of Luxembourg** | the jester at his court | The crown for the flame; the Lutsk crown turned to **leaves** |
| 1430s | **Žygimantas Kęstutaitis** | a whisper | Feeds his paranoia (the 1432 coup) |

## Others
*   **Jogaila's court lot-caster**: A good *reader* who has never cast an unfavourable lot. Henė likes him.
*   **The Beggar (Dievas Senelis)**: The other old power at the crossroads. Henė's stones are black; the Beggar's are grey.
*   **Perkūnas**: Lightning eventually comes looking wherever Henė sits at a duke's fire.
*   **The Flame-Keeper**: The last person Henė speaks to before he unmasks for good (Book V) *(suggestion; not yet canon)*.
