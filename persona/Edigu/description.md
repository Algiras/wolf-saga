# Edigu (The Steppe Butcher)

**Title**: Emir (*beglerbeg*) of the Golden Horde, of the Manghit clan; kingmaker of khans; regarded as founder of the Nogai Horde
**Era**: Book II (*Auksinis Šešėlis*, The Golden Shadow): the Vorskla, 12 Aug 1399
**Archetype**: The Tactical Genius / The Chthonic Threat
**Pronunciation**: e-di-GĖ-jus · /ɛdʲɪˈɡʲeːjʊs/ · English sound-alike "eh-dih-GAY-yoos" *(verify)*
**Power**: **Tier 0**. No craft. The **Steppe-Hunger** (*Stepių Alkis*) feeds through his victory at the Vorskla. Whether he serves it knowingly is open (author's choice); the canon only fixes that the Force feeds there (`world_rules/master_chronology.md`).

## Appearance
*   Compact, powerful build. He is a man who "lives on a horse."
*   Sharp, hawk-like features and a thin moustache.
*   Minimal armour of hardened leather and iron rings, built for speed.

## Personality
*   **Brilliant Strategist**: The man who defeats Vytautas at the Vorskla, with Temür Qutlugh. He understands the terrain and the psychological weaknesses of Western knights.
*   **Ruthless**: He seeks results, not glory. He views the Lithuanian expansion as a nuisance to be swatted.
*   **Loyal (to the Steppe)**: He cares for the strength of the Horde, and uses any khan as a puppet.

## Motivation
*   **Steppe Mastery**: To keep any Western power (Lithuania or Moscow) off the Horde's lands.
*   **Destruction of Rivals**: Hunting down Tokhtamysh and his line.
