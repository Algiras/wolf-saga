# Skirgaila: Timeline

1.  **The Instigator**: Fans the flames of conflict between Jogaila and Kęstutis.
2.  **The Envoy (1379–80)**: Negotiates with the Teutonic Order, which leads to the secret Treaty of Dovydiškės *(verify his exact role)*.
3.  **The Captor (early Aug 1382)**: Gives his safe-conduct to Kęstutis and Vytautas, then seizes them at Jogaila's camp near Trakai and sends them to Krėva.
4.  **The Hunter (autumn 1382)**: His trackers chase the escaped Vytautas into the Dykra and are drowned there.
5.  **Lord of Trakai (1384)**: Receives Trakai in the division, over Vytautas's head.
6.  **The Regent (1387)**: Appointed Jogaila's regent in Lithuania; Henė takes him as his new master.
7.  **The Defender (1390)**: Holds Vilnius against Vytautas and the Order.
8.  **The Defeated (1392)**: Loses Trakai and the regency to Vytautas after the Ostrów Agreement.
9.  **The Last Post (1395–97)**: Rules Kyiv, where he dies on 11 Jan 1397, poison rumoured. Henė drops the first stone.
