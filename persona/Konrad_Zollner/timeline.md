# Konrad Zöllner von Rotenstein: Timeline

1.  **The Election (1382)** [Record]: Chosen Grand Master after Winrich von Kniprode's death (24 Jun 1382). Kniprode is dead before Kęstutis is murdered.
2.  **Dubysa (31 Oct 1382)** [Record]: The Order's treaty with Jogaila (baptism in four years, Samogitia to the Dubysa); never ratified.
3.  **The Host (late 1382–83)** [Record]: Receives the fugitive Vytautas in Prussia (ch. 18–19).
4.  **The War (summer–autumn 1383)** [Record]: Declares war on Jogaila; campaigns with Vytautas.
5.  **The Font (21 Oct 1383)** [Record]: Vytautas is baptised "Wigand" at Tapiau. Zöllner believes it.
6.  **Königsberg (30 Jan 1384)** [Record]: Vytautas cedes Samogitia and becomes the Order's vassal; the Order builds Marienwerder for him.
7.  **The Betrayal (Jul 1384)** [Record]: Vytautas burns Marienwerder and Georgenburg. Zöllner's chess game is lost [Saga: his one moment of rage].
8.  **The Second Guest (1389–90)** [Record]: Receives Vytautas again after his second defection; the Treaty of Lyck *(verify)*.
9.  **Death (20 Aug 1390)** [Record]: Dies as the great 1390 campaign is being readied. The siege of Vilnius (Sep–Oct 1390) follows under the Grand Commander *(verify: `houses_and_lineages.md` says the siege "began under him")*.
