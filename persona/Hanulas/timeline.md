# Hanulas: Timeline

1.  **The Burgher (before 1382)** [Record]: A Riga merchant trading in Vilnius.
2.  **The Gift (1381–82)** [Saga invention]: The Riga-made golden chain changes hands through him; it will be the murder weapon at Krėva.
3.  **The Open Gate (spring–Jun 1382)** [Record]: Admits Jogaila's men to Vilnius while Kęstutis is on campaign.
4.  **The Silver (summer 1382)** [Saga invention]: Order silver and feast-day iron reach Skirgaila's men through him (ch. 5–7).
5.  **The Reward (1382–85)** [Record, verify]: Honoured by Jogaila; named in the Lithuanian embassy to Kraków (1385).
6.  **The Second Coat** [Saga invention]: From 1382 Henė the jester stands at Jogaila's side (ch. 5) while the merchant fades into the background.
