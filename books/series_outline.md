# Master Series Outline: Geležinio Vilko Saga (The Iron Wolf Saga)

## Theme: The Last Pagan Kingdom

The series explores the collision of two worlds: the ancient, mystical paganism of the Baltic forests and the cold, armored expansion of Christian Europe. It is a story of three cousins (Vytautas, Jogaila, and Skirgaila) whose love and hate for each other shape the map of the continent.

**The spine:** a pagan realm survives a century of crusade by a pact with its gods; one family's kin-murder breaks it; the murdered man's son spends fifty years building a Christian empire with his left hand while secretly keeping the old fire alive with his right, and the moment he dies the fire goes out and the gods become folklore.

## Book I: Vilko Tremtis (The Wolf's Exile) - [DRAFTED; under revision]

*   **Timeline**: 1377–1387 (the action runs Aug 1382 – spring 1387)
*   **Focus**: The fall of the Old Giants and the First Great Betrayal.
*   **Key Events**: Murder of Kęstutis at Krėva (15 Aug 1382), Vytautas's escape from Krėva in the maid's clothes, the deal with the Teutonic Order and the false baptism at Tapiau (1383), the July 1384 burning of the Order's Neman castles (the revision replaces the old "Siege of Vilnius", review H7), Trakai given to Skirgaila, the Union of Krėva (1385), and the Baptism of Lithuania (1387), when the Šventaragis flame is put out and Vytautas secretly lights the Last Flame.
*   **The Myth**: **The Shadow-Seller**'s manipulation and **The Amber Queen**'s guidance; Birutė's sacrifice at Palanga (1384).
*   **Protagonist**: Vytautas, Forms 1–4 (the Son, the Survivor, the Tool, the Traitor of Traitors).

## Book II: Auksinis Šešėlis (The Golden Shadow) — working title, see the note above

*   **Timeline**: 1387–1399
*   **Focus**: Ambition. From Skirgaila's Trakai, through the second defection and Ostrów, to empire and the Vorskla. The book the protagonist loses.
*   **Key Events**: Skirgaila's regency at Trakai; the flame hidden in the flooded foundations of the island castle; the second civil war (1389–92): flight to the Order, the Treaty of Lyck (1390), the siege of Vilnius and the burning of the Crooked Castle (1390, with Henry Bolingbroke); Sofija's marriage to Vasily I of Moscow (1391); the poisoning of Vytautas's hostage sons (1392); Ostrów (4–5 Aug 1392), where Vytautas becomes Grand Duke; Kyiv, Smolensk and the refuge of Tokhtamysh; Skirgaila's death in Kyiv (Jan 1397); the Black Sea raids and the Tatars and Karaims settled at Trakai; Salynas (12 Oct 1398); Jadwiga's death (17 Jul 1399); the catastrophic Battle of the Vorskla (12 Aug 1399).
*   **The Myth**: **Velnias**'s false vision of a throne on the steppe; the **Steppe-Hunger** (*Erdene-Chuluu*) feeding on the hubris of horizon-kings; the **Unbinding** reaching the countryside and the first wolf hunts of the unburned dead.
*   **The Atrocity**: The slaughter on the Vorskla River, where perhaps twenty princes (verify) and the coalition army were crushed by Edigu.
*   **Antagonists**: Skirgaila (defeated; dies 1397) → Edigu (wins).

## Book III: Žaliasis Miškas (The Green Wood)

*   **Timeline**: 1399–1411
*   **Focus**: Atonement through alliance. The Final Stand against the Iron Order.
*   **Key Events**: Kęstutis's sword recovered; the Union of Vilnius and Radom (1401); the Samogitian uprisings of 1401 and 1409 under Galminas; Raciąż (1404); the wars with Vasily I and the Ugra stand-off (1408); Ulrich von Jungingen elected (1407); the reconciliation between Jogaila and Vytautas; the pontoon bridge over the Vistula; the Battle of Grunwald (15 Jul 1410), with its disputed feigned retreat and Jungingen's death; the failed siege of Marienburg; the First Peace of Thorn (1 Feb 1411), which returns Samogitia only for the two cousins' lifetimes.
*   **The Myth**: **Eglė** and the Serpent-Kin guiding the scouts; the Last Flame carried to the field in a lantern and seen by a single žynys; Vytautas's thunder-oath binding **Perkūnas**; **Galminas** transforming and becoming a grove; the **Iron Saint**'s final screams.
*   **The Atrocity**: The carnage of Grunwald, turning the battlefield into a literal graveyard for an entire class of chivalry.
*   **Antagonist**: Ulrich von Jungingen (killed). Sigismund of Luxembourg, King of Hungary since 1387, is the Order's ally.

## Book IV: Nematoma Karūna (The Invisible Crown)

*   **Timeline**: 1411–1430
*   **Focus**: The Tragedy of Ambition and the vanishing dream of Kingship: a crown or the flame, never both.
*   **Key Events**: The Union of Horodło (1413); the Samogitian baptism (1413–17) as the Hidden Groves fall; the Council of Constance (1414–18); Ona's death and Vytautas's marriage to Julijona (1418); the Treaty of Melno (1422), which makes Samogitia Lithuanian for good; the Black Sea outposts (early 15th century, verify); the Congress of Lutsk (1429); the coronation crown intercepted by the Polish lords led by Oleśnicki (1430); Vytautas's death at Trakai (27 Oct 1430).
*   **The Myth**: **Jester Henne**'s last bargain, *the crown for the flame*, REFUSED; the crown turned to leaves on the roadside; the **Gold-Glutton**'s ledgers; the Last Flame goes out the moment Vytautas dies.
*   **The Atrocity**: The political strangulation of a nation's sovereignty through ink and seal.
*   **Antagonist**: Zbigniew Oleśnicki (wins the crown; loses nothing he believes in).

## Book V: Vilko Palikimas (The Wolf's Legacy)

*   **Timeline**: 1430–1450 (with the 1345 flashback; epilogue c. 1550s)
*   **Focus**: The ghosts. The aftermath and the haunting of history, seen through a new protagonist: **the Flame-Keeper**, a young Iron Wolf who guarded the vault, with **Birutė, the Amber Queen**, as frame narrator.
*   **Key Events**: The flame out and the thunderstorm at Vytautas's funeral; Švitrigaila Grand Duke (1430); Žygimantas Kęstutaitis's coup at Ašmena (1432); Jogaila's death (1 Jun 1434); the Battle of Pabaiskas (1 Sep 1435) and the winter of burning the unburned dead; Žygimantas's assassination at Trakai (20 Mar 1440); Casimir elected Grand Duke (1440) and crowned King of Poland (1447), with his privilege of 1447; the amber statue dimming (1445) and taken by the sea (1450). The Order's vassalage (1466) and the First Lithuanian Statute (1529) appear only in the epilogue.
*   **The Myth**: The **Root-Mother** reclaiming the forgotten groves; Henė unmasking with no stones left; the last howl of the **Iron Wolf** over Trakai; the fading of the mystical world into mere "folklore".
*   **Prequel Elements**: The Silent Pact oath (1345, at **Šventaragis**) shown in flashback through the cracked amber pebble, correcting the song the last žynys sings wrong.

## Book VI: Šventasis Miškas (The Holy Forest) - Optional Prequel

*   **Timeline**: 1345–1377 (opening in 1341)
*   **Focus**: The Era of Algirdas and Kęstutis, with **Kęstutis** as protagonist.
*   **Key Events**: The deposition of Jaunutis and the **Silent Pact** oath at Šventaragis (1345); the Battle of Strėva (1348), the first Amber Debt; Kęstutis and Birutė's meeting at Palanga (c. 1350) and Vytautas's birth; Kęstutis's ox-oath to Louis of Hungary (1351); his capture and escape from Marienburg (1361), the template for his son's; the fall of Kaunas (1362); Blue Waters (1362); Algirdas's Moscow campaigns (1368, 1370, 1372); Rudau (1370); Algirdas's death and cremation (1377).
*   **The Myth**: The Pact's three clauses sworn and the third spoken aloud; Perkūnas marking Kęstutis for justice; the Wildnis becoming the Root-Mother's Dykra; the Iron Wolves as Kęstutis's hidden guard; the **Iron Saint** growing at Strėva and Rudau.
*   **Antagonist**: Winrich von Kniprode (survives).
