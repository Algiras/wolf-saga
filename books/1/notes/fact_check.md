# Book I fact-check (Vilko Tremtis, c. 1377-1387)

Pass date: 2026-10-12. Rule: D3, history is the fixed spine; where historians disagree the saga picks a real side and says so on `books/sources.qmd` (Book I has no afterword; `sources.qmd` is the public statement); invention only fills gaps. Format: claim -> record (source) -> action. Binding: Jogaila b. c. 1362 (Tegowski 1999; VLE still says c. 1351(?)), Vytautas b. c. 1350 (VLE, lt/en wiki; pl wiki/Nikodem 1354-55). Book I states two ages only (Jogaila 4 / Vytautas 16 in ch5 = 1366; Marienburg "21 years" in ch8 and "23 years" in ch25), both consistent.

## Corrected

1. **Who warned Kestutis of Dovydiskes (ch1)** -> en.wikipedia (Civil War 1381-84, after Ivinskis 1978): **Kuno von Liebenstein**, komtur of Osterode and godfather of Danute, in Aug 1381; VLE gives no informant, Wigand's text was not accessible to me. The real Gunther von Hohenstein (c. 1310-1380, komtur of Osterode from 1349, later Brandenburg) is attested only by a Russian archaeology press release (weak) but would in any case have died before 1381. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_Civil_War_(1381%E2%80%931384) ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Dovydi%C5%A1k%C4%97s -> **corrected in ch1** ("Hohensteinas" -> "Kuno fon Liebensteinas").
2. **Kestutis takes Vilnius, 1381 (ch1)** -> autumn 1381 (VLE: Nov 1381; en.wikipedia: Jogaila away suppressing the Polotsk revolt, captured on his way back, freed with Krewa and Vitebsk) https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/kestutis/ -> **corrected in ch1**: "gates left unbarred, Jogaila found in the Lower Castle" -> the city opened without a fight, Jogaila was caught coming back.
3. **Hanul, 12 June 1382 (ch1, ch2)** -> Vilnius castle and city were taken by the townspeople led by the Riga merchant Hanul and handed to Jogaila on **12 June 1382**, while Kestutis was in the Severian land against Kaributas/Dmitry Korybut; Hanul became first elder of Vilnius 1382-87 https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/hanulas/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanul_of_Riga -> **corrected in ch1 and ch2**: "spring" -> June; "opened the gates for a bag of silver" -> took the castle and handed over castle and city (silver kept only as rumour).
4. **Date of arrest and Zolines (ch1)** -> armies met near Trakai **3 Aug 1382**, Kestutis and Vytautas lured to Jogaila's camp and arrested (VLE Kestutis/Skirgaila; en wiki); Kestutis found dead at Krewa on 15 Aug (Zoline) per en wiki/Britannica; sources also say "a week later" or first half of Aug -> **corrected in ch1**: "iki Zolines liko savaite su trupuciu" -> "dvi savaites" (later superseded by item 17: ch1 is now c. 18 Jul, "beveik menuo", before the fall of Trakai on 20 Jul; the arrest on c. 3 Aug is in ch4-6). Ch4-6 were checked for the autumn cues the brief mentions: **none found**, they are already summer (nupjautos rugienos, vidurvasaris, velyvos vasaros pavakare); ch8's "nors buvo dar rugpjutis, jau dvelke ... ankstyvas ruduo" and ch10's "saltas kaip ankstyva rudeni" are explicit similes about the cold castle and the date Zoline/mid-August is stated, so left.
5. **Kestutis's death (ch9-10)** -> found dead at Krewa; Jogaila said he hanged himself, "few believed him"; VLE: killed (strangled); lt wiki: strangled on the orders of Julijona or Skirgaila, body burned in Vilnius https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/kestutis/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C4%99stutis -> **side picked, left**: murder by strangulation with the Riga chain, the warden's "he hanged himself" as the official version (ch9, ch10), the pyre in Vilnius (ch10). Named in `sources.qmd`.
6. **Marienburg escape, told by Kestutis in Krewa (ch8)** -> 1361, about half a year; Alf (Adolfas) removed bricks and carried out rubble, Kestutis broke through the wall, crossed the empty terrace and dropped into the dry moat, where Alf waited with horses and a Teutonic knight's cloak; he rode out as a knight (http://www.veidas.lt/kestucio-nelaisve-ir-pabegimas-is-marienburgo ; https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/kestutis/) -> **corrected in ch8** (kitchen servant's dress and bucket -> wall dug out, white knight's mantle, horses; "kelis menesius" -> "beveik puse metu"; November). Matches ch25 (the sergeant Jonas at the last gate) and Book VI ch18.
7. **Birutė's fate (ch11, sources.qmd)** -> Wigand (c. 1394): Jogaila had her drowned (en wiki: at Brest, "likely in response to Vytautas's escape", with relatives Vidimantas and Butrimas killed); VLE: Kestutis had sent her to Brest in 1382, drowning "more likely"; Vytautas's 1390 complaint to the Order accuses Jogaila of killing both parents; at Constance (1416) Polish-Lithuanian delegates said she lived long and died naturally; Palanga burial only in Stryjkowski (1582) https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/birute/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_Civil_War_(1381%E2%80%931384) -> **corrected in ch11**: the drowning attempt is now at Brest in the Bug (Wigand), she survives (Constance), then walks to Palanga (Stryjkowski's tradition); one added paragraph names Wigand and the old-age report. Her end in Palanga in July 1384 (ch27) is invention (record: 1382 or later, unknown). Stated accurately on `sources.qmd` (old line "pasitraukimas i Palanga pagal kai kuriuos saltinius" replaced).
8. **Vytautas's escape disguised as a woman (ch12-14)** -> autumn 1382 with the help of wife Anna/Ona; Lithuanian Chronicle: she brought two maids and one swapped clothes with him; Wigand: he put on Anna's own clothes; he went to Janusz of Mazovia (husband of his sister Danute) and then to the Order https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_Civil_War_(1381%E2%80%931384) ; https://lt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vytautas_Didysis . The maid's death ("Elena"/"Alena") is a 19th-c. addition (Narbutt) -> **confirmed** (ch12-17: maid version, Vėlinės = autumn; Mazovia, Danute, Janusz in ch17 match). `sources.qmd` now says so.
9. **Vytautas's baptism as Wigand (ch19)** -> 21 Oct 1383 at Tapiau (VLE, lt wiki, en wiki) (pl wiki: 12 Oct, godfather the komtur of Ragnit Wigand von Beldersheim) -> **confirmed** the date; **added** the godfather in ch19 (pl.wikipedia). The "vasalo priesaika" at Tapiau was moved to the real act: the vassalage treaty is Konigsberg, 30 Jan 1384 (ch22 already says so) -> ch19: oath made an alliance oath, "vasalas" -> "netrukus vasalas".
10. **Konigsberg 30 Jan 1384 (ch22)** -> Vytautas ceded Samogitia to the Nevezis incl. Kaunas; he reconfirmed in the new Marienwerder fortress at Kaunas in Jun/Jul 1384 https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/vytautas-didysis/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_K%C3%B6nigsberg_(1384) -> **confirmed** (ch22 "sausio saltyje"; Kaunas fort; 1362 destruction of the old Kaunas castle).
11. **Georgenburg, July 1384 (ch24)** -> Vytautas took Georgenburg (Jurbarkas) by deception and burned it on **9 July 1384** (captured the vice-komtur Dietrich von Gruste and ten knights); New Marienburg also burned https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/jurgenburg/ -> **confirmed** (ch24 "devinta liepos diena"). The burning of the Kaunas depot and the sword in the fire are invention.
12. **Skirgaila gets Trakai (ch28, ch28b, ch30)** -> **1382** ("Skirgaila paskirtas Trakų kunigaikščiu (1382-92)", VLE); Jogaila promised Trakai to Vytautas in spring 1384 once Skirgaila was settled in Polotsk (taken Apr 1387); Skirgaila governor of Lithuania from 13 Mar 1386 https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/skirgaila/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skirgaila -> **corrected in ch28**: Jogaila no longer "gives" Trakai at the 1384 settlement; he says Skirgaila has held it since last summer and Trakai will be discussed again; Skirgaila "jau buvo gaves" it. ch28b ("zmogus, gaves Trakus") and ch30 ("Trakai atiteko tau") were already right.
13. **Krewa Union 14 Aug 1385 (ch28b)** -> 14 Aug 1385 https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/jogaila/ -> **confirmed** (the "three years since the death", Zoline, the 200,000 florins for William of Habsburg, Jadvyga not present).
14. **Vytautas's re-baptism as Aleksandras (ch29)** -> VLE: baptized in Krakow as Aleksandras in 1386; lt wiki: 15 Feb 1386; pl wiki: an Orthodox rebaptism after Lutsk with no date or place; Wasilewski ties the name Aleksandras to Orthodox naming -> **left (VLE side), disclosed** on `sources.qmd`.
15. **Jogaila's baptism, coronation, Lithuania's baptism (ch28-29)** -> baptized as Wladyslaw in Krakow 15 Feb 1386, married Jadwiga 18 Feb, crowned 4 Mar 1386; the baptism of the Lithuanians began early 1387 in Vilnius (VLE; the Vilnius bishopric charter is 17 Feb 1387) -> **confirmed** (ch29 "1387-uju vasario", Jogaila "jau karalius"; no scene of the 1386 events). Listed on `sources.qmd`.
16. **Liudvikas of Hungary d. Sep 1382; Kniprode d. Jun 1382; Zollner elected 1382 (ch17-18)** -> Louis d. 10 Sep 1382; Kniprode d. 24 Jun 1382; Conrad Zollner von Rotenstein Grand Master from 1382 -> **confirmed** (not re-searched, standard; flagged).
17. **Venue of the arrest (ch1-7)** -> Trakai (town/castle) surrendered to Jogaila and the Order on **20 Jul 1382** (VLE Kestutis: "07 20 ... uzemė Trakus"; en wiki: Vytautas retreated, the city surrendered 20 Jul); the armies met near Trakai on **3 Aug**, Kestutis and Vytautas were invited "derybu pretekstu" to **Jogaila's camp** and seized there (VLE Kestutis, VLE Skirgaila; en wiki), then taken to **Krewa**; Kestutis found dead there 15 Aug, Vytautas held at Krewa until autumn (VLE Vytautas, en wiki; no source puts the prisoners in Vilnius first; lt wiki says only "Krewa (or the Kreivoji castle in Vilnius)") https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/kestutis/ ; https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/skirgaila/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_Civil_War_(1381%E2%80%931384) -> **corrected in ch1-7, ch9, ch11, ch12, ch21, prologue** with the smallest edits: ch1-3 stay in Trakai castle but are dated *before* the fall (Zoline "a month away", c. 18 Jul; the envoys invite Kestutis to Jogaila's camp, Skirgaila's oath is for "Jogaila's camp"; ch2 "tomorrow we ride to Vilnius" -> "when Jogaila calls we ride"; ch3 "Birute ... tomorrow" -> "soon"); ch4 (retitled "Kelione i stovykla", file name unchanged) opens with one new paragraph: Trakai falls on 20 Jul, Kestutis withdraws to a pine ridge across the lake, two weeks of standoff, on 3 Aug the envoys return and the ride (c. 20 versts around the lakes, same beats: the Beggar, the stone) ends at the camp; ch5 Jogaila's hall = a Trakai manor requisitioned for his staff (French glass brought by wagon from Vilnius), "Vilnius belongs to us" -> "Lithuania"; ch6 "Geleziniai Vartai" = the iron-bound gate of the camp's palisade (Teutonic carpenters), archers on palisade and wagon-wall, thunder "beyond the lakes", Skirgaila's oath "two weeks ago in Trakai"; ch7 "Tylos Puota" = the same night in the manor and the tents behind its fence, Jonas's wolves left in Kestutis's own camp, "Vilnius is clean" -> "the camp is clean"; ch8-9 gate/Feast references now point to the camp; ch11 Birute leaves Trakai "soon after the men decide to ride to Jogaila, before his regiments arrive" (Jogaila's men then find the empty tower - matches the fall of Trakai); ch12 "the Vilnius night" -> "that night in the camp"; ch21 Daumantas "the day at the gate of Jogaila's camp"; prologue "negotiations in Vilnius" -> "negotiations with the uncle". The Vilnius pyre (ch10, ch12) stays: lt wiki and VLE say the body was burned in Vilnius. Not reconciled: the exact place of Kestutis's army between 20 Jul and 3 Aug is unrecorded (the saga puts it on a ridge across the lake from Trakai, invention); whether Kestutis was in Trakai castle before 20 Jul is not recorded (VLE says only that he rallied support; en wiki that he was in Samogitia after June); the route Trakai - Krewa (two to three days) is not narrated.

## Canon-doc conflicts (not edited)

- `world_rules/` and `wiki/` name **Gunther von Hohenstein** as the 1381 informant (master-chronology, pronunciation guide, faiths-clergy, houses-lineages "verify source"); the record (en wiki after Ivinskis) says Kuno von Liebenstein. `houses-lineages` already lists Liebenstein "verify offices".
- Canon dates "Autumn 1381" (Kestutis seizes Vilnius) are fine; "Vilnius reached by an open gate" in wiki Book I summaries should read "opened without a fight".
- Canon has Skirgaila receiving Trakai at the 1384/85 settlement ("Skirgaila keeps Trakai", antagonists page); the record gives it to him in 1382.
- Birutė's age/dates in `wiki` (c. 1330-1384) vs the record (death unknown; Wigand 1382, Constance old age).
- Hanul "opens a gate" in wiki/codex and continuity docs -> "takes the castle and hands it over".
- `books/1/references.bib` (outside this pass's edit scope) lists works I could not verify: Jurginis 1971 "Pagonybes ir krikscionybes santykiai Lietuvoje" (Mintis), the Nikon chronicle with publisher "Mokslas, Maskva, 1520", and a Beresnevicius entry whose key says 2000 but year 2004. `sources.qmd` lists none of them except the verifiable Dusburg, Dlugosz, Bumblauskas 2005 and Beresnevicius.
