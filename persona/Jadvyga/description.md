# Jadvyga (Jadwiga) of Anjou (The Saint-King)

**True Name**: Jadwiga (Lithuanian **Jadvyga**); crowned *Hedvig Rex*, King Jadwiga
**Title**: King of Poland (crowned 16 Oct 1384); wife of Jogaila (⚭ 18 Feb 1386)
**Age**: About 10 at her coronation, 12 at marriage (born 1373/74); dies aged about 25 on 17 Jul 1399
**Archetype**: The Sacrifice / The Holy Diplomat
**Pronunciation**: jad-VY-ga · /jɐdˈviːɡɐ/ · English sound-alike "yahd-VEE-gah" *(verify)*
**Power**: **Tier 5, the White Silence's unwilling vessel** (`world_rules/power_systems.md` §4; `hidden_immortals.md` §5). The White Silence's touch is granted, not asked: she heals with frost and holy calm, and warmth and sound leave her. At her death her body becomes the Force's anchor in the Polish heartland. She is its victim, not its servant.

## Appearance
*   Ethereal and delicate. Reddish-gold hair.
*   Dressed in the height of Western European fashion: crowns, silks and pearls.
*   Always carries a small black crucifix.

## Personality
*   **Wise beyond her years**: Crowned "King" at ten, she understood power while still a child.
*   **Deeply Pious**: She believes her marriage to the "barbarian" Jogaila is a mission from God to save the souls of Lithuania.
*   **Melancholic**: She had to give up her childhood betrothed, Wilhelm of Habsburg, for the good of her kingdom.

## Motivation
*   **The Mission**: The Christianisation of the North.
*   **Charity**: Building schools, helping the poor, endowing the Kraków academy (refounded 1400 with her bequest).
*   **Her Crown's Rights**: She defends the Polish crown's claims, including over Lithuania (the 1398 tribute demand).
