# Jogaila: Relationships

## Allies (Political)
*   **Jadvyga (Jadwiga), Wife (1386–99)**: The young King of Poland. Their marriage is political. She is pious; he is pragmatic. She holds the keys to his crown.
*   **Skirgaila (Brother)**: His enforcement arm. Brutal and loyal, doing the dirty work Jogaila won't touch.
*   **Julijona of Tver (Mother)**: Steered his succession in 1377; brokered the failed Moscow match (1384).
*   **Henė / Hanulas**: The Riga merchant who opens Vilnius to him (1382) and the jester at his side; the same Shadow-Seller.

## Enemies
*   **Vytautas (Cousin, *pusbrolis*)**: Jogaila respects Vytautas's skill but fears his popularity. He knows Vytautas is the only one who can unseat him.
*   **The Teutonic Order**: Despite his conversion, they don't believe him. They are an existential threat.

## Victims
*   **Kęstutis (Uncle, *dėdė*)**: Jogaila saw him as a relic of the past that had to be removed. Kęstutis had spared him in 1381.

## Later wives [Record]
*   Anna of Celje (1402), Elżbieta Granowska (1417), Sofija Alšėniškė / Sophia of Halshany (1422), mother of his sons.
