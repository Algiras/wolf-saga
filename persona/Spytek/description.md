# Spytek of Melsztyn (The Hussite Knight)

**Title**: Knight-magnate of the Crown; leader of the 1439 Hussite confederation (killed at Grotniki, 1439)
**Era**: Book V (*Vilko Palikimas*, The Wolf's Legacy)
**Archetype**: The Rebel Noble / The Progressive Warrior
**Pronunciation**: SPY-tek z mel-ŠTY-na · /ˈspɨtɛk z mɛlʂˈtɨna/ · English sound-alike "SPIH-tek z mel-SHTIH-nah"
**Power**: **Tier 0**.
**Note**: Not his namesake father, Spytek of Melsztyn the elder, who died at the Vorskla in 1399 (Book II).

## Appearance
*   Dashing and charismatic. He wears the latest Italian-style plate armour.
*   An open, friendly face that masks a fierce intellect.

## Personality
*   **Idealistic**: He is drawn to the Hussite movement's calls for reform.
*   **Rivalrous**: He despises the power of the Church (led by Oleśnicki) and sees the corruption in the Polish clergy.
*   **Allied to the East**: Unlike Oleśnicki, he favours a more equal partnership with Lithuania.

## Motivation
*   **Reform**: Breaking the power of the magnate oligarchy.
*   **Liberty**: Supporting the rights of the lesser nobility and the religious freedom of the Hussites.
