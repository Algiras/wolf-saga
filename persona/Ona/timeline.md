# Ona: Timeline

## Era: The Escape (Book I)
1.  **The Mourner (Aug 1382)**: After the arrest she comes to Krėva in mourning and is allowed to visit her husband.
2.  **The Plan**: Smuggles in a maid's dress, a knife and a sleep draught under her mourning cloak.
3.  **The Swap (autumn 1382)**: Cuts his hair and dresses him as a maid. Her maid Elena lies in his bed facing the wall. Vytautas walks out. Ona stays behind to delay discovery (ch. 13).
4.  **The Hostage (1383–84)**: Joins Vytautas in Prussia and is held by the Order as surety for his loyalty. Returns in 1384 after the reconciliation (ch. 28). *(The book must show how she leaves Krėva alive; see continuity C10.)*

## Later books (summary)
*   **Book II**: hostage again around 1390; the sons poisoned (1392); she stops looking at him. **Book III**: the diplomat. **Book IV**: dies at Trakai in 1418.
