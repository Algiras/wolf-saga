# Key Personas (Dramatis Personae)

*Names follow canon D4 (`world_rules/canon_decisions.md`). Full files live in the persona folders. Labels: **[Record]** = historical; **[Saga invention]** = ours.*

## House of the Wolf (The Gediminids)

### **Duke Kęstutis (The Old Boar)** [Record]
*   *Archetype*: Ned Stark. Honourable, warrior, pagan.
*   *Role*: Defender of the West (Trakai, Samogitia); co-ruler with Algirdas from 1345.
*   *Relationship*: Loyal brother to Algirdas; spared his nephew Jogaila in 1381, a mercy that killed him in 1382.

### **Duke Algirdas (The Silent Conqueror)** [Record]
*   *Archetype*: Stannis Baratheon (but successful). Stern, expansionist.
*   *Role*: Expands the realm east. Dies in May 1377, before Book I opens.
*   *Relationship*: Trusts only Kęstutis. Swore the Silent Pact with him in 1345 [Saga invention].

### **Jogaila (The Shadow King)** [Record]
*   *Archetype*: Littlefinger/Renly mix. Anxious, pragmatic, willing to convert for power.
*   *Goal*: Survival of his line; then the Polish crown (1386).
*   *Conflict*: Rivalry and blood-debt with Vytautas.

### **Skirgaila (The Iron Hand)** [Record]
*   *Archetype*: The overlooked enforcer. Book I's mortal antagonist ("the hands" to Jogaila's "will").

### **Vytautas (The Great Wolf)** [Record]
*   *Archetype*: Jon Snow / Robb Stark. A good man who becomes effective, and pays for it in people.
*   *Goal*: Avenge his father; then a sovereign Lithuania; in the end, the flame over the crown.
*   *Arc*: Escape from Krėva, alliance with enemies, victory at Grunwald, dies uncrowned (1430).

## The Antagonists

### **The Grand Masters of the Teutonic Order** [Record]
*   **Konrad Zöllner von Rotenstein** (1382–90), Book I; **Ulrich von Jungingen** (1407–10), Book III.
*   *Goal*: Conversion or conquest of the "last pagans of Europe".
*   *Methods*: The *Reise*, raids, treaties, hostages.

### **Henė, the Shadow-Seller** [Saga invention]
*   A shard of Velnias in mortal masks (Hanulas, then Henė the jester). The only antagonist present in every book.

## The Mystics

### **The Krivis of Šventaragis** [Saga invention]
*   *Role*: Keeper of the Silent Pact and of the eternal flame in Vilnius. Name to be chosen by the author.

### **Eglė** [Saga invention, on folk ground]
*   *Role*: Last *vaidilutė* of the Šventaragis flame; becomes Pelkių Eglė, the swamp's voice (D5).

### **Birutė** [Record + Saga invention]
*   *Role*: Palanga amber priestess, Kęstutis's wife, vessel who becomes the Amber Queen (D1).
