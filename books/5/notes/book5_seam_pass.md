# Book V seam pass

Continuity edit of ch 1-30 read in order, against the Book IV hand-off and its "Book IV seam pass". Minimal line edits only; no chapter rewritten, nothing committed, other books untouched.

## Decisions (the ledgers)

- **Oath years:** oath 1382 = 48 years in 1430; Last Flame lit 1387 = 43. Ch 1 (48) and ch 7 (43) already agree.
- **Keeper's sworn years:** krikštas at Kūčios 1405 = 25 years in 1430 (ch 1, 2), 34 in 1439 (ch 20), 44 at death (ch 28, 29). The outline's "45" is not used.
- **Wolf ledger:**
  - Kūčios 1430 (ch 7): 14 wolves = Pilkoji + Žiežirba + 12 others in the circle; 15 bowls, the fifteenth for Vytautas. Same 14 in autumn 1433 (ch 11).
  - Pabaiskas, 1 Sep 1435 (ch 13): 12 others + Pilkoji + Žiežirba. The night takes four: Kirmėlė, Lazdynas, Alksnis, Ąžuolas, so "dvylika virto aštuoniais" (8 others).
  - Winter 1435-36 (ch 16) takes five: Šermukšnis, Šiurpas, Kadagys, Erškėtis, Griovys. Left after Jurginės 1436: Pilkoji, Uosis, Spyglys, Dobilas + the keeper = "keturi, su juo penki" (ch 16, 21, 22, 24 "tris likusius").
  - Dobilas dies between 1441 and 1446, undated (ch 28). Uosis dies winter 1448-49 (ch 28). Pilkoji dies 2 Nov 1449 (ch 28).
- **Keeper's left arm (one progression):** ch 2 numb to the wrist for one night, feeling back by dawn, fingertips blue for a week; ch 13 numb to the elbow during the night's burning, two fingers dead for good by morning; ch 16 the numbness climbs month by month to the elbow by Christmas 1435 and stays (the "Unburned winter" arm of ch 20 and 27); ch 20 the right hand, now carrying the lantern, numb to the wrist for a week.
- **Pilkoji's age:** 70 in Sep 1435 (ch 13), 75 in Feb 1440 (ch 21), 76 in Jan 1441 (ch 24), 84 in 1449 (ch 28).
- **Pebble, first look:** June 1432, Rasos (ch 9). Ch 5 "pusantrų metų" after Kūčios 1430 fits; ch 27 now says 16 years (Sep 1448).
- **Birutė's rules (frame ch 1, 5, 10, 20, 25, 29):** (1) the crack hears both ways; (2) the pebble shows what lies before it and what its bearer feels, and amber from her shore remembers what it witnessed; (3) consecrated stone deafens and blinds her (ch 20); (4) so she sees Vasily II's blinding through **the first bead, the one that stayed with him in Moscow**, never through the second bead on Vytautas's slab; (5) her voice through the pebble ends at the name (ch 29), after that she hears only from her own shore.
- **Beads:** Sofija's boyar lays a **second bead** ("antrą karolį, iš to paties kranto; pirmąjį ji atidavė sūnui") on Vytautas's slab (ch 2); it is still on the slab in ch 20 and ch 25; ch 29 coda says she cannot hear it ("akmuo ten kurčias") and hears the Moscow bead.
- **Golden chain:** Kraków cathedral treasury, 8 years (Book IV ch 20, 30) -> Henė fetches it, stoneless, to the Krėva cell (ch 19, 14-15 Aug 1438) -> Žygimantas wears it and hears Kęstutis breathe (ch 19, 22) -> dust at his death (ch 22). Henė's stone: Jogaila's only, until ch 12; hands empty from then (ch 19, 24, 26, 30); bells wool-stuffed throughout.

## Unresolved items for the author

1. **Kūčios litany** (ch 29, 30) lists Pušis, Šešiapirštis, Kalvis, Jonas, Kirmėlė, Rūdis, Spyglys, Šiurpas, Šermukšnis, Dobilas, Uosis, Pilkoji; it omits Alksnis, Lazdynas, Ąžuolas, Kadagys, Erškėtis, Griovys. Fine as a family litany that has lost names, but add them if the ledger should be complete.
2. **Ch 18 / 8 / 21:** Žygimantas's "Ar jis kentėjo?" is said to have been asked "per Vytauto laidotuves" (ch 18, 21, 24) but ch 2 shows no exchange and ch 8 reads like a first meeting. Left as an offstage exchange at the funeral.
3. **Švitrigaila's Lutsk**: ch 3 (1431) and ch 15 (1436-37) are consistent with each other; history of Lutsk in 1431 (siege, truce) is Saga-thin, as the ch 3 notes say.
4. **Birutė's italics:** ch 20 and 25 frames are italic paragraphs; ch 1, 5, 10, 29 are plain first person. Harmonise if one format is wanted.
5. **Ch 1 vs ch 29 water levels:** ch 1 has the sea reach her knees "tonight", ch 29 the shoulders and chin the same evening; treated as one night, ch 1 being the opening of the long telling.
6. **Pilkoji's wolf-skin "prieš keturiasdešimt penkerius metus" (ch 28)** and "sponsorius" are unverified against Book II/III.
7. **Verify items still open from the chapter notes:** Jogaila at Grodno 15 Oct 1432 (ch 10); Ašmena geography; 1447 privilege date and clauses; Casimir's proclamation on 29 Jun 1440; Olaus Magnus's description ("ex-archbishop"); grandfather aged 94 in 1553 with 1466 at age seven.
