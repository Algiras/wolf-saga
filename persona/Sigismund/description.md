# Sigismund of Luxembourg (The Golden Emperor)

**Title**: King of Hungary (1387), King of the Romans (1411), King of Bohemia (1419/36); crowned Holy Roman Emperor only in 1433
**Era**: Book IV (*Nematoma Karūna*, The Invisible Crown): the Congress of Lutsk (1429) and the crown offered to Vytautas
**Archetype**: The Manipulator / The Grand Strategist
**Pronunciation**: (see pronunciation guide)
**Power**: **Tier 0**. The **Gold-Glutton** prospers around him, and **Henė** is the jester at his court in 1429–30 (D2): the crown he offers is the Shadow-Seller's last bargain.

## Appearance
*   Tall, with a meticulously groomed forked beard.
*   Always dressed in gold-threaded velvet. He smells of incense and expensive wine.
*   Possesses a magnetic, actor-like presence.

## Personality
*   **Charming and Deceptive**: The ultimate diplomat. He uses people's ambitions against them.
*   **Visionary**: He wants a united Christendom under his leadership, chiefly to fight the Ottoman Turks.
*   **Opportunistic**: He offers Vytautas the crown mainly to break the Polish-Lithuanian union, which he sees as a threat to his own power.

## Motivation
*   **Chaos**: To keep his neighbours fighting each other so he remains the arbiter.
*   **Defence of Christendom**: He truly fears the Ottoman advance and seeks allies.
