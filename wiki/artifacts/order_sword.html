Kryžiaus kalavijas (the Order’s sword)¶

Description¶

A heavy straight German sword with a cross-shaped hilt, consecrated at Marienburg; it smells of cold steel and “a new religion” (Book I ch. 19, 24).

Kryžiaus kalavijas (the Order’s sword)

Type

artifact

Years

made 1383

Note

Kryžiaus kalavijas: consecrated broadsword of the Teutonic Knights bearing the cruciform cross.

Power¶

It dampens Kalba around it (Power systems §5). While Vytautas carries it, his pebble cannot answer, his blood feels strange and his Five-Voice is closed to him. It also marks him publicly as the Order’s vassal.

Rule & cost¶

Consecrated metal smothers amber and fire. It can be removed only by a free act: Vytautas must put it in fire himself (Book I ch. 24). As the replacement of Kęstutis’s sword it bears no history, only a debt.

Origin¶

[Historical record] Konrad Zöllner von Rotenstein was Grand Master 5 Oct 1382-20 Aug 1390; Vytautas was baptised “Wigand” at Tapiau on 21 Oct 1383 and swore vassalage by the Treaty of Königsberg, 30 Jan 1384. [The saga’s invention] The sword is presented to him at the baptism by Zöllner (Book I ch. 19), made by the Order for its protégé.

Arc across the books¶

  • Vytautas, Oct 1383-Jul 1384. It knocks his thigh on the Order’s march into Lithuania (Book I ch. 20). He fights his first battle against his own people with it (ch. 21).

  • July 1384. He unbuckles it at Marienwerder (Naujapilis) and throws it into the burning tower, whereupon the pebble lights and the silence lifts (I ch. 23-24; Marienwerder).

  • After. No other holder. Later Order swords at Grunwald (III ch. 18, the “two swords” of the record) are a separate matter.
