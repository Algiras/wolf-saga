Dievai¶

God

Description

Aušrinė (the morning star)

Morning Star; goddess of dawn, first celestial light, and beauty.

Bangpūtys

God of tempestuous sea winds, surging waves, and stormy Baltic waters.

Dievas (Senelis)

The Sky Elder (Senelis); primordial remote supreme god of Baltic cosmology.

Gabija

Goddess of the sacred domestic hearth flame; protector of homes and vows.

Giltinė

Mirties ir kūniškojo išėjimo deivė; Laimos sesuo ir atsvara.

Jūratė

Goddess of the Baltic Sea; queen of the amber palace beneath the waves.

Laima

Goddess of fate, fortune, individual destiny, and childbirth.

Medeina

Goddess of forests, wild beasts, wolves, and the wilderness hunt.

Mėnuo

The Moon god; night warrior, celestial husband of Saulė.

Perkūnas

God of thunder, lightning, oak groves, justice, weapons, and warrior oaths.

Praurimė

Doubtful: known mainly from 19th-century mythographers (Narbutt); authenticity disputed. The saga keeps her as the Stars element.

Saulė

The Sun goddess; celestial mother, source of life, light, and warming grace.

Velnias

Lord of the underworld, the dead, subterranean waters, cattle, wealth, and bargains.

Vėjopatis

Lord of winds and breezes; master of sea-winds and storm paths.

Žemyna

Earth goddess; mother and nourisher of all living beings, sworn by earth in oaths.
