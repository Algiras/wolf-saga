# Uliana of Tver (The Dowager Wolf)

**True Name**: Uliana Alexandrovna of Tver; in Lithuanian, **Julijona Tverietė** (folder renamed `Uliana_Tveriete` so she is not confused with Julijona Alšėniškė)
**Title**: Grand Duchess of Lithuania (⚭ Algirdas 1349), Dowager Grand Duchess from 1377
**Age**: Late 50s in 1382 (born c. 1325). Dies 17 Mar 1391
**Archetype**: The Matriarch / The Chessmaster
**Pronunciation**: ju-li-jo-NA · /jʊlʲɪjoˈnɐ/ · English sound-alike "yoo-lih-yoh-NAH" *(verify)*
**Power**: **Tier 0**. Orthodox; no gift and no bargain. Her power is the dynasty.
**Not to be confused with**: **Julijona Alšėniškė** (Uliana Olshanska), Vytautas's second wife (⚭ 1418), who has no folder.

## Appearance
*   Dignified and regal. Her face is a map of decades of political survival.
*   Always wears Orthodox icons hidden beneath Lithuanian patterns.
*   Highly literate, often found with scrolls in a court of warriors.

## Personality
*   **Calculating**: She managed the transition of power from Algirdas to Jogaila (1377), over Algirdas's elder sons by Maria of Vitebsk.
*   **Devout**: While she accepted a pagan court, she pushed her children toward Orthodoxy and the East.
*   **Pragmatic**: She cares about the dynasty, not the gods.

## Motivation
*   **Dynasty**: Ensuring her sons (Jogaila's line) rule above all others.
*   **Eastward Shift**: Pulling Lithuania away from the Teutonic West and toward the Rus' principalities. She is Tver's woman in Vilnius, and Tver is Moscow's great rival.
