# Tokhtamysh (The Exile Khan)

**Title**: Khan of the Golden Horde (deposed); Vytautas's guest and ally from c. 1395–97
**Era**: Book II (*Auksinis Šešėlis*, The Golden Shadow): refuge in Lithuania and the Vorskla (1399). Dies 1406
**Archetype**: The Fallen King / The Desperate Ally
**Pronunciation**: (see pronunciation guide)
**Power**: **Tier 0**. The Steppe-Hunger's lure made flesh: through him Vytautas is offered the vision of an empire to the Black Sea and beyond.

## Appearance
*   Dressed in tattered but fine silk robes and a silver-plated headdress.
*   Wears his beard in the Mongol style.
*   Once a figure of immense power, now he looks hunted, his eyes reflecting the loss of an empire.

## Personality
*   **Arrogant and Proud**: Even in exile, he treats Vytautas as an equal or a subordinate, which creates friction.
*   **Vengeful**: His only goal is to reclaim the Horde and see Timur's and Edigu's heads on spears.
*   **Unreliable**: He makes grand promises of tribute and territory that he no longer controls.

## Motivation
*   **The Golden Throne**: To return to Sarai and rule the steppe once more.
*   **Vengeance**: Against Timur (Tamerlane) and the turncoat Edigu.
