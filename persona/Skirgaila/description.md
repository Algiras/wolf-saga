# Skirgaila (The Iron Hand)

**True Name**: Skirgaila; Orthodox name Ivan; baptised Catholic Kazimierz (1386)
**Title**: Duke of Polotsk; Duke of **Trakai** (1382/84–1392); Jogaila's regent in Lithuania (from 1387); Duke of Kyiv (1395–97)
**Age**: About 28 in 1382 (born c. 1354). Dies in Kyiv, 11 Jan 1397
**Archetype**: The Enforcer / The Machiavellian Brother
**Pronunciation**: skir-GAI-la · /skʲɪrˈɡɐɪɫɐ/ · English sound-alike "skihr-GUY-lah"
**Power**: **Tier 0**. **Bought** power: bargained with the Shadow-Seller at Krėva (the price: to be used and discarded); Henė collects him in 1397. He broke his own **safe-conduct oath** (Aug 1382), so Perkūnas's enmity follows his line (`world_rules/power_systems.md` §2.6).

## Appearance
*   Strong, martial bearing. Often seen in armour, even off the field.
*   Cold, calculating expression. He has the "look of a man who knows where the bodies are buried, because he put them there."
*   Prominent scar across his knuckles.

## Personality
*   **Brutal and Loyal**: He is Jogaila's shadow. He does the things Jogaila cannot or will not do, and is never thanked.
*   **Ruthless**: Suspected of the murder of his uncle Kęstutis. He believes that the survival of the dynasty justifies any crime.
*   **Hard-Drinking**: Known for bouts of drinking followed by explosive violence or deep depression.

## Motivation
*   **Security**: Ensuring Jogaila's throne is absolute.
*   **Recognition**: Being seen as more than "the hands". Trakai is the prize he finally gets.
