Surety until Book I ch. 27 (1384). The bond itself, and the pebble as its barometer, until 1430

One anchor per caster. The pebble answers to the bearer's truth. After 1384 there is no surety: he pays full price
Counters
Consecrated stone deafens the pebble; the Order's sword closes the Five-Voice; a binder's own death
Effect

The promises. Hers: Jo skola, mano skola (his debt is my debt). His: Kol aš gyvas, paskutinė ugnis neužges (while I live, the last fire will not go out).

The Five-Voice. Lets an ungifted bearer call a little of every boon.

At greater cost

Birutė's decline. Voice failing after the Krėva fog, sight going, until ch. 27 at Palanga.

After the surety. Book II ch. 2: the spring damp doubled the cost, and Birutė was not beside him.

Breaking it

The penalties. Loss of gift, curse of the elements, memory loss, madness, death in the extreme.

The promise. Failure of the promise means letting the flame die while he lives.

Note

Birutė’s ancient binding spoken over Vytautas in 1382 to preserve his life.

Terms¶

[The saga’s invention], made in Book I ch. 3 (1382): the Senovės Sutartis by which Birutė binds her son Vytautas. Four requirements, all on the page: amber charged by the binder (the pebble); the binder’s blood on it (a crescent vaidilutė knife across her left palm); the bound one’s true name (slaptas vardas), a name he has never heard, spoken by her; and an unbreakable promise both ways. Hers: “Jo skola — mano skola” (“his debt is my debt”). His: “Kol aš gyvas, paskutinė ugnis neužges.” (the flame promise).

Parties & witnesses¶

Binder and surety: Birutė, who stands laidas for the bound. Bound: Vytautas (“surištas”, the third path to Kalba). Witness: the elements, and the Amber Queen, to whose request Birutė answers “if someone must pay, let it be me, not him.” She comes to it half-blind from the Praurimė vision at Dagotuvės. The pebble (Motinos gintaras) is the token; see the event.

Breaches¶

Penalties are unchanged from the old doc: loss of gift, curse of the elements, memory loss, madness, death in the extreme. A cost can be deferred, not refused, and deferred costs compound. Failure of the promise: letting the flame die while he lives.

Consequences¶

Surety. Part of every price Vytautas incurs is paid by Birutė wherever she is: his costs look light in Book I while her voice and sight fail (the Krėva fog, ch. 14, 25). In ch. 27 (1384) she pays the whole remaining debt at once at Palanga (the sacrifice), and from Book II there is no surety: at the foundations of the island castle “the spring damp doubled it, and Birutė was not beside him” (Book II ch. 2); Vorskla has no mother’s fog, only shame. The pebble stays the bond’s token and barometer.
