# Book III, Part 1 (ch. 1–10): notes for other parts

## Dates and places used
- **Ch. 1:** Jan 1400, thaw then frost; Dirvėnai (Gedgaudas's village on the Dubysa), Medvėgalis hill. Ch. 1 is placed in winter 1399–1400, i.e. just after Book II's seam (Dec 1399 Marienburg, Galminas hearing the Grand Master's hall beam).
- **Ch. 2:** spring 1400 (Dnieper ice-break), Skirgaila's Kyiv estate; three weeks' road north; Trakai island.
- **Ch. 3:** Vilnius, Lower Castle, 18 Jan 1401; Radom, 11 Mar 1401 (Polish lords; archbishop of Gniezno unnamed); Jogaila at Wawel four days later; March frost on Jadwiga's tomb.
- **Ch. 4:** April 1401, the Order's new fort at the Dubysa ford; two more forts burn the same week (Venta bank, Neman bend; none named, "verify which"). Smolensk revolt reported by Kirmėlė (Yuri Svyatoslavich back with Ryazan help).
- **Ch. 5:** Jan–Feb 1402; Marienburg (Konrad von Jungingen), then a nine-day winter Reise to Medininkai (burned); Jogaila's wedding (29 Jan) is "two days ago" in the Medininkai tent scene.
- **Ch. 6:** 22 May 1404, Raciąż, a parish church and tents; Smolensk retaken "before winter" 1404 (no battle stated).
- **Ch. 7:** Vėlinės 1404 (village Kalniškės) to the nine nights after Kūčios 1404/05 (Dykra, western/Samogitian side).
- **Ch. 8:** 13 Sep 1405, eve of Dagotuvės, Trakai island castle (two wings finished, third in scaffolding).
- **Ch. 9:** Kūčios 1405 (midnight rite in the vault), nine nights in the southern Dykra, return early Jan 1406.
- **Ch. 10:** Moscow winter 1406 (Pskov war), Ugra stand-off Aug 1408, Švitrigaila arrives in Moscow autumn 1408, Edigu before Moscow Dec 1408 (chapter ends before the ransom/withdrawal).

## Facts established (please respect)
**Vytautas (Form 6).** Black plain armour, Kęstutis's sword on his belt, unwashed ("Rūdys yra sąskaita"). Hands grey with Kalvis's ash (never washed, visible to everyone including Sofija in 1408). Right ear works, left is deaf; sight blurred beyond two steps and cannot read (needs a scribe: Danila at Kyiv). He hears the Counter's voice under the Kyiv estate: "Trisdešimt" (30 years left, in 1400; consistent with Book II's 35 from 1395). He left his brown Ruthenian kaftan hanging in the empty sword chest in Kyiv; the Kyiv estate was sold, the stripped Pechersk icon oklads returned, the debts paid to the Order. Henė is absent through 1400 (reappears 1401).
**Pebble:** cold through 1404 (explicitly "five years" in 1405). Stirs once at the refusal in ch. 8 ("kaip pavargusios rankos pirštas", a single tremor, then nothing; later "truputį šilta" when he tells Ona). In ch. 9 it turns warm briefly when Žiežirba's name goes into the flame (Vytautas raises his hand to his chest). Do not let Part 2 call it warm before ch. 17; "stirring" is the most it has done.
**The sword (ch. 2):** found by Spyglys's scent in the third of twelve oak chests; rust along the *perkūnėlis* under the grip, one blot per oath-break (Dovydiškės, Skirgaila's safe-conduct, Tepliava baptism, the Order oath/Marienwerder, Lyck, Vorskla). Rusted to the guard; not cleaned. Žiežirba: "Jam skauda. Po rankena." Vytautas: "Mumis." (Rust "stopped spreading" at the oath in ch. 17 is Part 2's business.)
**Ona** looks at him again, speaks plainly. She does not say whether she knows about the flame, but she keeps sending him down to the vault ("Berniukas klausia apie tave") and ran the boy's **šermenys** (wake supper) before the krikštas, never speaking his name, only "vaikeli". She taught him to read (confirmed by ch. 21 in Part 3, consistent).
**Henė:** wears the golden chain (from Skirgaila's death) openly in 1405; **two stones** throughout (ch. 5: "Trečias nukrito į grindis Kijeve"). Švitrigaila **has no stone**: "Tu dar nesvertas." Motif **kreida/anglis** (chalk/charcoal) from Book II ch. 12: Henė offers to write Švitrigaila's name in charcoal; he says "Ne šiandien". Henė appears in ch. 3 (drafted the clause *po jo mirties*), ch. 5, ch. 8. Never lies in a deal, "aš tik užrašau".
**Henė's offer (ch. 8):** on the eve of Dagotuvės (Žemyna's zenith), Švitrigaila's blood spilled by **any Gediminid hand** on Medvėgalis hill pays "the old debt" (royal blood for land lost); Order broken in a season; but the contract says "kol skola nesumokėta", and blood breeds a new debt, so it renews the Pact's price for **every generation** of Trakai's rulers. Vytautas refuses with the Ona beat; this is the first deal he has refused since saying *sutinku* at the Vorskla (see deviation below).
**Švitrigaila:** fled Vitebsk Dec 1401 with six men; Order recognised him in a parchment with three seals; Order released him after Raciąż (Konrad: "Dabar durys atviros"); lives at the Peninsula Castle in Trakai from spring 1404, jokes loudly, blushes when answered truthfully (Book II tic). Defects to Moscow autumn 1408 with thirty boyars; Vasilijus gives him towns. Tells Sofija: "Tavo tėvas turi daugiau skolų, nei žino."
**Jogaila:** at Vilnius in Jan 1401 he nearly says "dėde" to Vytautas and asks about wine instead. His secret (Krėva) is **not specified**: only "the pause before the word", a silence that was an answer, and he is no longer sure whether it was a question (kept deliberately open for ch. 24/27). Jadvyga's three requests: keep the Kraków school, money for the Vilnius church ("jie turi savo kalbą"), go to Vytautas without the king's voice. At her tomb sound dies at the third pillar, frost in March; his palm froze for four days. He agrees to the Celje match (Anna ~21).
**The Samogitian cord (ch. 1, 4, 6):** the Medvėgalis žynys ties one knot per oak felled for the Order's forts. He unties all **140** when the first fort burns (ch. 4); the second cord (**2,653 knots**) is brought to Raciąż in a loaf by Gedgaudas's daughter-in-law and hangs on a hook in the Trakai vault by the flame. Žiežirba: "Jie dar žali."
**The Medvėgalis žynys (unnamed throughout):** tier 4. His grove fire "neteisėta jokiame krašte" *leans east* "nuo to pavasario, kai Vilniuje užgesino jų šventąją ugnį", twelve years in 1400; he does not know what burns there and does not want to. This is what ch. 17 pays off. He keeps the 1329 baptismal shirts in the grove oak. He told Galminas the first knot "tik vieną". After the burnt offering of ch. 4 he says to Galminas "Tu galėjai pasakyti 'ne'... Tik žinok."
**Galminas:** does not eat (women leave food on a stone), bleeds light green sap; after the burning of the Order's fort commander (ch. 4) the sap in his palm is **black** and he cannot separate his pity from the Root-Mother's pleasure. In ch. 1 he heard the Marienburg beam's words ("Kitą kartą mes juos baigsime") and told the žynys it was the younger brother (Ulrich). He can soften ground under cavalry (ch. 4).
**Hostages (ch. 1):** 41 boys aged 8–14, one per elder, taken from Dirvėnai to Königsberg ("Kai taika sustiprės, jie grįš"). The komtur of the escort is unnamed. Gedgaudas's grandson **Daumantas** (11) takes grandfather's mittens; his mother is the woman at Raciąż. Unresolved: do the hostages return? (Konrad at Raciąż repeats "kai taika sustiprės".)
**Ch. 4:** the Order's fort commander (unnamed *pilies vaitas*, 50–60, says he did three Reises, 23 villages, 64 homesteads) is burned on his horse by a **Šatrija žynys (tier 3, broken nose, unnamed)** as a Perkūnas offering; he sings *Salve Regina*; first spring thunder follows. No illusion, no magic spent by the žynys. Kirmėlė brings 60 crossbows in salt wagons; Vytautas's deniability ("Kunigaikštis nieko nedavė").
**Konrad / Ulrich (ch. 5, 6):** Konrad privately tells Vytautas Ulrich "tiki, kad Dievo valia yra smūgis, aš, kad ji yra pergamentas" and wants "dokumento, kurio niekas negalėtų panaikinti, išskyrus Dievą". **Ulrich speaks fluent Lithuanian** (learned "iš priešo") and says to Vytautas at Raciąż: "Jūs gražiai prisiekėte... Todėl laukiu." (His "next time" carry-over.) Brother **Dietrichas** (limp, kind, from Book II) escorts Švitrigaila; says of the sons' deaths: "Nežinau."
**Medininkai (ch. 5):** castellan Daugirdas (old, taught Švitrigaila archery) refuses to open and withdraws the garrison; the Order burns it; Švitrigaila's one act of will is to open the side gate for the village women and children.
**Raciąż (ch. 6):** the Order drops Švitrigaila; Samogitia sold again so that "Ordinas man už nugaros" does not interfere with Smolensk. Jogaila (42) asks "ar tu mane nekenti?" (first time in 22 years). Vytautas: "Neturiu laiko tam."
**Pilkoji (ch. 7):** see deviation. Born c. 1366 (38 in 1404), village layer-out of the dead and midwife; always carries hearth-ash in her apron pocket (confirmed by ch. 29 of Part 3); husband drowned in the Dubysa 1397; daughter (14) died of fever Sept 1404 and was buried in the new churchyard; at Vėlinės 1404 the buried dead of Kalniškės (Unburned crossed with hungry vėlės) rise, sit at the cold hearths and take warmth; she names them by their old names (Kantrimas, Mingailė, Daugirdas); the village priest (unnamed Samogitian) helps with his candle after holy water fails; at dawn the dead walk NW into the Dykra and the bog. She follows; **Pušis** and two young wolves stop her; the bog (Root-Mother) decides "paprotys yra tai, kas jau buvo padaryta, ne tai, kas turi būti; Jos tai nebesaisto". Price: name, hearth, road up, "Tik pelkę ir mus". She keeps "Pilkoji" as her skin-name. Delay: six weeks learning, nine nights hunted (caught three times), drowning on the ninth night; **she cannot weep**.
**Žiežirba (ch. 9):** 14, almost 15. His true name is **not known to anyone, including him**: it was kept by his mother's coal; at the rite he speaks it "kaip kvėpavimas po šalčio" and forgets it at once (rule 4); he remembers only the shape of his mother's lips. Sponsor (*vilktėvis*): **Kirmėlė**, staking his life ("tai bus pirmas dalykas per daugelį metų, kuris man pasiseks pagal planą"); Pušis present; Pilkoji (jauniklė) hunts and, on the fourth night, teaches him to burrow in snow and to breathe on his hands "kaip į anglį" ("Nesakyk, kad kalbėjau su tavimi"). Throat-mark cut with a bone knife by Kirmėlė and live ash rubbed in. Wolf-hide is Kirmėlė's old wolf. Oath on the **Iron Wolf's skull** (grey, light, on a pine stump in the oldest bog; Pack's Word recited). **Cost:** cannot weep, and **cannot sleep under the roof of the lit vault** (iron_wolves §5.2): from January 1406 he sleeps on the top stair outside the vault door, and Vytautas promises him "lova ten, kur šviesa nepasiekia". The flame still leans to him across the threshold.
**Sofija (ch. 10):** Moscow; the Kremlin cathedral stone is cold, her amber bead (from Birutė at 9) is cold in the chapel, weak by the Moskva, **hot at the Ugra** with a sound of surf behind her. Children named: **Ivan** (12 in 1408) and **Anna** (15). Vasily sleeps with a hand on a dagger. She carries Ona's herb pouch and the note "jei kas nors staiga susirgtų, paklausk, ką jis valgė". Ugra: sudden illness in both camps (rumour of poison) is traced to a backwater with three dead horses, fish and crayfish; Vytautas has to ask "Ar tu valgei?" because Ona told him to. Vasily calls him *uošvi*, Vytautas calls him *žente*; the Ugra is the border. Dec 1408: Vasily goes to Kostroma to raise troops (record says he left Moscow; her staying is invented); Moscow burns its own suburbs; snow-devils stand around Edigu's campfires; horsehair on the well rope in the Kremlin. Her unsent letter: "Maskva dabar mano, ir aš neišvažiuosiu. Jei kada nors tau jos prireiks, paprašyk manęs."

## Objects and magic ledger (Part 1)
- No mortal working over a person's scale. The costs on the page: Galminas's sap (black); Pilkoji's tears and road up; Žiežirba's name, tears, bed; Kirmėlė's life staked; Vytautas's hearing/sight (no new cost). The one casting-level event, the **name-to-fire** in ch. 9, uses no formula (an exchange with the flame itself).
- Henė's stones: two. Golden chain: on Henė. Silver wolf: on Vytautas (Jogaila cannot look at it for longer than "tris atodūsius"). Kneeling statue: not visited in Part 1.
- Unbinding level 3 shown by: the Kalniškės dead; Henė's offer; the Reise dead are not shown.
- Wolves: Pušis, Kirmėlė, Spyglys, Pilkoji, Žiežirba (candidate to wolf in Part 1). Brotherhood still has no Wolf-Father.

## Deviations from the outline (and why)
- **Pilkoji (ch. 7):** the outline says a fourteen-year-old girl. **Canon Q13's default** (canon_decisions.md) says "Make Pilkoji older (born c. 1365)" because the Flame-Keeper is born c. 1390 and she is his foster-mother. I wrote her as a 38-year-old village layer-out and midwife whose own 14-year-old daughter is among the risen dead. This makes her about 83 in 1449 (Book V ch. 28, "Pilkoji Atsigula") which is long-lived but she is a wolf; Book V may want to say so.
- **Ch. 8:** the outline says "first deal he has turned down since Krėva". Book II ch. 9 already shows a refusal (the ragana's offer, 1392), so the text says "pirmasis dalykas, kurį atsisakei nuo tada, kai pasakei *sutinku*" (since Book II ch. 20).
- **Ch. 1:** the opening is set in January 1400 (not "winter 1399–1400" vaguely) so that Galminas has already heard the Marienburg beam (Book II epilogue); the elders' children are taken the same winter.
- **Ch. 3:** I put Jogaila physically in Vilnius for the 18 Jan 1401 act and in Radom/Kraków in March (record: the Radom act was by the Polish lords; his presence at both is plausible but verify).
- **Ch. 5:** Medininkai is burned in a winter Reise (Feb 1402) with Brother Dietrichas present; the outline's "Henė's smile at Trakai" is the Trakai-stable memory from Book II ch. 12.
- **Ch. 6:** terms limited to "Samogitia sold again; Order drops Švitrigaila", nothing on Dobrzyń (verify).
- **Ch. 10:** the outline's "grandfather and son-in-law, two riders" is rendered as father-in-law/son-in-law (Vytautas is the *grandfather* of Sofija's children, which the text does not stress). The "rides between camps" is a ford crossing on horseback; the illness cause (dead horses) is invented.

## Canon questions
- Ulrich speaking fluent Lithuanian is new (Part 2 ch. 11 and the Grunwald chapters should not contradict).
- The tally-cord and the second cord's count (2,653) are Saga invention; the žynys's flame leaning east and the vault cord matter for ch. 17.
- Whether the Order returns any of the 41 hostages: left open ("kai taika sustiprės").
- The cost of the first Perkūnas offering (ch. 4) was kept moral (Galminas's black sap), not magical.
- Švitrigaila's presence at Trakai 1404–08 is invented; Book V says Vytautas kept him at Kremenets c. 1409–18 (he can be sent there after 1408 by Part 2/3 if needed).
