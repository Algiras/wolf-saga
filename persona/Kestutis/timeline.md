# Kęstutis: Timeline

## Era: The Fall (Book I)
1.  **The Guardian**: Defends the western borders while Jogaila, Grand Duke since 1377, plots in the east.
2.  **The Discovery (1381)**: Warned by Kuno von Liebenstein, komtur of Osterode, learns of Jogaila's secret deal with **the Order** (Treaty of Dovydiškės, 31 May 1380).
3.  **The Confrontation (autumn 1381)**: Seizes Vilnius, captures Jogaila, reads the treaty, and releases him to Krėva and Vitebsk.
4.  **The Loss of Vilnius (spring–Jun 1382)**: While he campaigns against Kaributas, Jogaila retakes the city.
5.  **The Trap (early Aug 1382)**: Comes to parley at Jogaila's camp near Trakai under Skirgaila's safe-conduct. Seized with Vytautas and sent to Krėva.
6.  **The End (15 Aug 1382, the Assumption)**: Found strangled in Krėva with a golden chain, the Riga-made "gift" that came through Hanulas (Henė's mask). Jogaila claims suicide.
7.  **The Pyre (Aug 1382)**: Cremated in Vilnius with full pagan rites. Kin-blood breaks clause 2 of the Silent Pact.
