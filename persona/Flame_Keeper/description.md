# The Flame-Keeper (Ugnies Sargas)

**True Name**: **To be chosen by the author.** As a sworn wolf he will bear a skin-name (*kailio vardas*) [Saga invention]
**Title**: Guard of the secret flame in the Trakai island vault; the **last sworn Iron Wolf**
**Era**: Book II (rescued as a child), Books III–IV (the vault), **Book V protagonist** (1430–50)
**Age**: Born c. 1390; about 40 when the flame goes out (1430)
**Archetype**: The Last Keeper / The Witness
**Pronunciation**: (see pronunciation guide)
**Power**: **Granted**, the **Wolf-Change** (outside the tier ladder), if he is sworn before 1430 (open question 1). He carries two artifacts in Book V: **Kęstutis's silver wolf** (from Pilkoji) and **Birutė's pebble**, which he finds cold after Vytautas's death (`world_rules/power_systems.md` §5).

## Origin [Saga invention]
*   A boy rescued during the first **Unbinding** hunts of Book II, when his village's unfed dead rose.
*   Sworn young and raised in the Trakai vault as the flame's secret guard; **Pilkoji**, the only *Vilkų Motina*, is his foster-mother in all but name.

## Appearance
*   Lean, pale from years underground; smoke-darkened hands; a throat-mark from the krikštas.
*   Carries the ash-pot, the wolves' most important office after 1387.

## Personality
*   **Faithful to a fire, not a man**: He has kept the flame longer than he has known anything else.
*   **Watchful**: He has seen Vytautas old, half-deaf and alone in the vault, and knows what the flame cost.
*   **Stubborn, then wise**: He begins certain the flame can be relit and ends knowing it cannot.

## Want / need / arc (Book V)
*   **Want**: Relight the flame after Vytautas's death (27 Oct 1430).
*   **Antagonists**: **Žygimantas Kęstutaitis**, who would use him, and the unbound dead (Pabaiskas, 1435).
*   **Arc**: He learns the Pact cannot be re-sworn by one man, and lets the gods **become stories** (the Law of Memory: "the land remembers"). At the close he chooses whether to lie down in the bog or go home and be named by the fire one last time. The author decides.
*   **Frame**: Birutė narrates his story as the last thing she remembers before the sea takes her statue.
