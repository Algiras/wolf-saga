## Premise

The Grand Duchy is the last pagan realm in Europe, and it should not have survived the Order's century of crusade. In 1345 two brothers, Algirdas and Kęstutis, push their brother Jaunutis off his chair and, in the Šventaragis valley at night, swear the **Silent Pact** with the old gods: the land will not be conquered while a Gediminid keeps the sacred flames, while no Gediminid sheds Gediminid blood, and while royal blood is paid for land lost. Kęstutis, the younger brother with his hand on the hilt, says the last clause aloud, and his brother never forgives him for it. Across thirty years of war Kęstutis pays the Pact's price in kin and keeps his word even when it costs him: he marries an amber priestess of Palanga by a trick the gods must accept, swears a pagan oath to a Christian king that he keeps to the letter, escapes Marienburg by wit alone, and buries his brother by fire in 1377. **The reader learns the Pact's three clauses here, so every breach in Book I lands.**

## Magic and power state at the start (1345; graph progressions)

- **Silent Pact:** none before ch. 4; **sworn** in 1345 (ch. 4). It **weakens** in 1377 when Algirdas dies (ch. 29).
- **Unbinding level:** **0**. Spirits keep custom; the žaltys guards the house; the wolves police the old courtesy.
- **Practitioners:** tier 4: **6** · tier 3: **60** · tier 2: **600** (falling to about 4 / 40 / 400 by 1382; the losses are the war's). The tier-4s include **the Krivis** of Šventaragis *(suggested name Sirvydas, `religion_and_clergy.md` §2.2)* and **Birutė** of the Palanga line.
- **The Amber Queen:** walks through a living vessel of the Palanga line (Birutė's grandmother, the "old priestess" of `legends_and_songs.md` §5) *(Saga suggestion)*. Birutė is chosen as the next and last.
- **The pebble:** **does not exist yet** (made in 1382). **Henė's stones:** **none** (made in 1380).
- **Iron Wolves:** about **100**, rising toward 120; sworn to the Root-Mother (body) and the Šventaragis flame (name). Kęstutis is given the silver wolf in 1345.
- **Artifacts:** Kęstutis's sword (forged for him, a *perkūnėlis* under the grip, still bright); the Iron Wolf's skull (since 1323); the silver wolf (1345).
- **The Dykra:** the Wildnis is being emptied by raids and taken by the Root-Mother (ch. 9).

## Hands on to Book I

Kęstutis alone holds the oath, bound by his word to protect the nephew who will kill him; Jogaila Grand Duke, Uliana behind him, Maria's sons passed over; the Pact **weakened**; the Shadow-Seller studying the heir; the hidden nine at Kęstutis's side and Šešiapirštis grey; Birutė, the Amber Queen's chosen vessel, dreaming of a Black Ship; Vytautas the Son, married to Ona, father of Sofija, friend of his cousin.
