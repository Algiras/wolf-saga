# Danutė Kęstutaitė (The Masovian Sister)

**True Name**: Danutė; baptised Ona (Anna) [Record]
**Title**: Duchess of Masovia (Warsaw, Czersk), wife of **Janusz I of Masovia** (⚭ c. 1373; *verify the date*)
**Era**: Book I (Vytautas's refuge, late 1382); a background ally through Book IV
**Age**: About 24 in 1382 (born c. 1358; *verify*). Dies 1448
**Archetype**: The Safe Harbour / The Clear-Eyed Sister
**Pronunciation**: da-NU-tė · /dɐˈnʊtʲeː/ · English sound-alike "dah-NOO-tay"
**Power**: **Tier 0** as written. *Open question*: the gift runs down the **maternal line** (`world_rules/magic_rules.md` §2). As Birutė's daughter she could carry a latent amber gift that never woke (or that the Church in Warsaw taught her to fear).

## Record [Record]
*   Daughter of Kęstutis (the saga treats all his children as Birutė's).
*   Married into the Masovian Piasts, vassals of the Polish crown and neighbours of the Order. From 1387 her sister-in-law at Płock is **Aleksandra Algirdaitė** (Jogaila's sister), who marries Siemowit IV that year.
*   **Vytautas fled through Masovia** after Krėva, to Danutė and Janusz, before going to the Order (late 1382).

## In the saga [Saga invention]
*   The one Kęstutaitis who married into Christendom early and lived quietly; she lights a candle in church and a splinter at home.
*   Takes in the shaved, starving brother in a maid's dress without a word of mockery, and sends him on to Prussia with Janusz's guides.

## Relationships
*   **Vytautas (Brother)**: Shelters him in 1382; a steady ally all her life.
*   **Janusz I (Husband)**: Vytautas's brother-in-law and steady ally.
*   **Rimgailė (Sister)**: Marries Henryk of Masovia in 1392.
*   **Aleksandra Algirdaitė**: Jogaila's sister, who marries her husband's brother in 1387: the daughters of Algirdas and Kęstutis end up sisters-in-law in Masovia while their brothers fight.
