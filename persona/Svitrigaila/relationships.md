# Švitrigaila: Relationships

## Kin
*   **Jogaila**: He resents his brother's authority and frequently rebels against him.
*   **Skirgaila**: Fears and hates him. Skirgaila often treats him like a child.
*   **Julijona of Tver**: His mother. She favoured him, which likely fed his sense of entitlement.
*   **Kęstutis**: His uncle (*dėdė*).

## Allies (Temporary)
*   **The Teutonic Knights**: Frequently seeks their aid in exile (1402; the Christmemel alliance, 1431).
*   **The Orthodox Lords**: They support him against Polish-Catholic influence.

## Enemies
*   **Vytautas**: A rival for the leadership of those who oppose Polish influence; his gaoler at Kremenets (c. 1409–18).
*   **Žygimantas Kęstutaitis**: Overthrows him in the coup of 1 Sep 1432; defeats him at Pabaiskas (1435).
