In 1410 [Historical record]¶

A Masovian monastery town on the Vistula. The allied army of Jogaila and Vytautas crossed the river here on a pontoon bridge prefabricated near Kozienice in winter and spring 1410, floated down and assembled in about half a day on 30 June; the crossing ran from 30 June to 2 or 3 July (en.wikipedia, Czerwińsk Floating Bridge). The exact spot above the monastery is uncertain. Janusz I of Masovia fought at Grunwald with his Masovians.

In the saga¶

The saga’s Lithuanian spelling is Červinskas. The Brest council of December 1409 (Book III ch. 13) names the crossing place; the crossing itself is ch. 15, Tiltas per Vyslą, told from the wolves’ bank.

Scenes set here¶

Book III ch. 15; see the bridge.
