# The Krivis: Timeline [Saga invention unless marked]

1.  **The Line**: Inherits the fire and the staff; the line may claim Lizdeika (open question).
2.  **The Pact (1345)**: At Šventaragis, the night Algirdas and Kęstutis depose Jaunutis [Record: the deposition], he binds them in the **Tylioji Sutartis**: no conquest, in exchange for the flames kept, no Gediminid blood shed by a Gediminid, and the Amber Debt.
3.  **The Teller**: Tells young Gediminids the Krivis's version of the Iron Wolf legend.
4.  **The Prophecy Night (1382)**: Sends Eglė to answer Jogaila's captain (prologue). After the insult she leaves, and he lets her go.
5.  **The Breaking (15 Aug 1382)**: Feels clause 2 break when Kęstutis dies at Krėva.
6.  **The Extinguishing (Feb–Mar 1387)**: Stands by the flame as Jogaila's clergy put it out [Record: the fire extinguished, per Długosz]. As the water hits the embers he speaks the Pact's ending aloud, "*Sutartis baigta*", so the spirits of place hear it from a human mouth. The Unbinding begins. He walks into the felled grove and is not seen again.
