# Eglė (The Queen of Serpents)

**True Name**: Eglė; after the Dykra, **Pelkių Eglė** (Eglė of the Swamps)
**Status**: Born mortal; the last *vaidilutė* of the eternal flame in the **Šventaragis valley** (Vilnius), then the Root-Mother's voice (canon D5) [Saga invention, on the folk tale *Eglė žalčių karalienė*]
**Role**: The Keeper of the Bridge (between humans and gods)
**Era**: Prologue and Book I (ch. 15–16, 23); Books II–III; fades in Book IV as the Hidden Groves fall
**Pronunciation**: eg-LĖ · /ɛɡˈlʲeː/ · English sound-alike "eg-LAY"
**Power**: **Tier 3** before the prologue (*vaidilutė*, born to Fire). After she walks into the Dykra she is **taken** by the Root-Mother: **granted** power, outside the ladder, the swamp speaking through her. She is **not** an amber priestess.

## The Mythic Role
She is the priestess of the prologue's prophecy night (her last night as a mortal). After Jogaila's captain insults her she leaves the city on foot and, days later, walks into the Dykra.
*   **The Gap Filler**: She appears at major turning points (Vytautas's flight, the ashes of ch. 23, Grunwald) as a girl with dark, moss-tangled hair, green eyes and a yellow-cheeked žaltys coiled around her neck.
*   **The Messenger**: She gives the characters "The Choice": if they take the path of blood, the gods will take their piece.

## Interaction
*   **Vytautas**: Meets her in the swamp during his escape (ch. 15). He calls her "Gintaro Šventikė" and is wrong; her silence corrects him: "Ne gintaro. Šaknų." She saves him and takes her price (ch. 16): she accepts his buckle on the offering stone and refuses the silver wolf, which is not his to give.
*   **Jadvyga**: Appears to her in a dream, warning her that "The White Silence will freeze your heart before it saves your soul."
*   **The Krivis**: Her superior at Šventaragis, who sent her to give the prophecy he would not face.
