# Henė / Henne (The Shadow-Seller)

**True Name**: None. ***Šešėlių Pardavėjas***, the Shadow-Seller: a **shard of Velnias**, lord of the dead, of wealth and of bargains (canon D2) [Saga invention]
**Spelling (D4)**: **Henė** in Lithuanian text, **Henne** in English docs. Not "Jene", "Jenė" or "Jester Henne" in prose.
**Masks**: **Hanulas** the Riga merchant (1382, first); **Henė the jester** (best known); others as needed
**Era**: Every book, I–V. The only antagonist present throughout
**Archetype**: The Trickster-Broker / The Hollow Behind the Mask
**Pronunciation**: HE-nė · /ˈɣʲæːnʲeː/ · English sound-alike "HEH-nay" *(verify)*
**Power**: **A Force** (Layer II), outside the tier ladder. Source of **bought** power (`world_rules/power_systems.md` §1, §3). He grants anything, worded precisely; the price is the **thread**, which compounds. He is **not bound by the Law of Truth**: his floating stones and unmasking are Force-acts, not mortal craft.

## Appearance
*   A jester in black and red; at times pure black that "drinks the light"; at the Order's chapel, rich German furs; at the end a bright, joyful jester's mask. The changes are deliberate: a mask-wearer dresses for the buyer.
*   Juggles **three blackened stones**, one per cousin (Jogaila, Skirgaila, Vytautas). Not "balls" (D2). They are never the Beggar's grey stones.
*   Beneath the mask (ch. 30): **"not a man, but the hollow where the dead keep the past"**: the realm of the dead seen from inside, all of the past at once.

## Personality
*   **Hungry and fair**: Velnias never cheats; he *drafts*. Every deal is kept to the letter.
*   **Free at first**: The first favour costs nothing. The thread comes later.
*   **Serves the deal, never the man**: He changes masters without regret.
*   **Vain about time**: He sees all of the past and some of the future, and shows off (the deliberate maize anachronism in his 1384 tale; author may cut).
*   **Favourite proverb**: *Velnias ne toks baisus, kaip jį piešia* ("The devil is not as terrible as he is painted"). He says it about himself.

## Signs of a bargain in progress
Sounds die (the **Breath of the Void**), coins turn warm, a figure stands at the edge of the firelight, three stones are in the air.

## How he is beaten
Only by **refusal** or **cleverness** (the folk crop-division tale), never by force or faith. A Praurimė seer can see through his workings at the cost of blindness. **The series climax is Vytautas's refusal at Lutsk (Book IV).**

## The score (stones)
Three (Book I) → two (Skirgaila collected, 1397) → one (Vytautas refuses, Book IV) → none (Book V: he unmasks, because there is no one left to deal with).
