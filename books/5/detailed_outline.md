## Premise

On the night Vytautas dies, the Last Flame goes out in the Trakai vault, and the young wolf who has guarded it since he was a child is left with a cold pot of ash, a cracked amber pebble and a silver wolf no one will honour. **The Flame-Keeper** (an invented *vilkatas*, `character_arcs.md` §4; working skin-name **Žiežirba**, "Spark") wants one thing: to light it again. The heirs of the two cousins go to war over the realm (Švitrigaila against Žygimantas Kęstutaitis), Žygimantas wants the keeper and his wolves as a tyrant's hounds, the Shadow-Seller has nothing left to sell, and the unfed dead of a century rise on the field of Pabaiskas. The keeper learns that a fire can be lit by anyone, but **the Pact cannot be re-sworn by one man**, and he lets the gods become stories. **Frame:** Birutė, the Amber Queen, tells his story as the last thing she remembers before the sea takes her statue, closing the loop opened by `00_pradzia.qmd`.

## Magic and power state at the start (27 Oct 1430; graph progressions)

- **Silent Pact:** **folklore**. The Hidden Groves fell 1413–17; the Last Flame goes out in ch. 1.
- **Unbinding level:** **5** (holds to 1450). Quenched hearths, the Unburned, the Voiceless everywhere; priests blind to all of it.
- **Practitioners:** tier 4: **0** · tier 3: **9** · tier 2: **90** (by 1450: 0 / 5 / 50).
- **The pebble:** **cracked** (at Vytautas's death); cold in the keeper's hand; still carries Birutė's last voice through the kneeling statue.
- **Henė's stones:** **one** (Jogaila's) → **none** at Jogaila's death (1 Jun 1434, ch. 12).
- **Live ash:** none left after the flame dies; there will be no more krikštas.
- **Iron Wolves:** about a dozen by 1435 (`iron_wolves.md` §1), led by **Pilkoji**, *Vilkų Motina*; none by 1450.
- **Artifacts:** the silver wolf and the pebble willed to the keeper (handed over in ch. 7); the golden chain (with Oleśnicki's envoy in 1429–30; returns to Henė, then Žygimantas, ch. 19, Saga choice); the kneeling statue at Palanga (dims in 1445, taken by the sea in 1450); the Iron Wolf's skull (into the bog, ch. 28).

## Hands on

The series ends here. For readers going on to **Book VI** (*Šventasis Miškas*, the prequel): the last žynys has sung the Silent Pact wrong (ch. 27), and the keeper has seen the true oath in the pebble (ch. 5). Book VI shows the oath itself.
