# Uliana of Tver: Timeline

1.  **The Match (1349)**: Marries Algirdas (1349, en.wikipedia; the persona once said 1350), bringing Rus' influence to the capital.
2.  **The Succession (1377)**: Secures Jogaila's ascent over his older half-brothers.
3.  **The Negotiator (1384)**: Brokers the Moscow offer: Orthodox baptism for Jogaila and a daughter of Dmitry Donskoy as his bride.
4.  **The Twilight (1385–86)**: Sees her Eastern plans fail as Jogaila turns toward Poland at Krėva.
5.  **The End (17 Mar 1391)**: Dies (en.wikipedia, Uliana of Tver; sources conflict on whether she took the veil, at Vitebsk or at the Kyiv Pechersk Lavra).
