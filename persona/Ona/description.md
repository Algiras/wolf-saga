# Ona (The Duchess)

**True Name**: Ona (Anna); parentage unknown (a Smolensk princess or a Lithuanian boyar's daughter are both proposed; keep it a mystery in prose)
**Title**: Duchess of Grodno in 1382, as Vytautas's wife (Trakai was Kęstutis's, then Skirgaila's 1384–92, and Vytautas's only from 1392); Grand Duchess of Lithuania 1392–1418
**Age**: About 30 in 1382 (married c. 1370; her daughter Sofija was born 1371). Dies 1418 at Trakai
**Archetype**: The Clever Survivor / The Partner
**Pronunciation**: o-NA · /oˈnɐ/ · English sound-alike "oh-NAH" *(verify)*
**Power**: **Tier 0**, with herb-lore (Žolės) in practice: the sleep draught of the escape is hers. No gift; her weapons are planning and nerve.
**Kinship terms (D9)**: Kęstutis is her *uošvis*, Birutė her *anyta*.

## Appearance
*   Fair hair and sharp, observant blue eyes.
*   Deceptive fragility; she looks like a court lady but has the stamina of a soldier.
*   Modest dress, but always carries a hidden dagger.

## Personality
*   **Cunning**: She navigates court politics better than her husband. She sees traps before they are sprung. Her "silly grieving woman" is a mask.
*   **Devoted**: Her loyalty to Vytautas is absolute, but she is not afraid to challenge him when he is wrong. "This is not a love scene under the birches. This is a war council."
*   **Brave**: Walking into Krėva to free her husband required nerves of steel.

## Motivation
*   **Family**: To protect her husband and children, who are twice held as hostages (1383–84 and around 1390).
*   **Survival**: She knows that if Vytautas falls, she is dead.

## Arc
His conscience, then his judge: she stops looking at him after the sons die (1392). See `books/character_arcs.md` §3.
