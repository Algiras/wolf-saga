# Henė: Timeline

## Book VI (prequel, 1345–77)
1.  **The Watcher**: Haunts Algirdas's court while the Silent Pact holds.

## Book I (1377–87)
2.  **The First Thread (31 May 1380)**: Dovydiškės, the first crack in the Pact, is his first thread on Jogaila.
3.  **Hanulas (spring–Jun 1382)**: As the Riga merchant he opens Vilnius to Jogaila; Order silver and the golden chain pass through his hands.
4.  **The Jester (1382)**: At Jogaila's side (ch. 5–6); brokers the trap. One stone wobbles (ch. 6).
5.  **Krėva (15 Aug 1382)**: The Breath of the Void: Kęstutis dies in silence. Kin-blood breaks the Pact, and he collects.
6.  **Everywhere**: At the Order's chapel (ch. 19), at the ruined-church truce (ch. 26), a chill at Trakai (ch. 28). In 1384 he tells his crop-division tale at the division of the realm.
7.  **The Cell (spring 1387)**: Comes to Skirgaila in Kęstutis's death cell, juggles three stones, lets Skirgaila's wobble, unmasks the void, foretells the Vorskla (ch. 30).

## Book II (1387–99)
8.  Serves Skirgaila; joins Vytautas after Ostrów (1392). **Collects Skirgaila (Jan 1397)**: two stones. Shows Vytautas the Vorskla vision.

## Book III (1399–1411)
9.  Advises both sides.

## Book IV (1411–30)
10. **Lutsk (1429–30)**: Jester at Sigismund's court. Offers Vytautas the crown for the flame. Refused. Turns the crown to leaves by the roadside. One stone.

## Book V (1430–50)
11. Whispers to Žygimantas. With Jogaila dead (1434) the last stone falls *(inferred; canon says only "none")*; he **unmasks for the last time**.
