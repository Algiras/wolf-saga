# Švitrigaila (The Storm-Bringer)

**True Name**: Švitrigaila; baptised Catholic Bolesław (1386)
**Title**: Duke of Vitebsk; later Podolia, Chernihiv and Lutsk; Grand Duke of Lithuania 1430–32; rival Grand Duke thereafter
**Age**: About 12 in 1382 (born c. 1370; c. 1355 is also given). Dies 10 Feb 1452, ruling Volhynia
**Archetype**: The Rebellious Youth / The Chaotic Pretender
**Pronunciation**: švi-tri-GAI-la · /ʃvʲɪtʲrʲɪˈɡɐɪɫɐ/ · English sound-alike "shvih-trih-GUY-lah"
**Power**: **Tier 0**. No gift and, so far, no bargain. In Book V he is the East's champion; the Unbinding's dead follow his wars (Pabaiskas, 1435).

## Appearance
*   Lanky and restless. He looks like a younger, more frantic version of Jogaila.
*   Quick to anger, often flushed with blood when challenged.
*   Often seen with a mismatch of gear: part Lithuanian fur, part Polish silk, part Rus' mail.

## Personality
*   **Ambitious and Unstable**: He wants everything his brothers have, but lacks the patience or tact to get it.
*   **Unpredictable**: Will ally with his worst enemies (the Teutonic Order) just for a chance at a throne.
*   **Resentful**: Feels sidelined by Jogaila and Skirgaila.

## Motivation
*   **Grandeur**: He believes himself destined for the grand-ducal throne.
*   **Vengeance**: Against the cousins and brothers who he feels have denied him his "fair share".
