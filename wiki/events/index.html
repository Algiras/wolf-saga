Events¶

Event

Description

Date

Silent Pact sworn

All Souls’ Night 1345: Algirdas, Kęstutis, and the Krivis swear the Silent Pact at Šventaragis.

1345

Algirdas & Kęstutis depose Jaunutis

Algirdas and Kęstutis depose Jaunutis in winter 1344/45 and establish the co-ruling diarchy. The saga’s invented November 1345 Pact is a later event.

1345

Kęstutis marries Birutė

Kęstutis abducts and marries Birutė, sacred vaidilutė of Palanga (c. 1350).

1350

Kęstutis escapes Marienburg

Alf (Adolfas) removed bricks over months; Kęstutis broke through the wall, dropped into the dry moat, rode out as a knight; on foot over the Drwęca to Mazovia. Capture: Lake Ublik, March 1361.

1361-11-11

Kęstutis captured at Lake Ublik

Capture 21 Mar 1361 (Wigand; Wartberge: 13 Mar). A small returning band of knights fell on his camp; he was over sixty. Held about half a year.

1361-03-21

Algirdas dies; cremated

Algirdas dies in May 1377; cremated with 18 battle chargers in the sacred grove.

1377

Treaty of Dovydiškės

The Order’s informant to Kęstutis in Aug 1381: Kuno von Liebenstein (not Hohenstein).

1380-05-31

Kęstutis takes Vilnius; Jogaila caught on his way back

Kęstutis discovers Dovydiškės, seizes Vilnius in autumn 1381, and deposes Jogaila.

1381

Hanulas and the townsfolk take Vilnius for Jogaila

Hanulas and German merchants open Vilnius to Jogaila on 12 June 1382.

1382-06-12

Kęstutis & Vytautas lured to Jogaila’s camp near Trakai and arrested

3 Aug 1382, after Trakai fell on 20 Jul. Book I stages it at the camp: the Iron Gates are the camp palisade gate, the Feast of Silence is held in a requisitioned Trakai manor. Kęstutis’s body is burned in Vilnius.

1382-08-03

Kęstutis strangled at Krėva

Kęstutis is strangled in Krėva dungeon on 15 August 1382; breaks Pact clause 2.

1382-08-15

Birutė binds Vytautas (Sutartis, surety, flame promise)

Birutė binds Vytautas with a Senovės Sutartis and passes him the flame promise (1382).

1382

Perkūnas claims Vytautas

Lightning strikes Krėva tower; Perkūnas marks Vytautas as his tool of vengeance.

1382

Vytautas escapes Krėva in the maid’s clothes

Vytautas escapes Krėva disguised in Ona’s maid’s clothes in late autumn 1382.

1382

Eglė leads Vytautas through the Dykra

Eglė guides the hunted Vytautas across the treacherous Dykra wilderness to Mazovia.

1382

Trakai granted to Skirgaila (1382-92)

Trakai fell on 20 Jul 1382; Skirgaila duke of Trakai 1382-92 (VLE). The 1384 settlement (ch. 28) only confirms it; Jogaila promised it to Vytautas in spring 1384.

1382-07

The Beggar turns Kęstutis’s coin to stone

The Beggar (Velnias / Henė) turns Kęstutis’s coin to stone on the road to Jogaila’s camp (1382).

1382

Vytautas shelters with Janusz I in Masovia

Vytautas and Ona shelter at the court of Janusz I in Masovia after escaping Krėva (1382).

1382

Vytautas before Zöllner at Marienburg

Vytautas appears before Grand Master Zöllner in Marienburg to request crusader assistance (1382).

1382

Vytautas baptised ‘Wigand’ (a lie)

Vytautas accepts tactical baptism as ‘Wigand’ at Tapiau on 21 October 1383.

1383-10-21

Treaty of Königsberg (Samogitia ceded)

Treaty of Königsberg: Vytautas signs away Samogitia to secure Teutonic military aid.

1384-01-30

Vytautas burns Order castles; turns on the Order

Vytautas turns on the Order, burning crusader fortresses along the Nemunas in July 1384.

1384-07

Truce in the ashes with Jogaila

Vytautas and Jogaila swear a fragile truce amidst the scorched ruins in autumn 1384.

1384

Birutė’s Amber Sacrifice

Birutė walks into the Baltic waves at Palanga, paying the Amber Debt (autumn 1384).

1384

Union of Krėva

Act of Krėva: Jogaila agrees to marry Jadwiga, Christianize Lithuania, and rule Poland (1385).

1385-08-14

Jogaila baptised, married, crowned in Kraków

Jogaila is baptised Władysław, marries Jadwiga, and is crowned King of Poland in Kraków.

1386-02-15

Baptism of Lithuania; Šventaragis flame put out

Spring 1387: formal Christian baptism of Lithuania; Šventaragis flame officially extinguished.

1387

Vytautas secretly lights the Last Flame

Night of the 1387 baptism: Vytautas secretly rekindles the Last Flame in the ruins.

1387

Vytautas flees to the Order again

Vytautas defects to the Teutonic Order a second time after Jogaila fails to return Trakai (1390).

1389

Siege of Vilnius (11 Sep - 7 Oct); Crooked Castle falls 16 Sep

Only the Crooked Castle fell; the Lower and Upper Castles held. Tautvilas killed by a crossbow bolt; Karigaila died in the fall.

1390-09-11

Treaty of Lyck; hostages demanded

Signed by Marquard von Salzbach, komtur Arnold von Bürglen and Thomas son of Survila. Hostages: Žygimantas, Ona, Sofija, Rymgailė, Ivan Olshanski and other nobles.

1390-01-19

Vytautas’s hostage sons poisoned (Grunau tradition, contested)

Tradition: the Order’s revenge after the break, before Ostrów (4 Aug 1392).

1392-07

Agreement of Ostrów

Pact of Ostrów: Vytautas reconciled with Jogaila, becoming actual ruler of Lithuania (1392).

1392-08-04

The break: Ritterswerder guests seized, Order forts burned

Summer 1392; no source gives a day (the saga’s Rasos night is invention).

1392-07

Skirgaila dies in Kyiv

Skirgaila dies mysteriously in Kyiv in January 1397, suspected of being poisoned.

1397

Treaty of Salynas

Treaty of Salynas (1398): Vytautas cedes Samogitia to the Order to secure his southern crusade.

1398-10-12

Battle of the Vorskla

Battle of the Vorskla (12 Aug 1399): catastrophic defeat of Vytautas by the Golden Horde.

1399-08-12

Jadvyga dies

Queen Jadwiga of Poland dies in childbirth on 17 July 1399; the White Silence deepens.

1399-07-17

Union of Vilnius and Radom

Pact of Vilnius-Radom (1401): confirms Vytautas as Grand Duke for life.

1401

Peace of Raciąż; Marquard insults Birutė

22-23 May 1404 (en.wikipedia 22nd, VLE 23rd). The insult to Birutė after the peace is attested for Marquard (en.wikipedia, citing Ekdahl).

1404-05-22

Battle of Grunwald / Žalgiris

Battle of Grunwald (15 July 1410): allied Polish-Lithuanian triumph over the Teutonic Order.

1410-07-15

Pontoon bridge over the Vistula at Czerwińsk

Prefabricated near Kozienice in winter-spring 1410, floated down, assembled in about half a day on 30 Jun; the king and most Lesser Poland troops crossed that day, the Lithuanians arrived the same day (en.wikipedia, Czerwińsk Floating Bridge).

1410-06-30

Union of Horodło; Samogitian baptism begins

Union of Horodło (1413): Polish heraldic adoption of Lithuanian boyars; Samogitian baptism begins.

1413-10-02

Samogitian bishopric founded at Medininkai

Established on the Council of Constance’s directions on 24 Oct 1417; first bishop Matthias of Trakai (Motiejus Trakiškis), 1417-22. The saga makes it the end of the Hidden Groves.

1417-10-24

Ona dies

Grand Duchess Ona dies in Trakai on 31 July 1418; the saga invents her final words and letter.

1418-07-31

Congress of Lutsk: the crown offered

Congress of Lutsk (Jan 1429): Sigismund, King of the Romans, proposes crowning Vytautas King of Lithuania.

1429

Crown embassy blocked at the Polish border (Aug 1430); Vytautas refuses Henė

August 1430: preliminary couriers are robbed; the later crown embassy turns back. Henė’s leaf-filled chest is the saga’s deliberate invention.

1430

Vytautas dies at Trakai; the Last Flame goes out

Vytautas dies at Trakai on 27 October 1430; the secret Last Flame goes out.

1430-10-27

Polish lords seize Podolia after Vytautas’s death

Polish magnates seize Podolia immediately following Vytautas’s death in late 1430.

1430-11

Treaty of Christmemel (Švitrigaila and the Order)

Treaty of Christmemel (1431): Švitrigaila allies with the Teutonic Order against Poland.

1431-06-19

Truce of Czartorysk

Agreement reached 26 Aug 1431; Švitrigaila issued the Czartorysk document on 1 Sep and Jogaila his on 2 Sep (Polechow and Szybkowski, Roczniki Historyczne 83, 2017). Dated 1 Sep; runs to 24 Jun 1433.

1431-09-01

Žygimantas’s coup at Ašmena

Žygimantas stages a night coup against Švitrigaila at Ašmena on 31 August 1432.

1432-08-31

Grodno: Žygimantas sworn Grand Duke before Oleśnicki (Jogaila absent)

Treaty of Grodno (1432): Žygimantas recognized as Grand Duke under Polish suzerainty.

1432-10-15

Battle of Ašmena: Žygimantas defeats Švitrigaila

Battle of Ašmena (Dec 1432): Žygimantas repulses Švitrigaila’s initial counter-attack.

1432-12-08

Jogaila dies at Gródek

King Jogaila dies at Gródek on 1 June 1434 at the age of seventy-two.

1434-06-01

Jogaila catches his chill at Medyka (nightingales)

Late April 1434; Długosz’s story of the king standing at night to hear the nightingales. The saga’s cold-and-silence frame around it is invention.

1434-04

Battle of Pabaiskas

Battle by Lake Žirnajai and the Žirnaja stream. Mykolas Žygimantaitis commanded with Polish support; Kerskorff was killed. Žygimantas Kaributaitis was wounded and died in captivity under unclear circumstances.

1435-09-01

Peace of Brest (Brześć Kujawski): the Order drops Švitrigaila

Peace of Brest (Dec 1435): Teutonic Order forced to abandon Švitrigaila after Pabaiskas.

1435-12-31

Goštautas gives the warm coin to a beggar

Feb 1437, Vilnius. The Counter: “one coin reached a person”. The beggar is not named; read as Dievas.

1437

Žygimantas assassinated at Trakai

Žygimantas is assassinated in Trakai Peninsula Castle on Palm Sunday, 20 March 1440.

1440-03-20

Casimir’s privilege for Lithuania

Casimir’s Privilege of 1447 confirms Lithuanian territorial integrity and noble rights.

1447-05-02

The keeper is named at the Palanga fire

Driftwood fire on the longest night; the Amber Queen speaks through the pebble.

1449-12-12

Žiežirba dies; cremated on the beach

Early March 1450. The sea takes the kneeling statue (Book V ch. 29).
