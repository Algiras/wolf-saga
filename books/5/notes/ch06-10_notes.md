# Notes: Book V ch. 6–10 (Part I close, "Tuštuma")

## Files and length
06 `06_paskutinis_akmuo.qmd` (~1,600 w; Jogaila, Kraków, Dec 1431) · 07 `07_pilkosios_dovana.qmd` (~1,900; Žiežirba, Dykra, Kūčios 24 Dec 1430) · 08 `08_samokslas_trakuose.qmd` (~1,650; Žygimantas, Trakai, July 1432) · 09 `09_asmenos_naktis.qmd` (~2,050; Žiežirba, Ašmena, night 31 Aug–1 Sep 1432) · 10 `10_gardino_sutartis.qmd` (~1,650; Birutė frame + Žygimantas, Grodno, 15 Oct 1432).
Note on order: ch 7 is earlier in story time (Dec 1430) than ch 6 (Dec 1431), as the outline has it.

## Established facts
**Ch 6.** Jadvyga (b. 1408, "dvidešimt treji") dies at dawn after a four-night vigil in Wawel; exact day not given. Sonka stands in the light so Jogaila can lip-read; Jogaila hears with holes and reads lips (matches ch 12). He tries "Krėvoje..." to Jadvyga and the sound dies: without Vytautas's hand on his wrist the Void's silence still holds his tongue (this is the "bandžiau kelis kartus" of ch 12). Zbignevas's words always come through whole (matches ch 12). Court whisper "nuodai", hushed by Zbignevas; Jogaila does not ask. **Henė was not at the deathbed**: she died without a bargain. Jogaila reasons the bargain lasts only while he lives (the stone falls with him, ch 12). Henė appears only in a dream, turning **one** stone. Jogaila repeats "Nieko. Palaukime" to Zbignevas (Vytautas's "tu visada leisi"). Sons: Vladislovas 7, Kazimieras 4. Podolia taken in spring 1431; the Poles now talk of Lutsk. **First sighting of Kęstutis** at the foot of the bed the night after the death (tall, plain dress, no weapon, grey beard, leaning forward like a man just off a horse): Jogaila tells himself "miego klaida" and shuts his eyes (matches ch 12 "prieš trejus metus ... nusprendė, kad tai miego klaida").
**Ch 7.** Kūčios 1430, Dykra, frozen bog. **Fourteen wolves** (incl. Pilkoji and Žiežirba), fifteen bowls: the fifteenth is set by Pilkoji for "Vilkų kunigaikščiui" (Vytautas); ch 29 echoes it. Named: Uosis (ash-bearer, "pelenininkas"; carries a clay *ąsotis* of dead ash), Spyglys, Alksnis (youngest sworn), Šiurpas, Kirmėlė (stays apart, touches the silver last "kaip karštą keptuvę"; sold the hidden nine in 1382). Since the flame died, wolves wake feeling "someone held the end of a rope and let go". Julijona gave silver wolf and pebble to Pilkoji at the water gate the morning after the death; Pilkoji kept them unopened two months. **Silver wolf**: palm-long, worn, one tooth broken, blackened in the hollows. **Pebble**: pea-sized, cloudy as old honey, a hairline crack seen from one angle; cold; held to the ear it gives breathing, "jūra". Pilkoji: "Nežiūrėk į jį, kol neprireiks" (matches ch 5). Each wolf touches his throat-scar, then the silver: "jie pažino". A **candidate boy** (18, wake held three weeks before, vilktėvis Spyglys, no skin-name yet) waits for the krikštas; there is no flame to take his name. Žiežirba opens the lantern; the lightning-fire "leans to listen" but only hears; Pilkoji forbids: a name given to a fire that cannot keep it goes "kaip vanduo į smėlį". Boy to be sent home after the twelve nights ("Namuose mane palaidojo"); ch 9 says he went home and nobody in his village says his name. The lightning-fire is already in the **old horn lantern** (not the clay jar), as notes ch 1–5 require. Žiežirba: "Aš ją uždegsiu iš naujo."
**Ch 8.** July 1432. Lords (Goštautas, Kęsgaila's son, Astikas + two cousins) come to Trakai by boat "to fish"; talk in boats. Žygimantas eats only from the common pot (Book II ch 6 rule). His son **Mykolas** rows. Henė (visible to Žygimantas only) with one stone: "Jogaila neišgyvens dvejų metų"; the wolves answer the flame; "Yra kita [ugnis]". Žygimantas leaves bread on the water-gate steps three nights (Kęstutis's custom; third morning only a wet leaf). Night meeting in the empty vault (61 steps; a Karaim with a brass lamp at the door does not stop him). He asks Žiežirba to light the flame for him (a lit flame before the wolves); Žiežirba: "nežinau, ar galiu... jei pavyks, tai nebus tau"; refuses to swear; Žygimantas: "Tais, kurie prisiekia, aš niekada nepasitikėjau." Plan: the Ašmena inn by the ford; no Gediminid blood; Žiežirba will "mark" the Polotsk road (leave it open). Henė on the stairs: "Vilkai kantrūs."
**Ch 9.** The inn is on the **south bank**; the road from the water climbs to it; Polotsk road is north of the ford. A **20-pace patch of frost** on the road where it meets the water; horses slow there. Residue (Žiežirba reads it by hand): Kęstutis rode upright with **hands bound in front** (reconciled with ch 19), told the guard "Arkliui reikia" at the ford, took bread from the innkeeper's wife and shared half with the guard who shouted at him; Vytautas's heat in a closed wagon; the Void's Breath walking beside the cart "kaip žmogus", drinking sound. Birutė's pebble hums louder there. Wolves present: Uosis, Spyglys, Šiurpas + Žiežirba; dogs fall silent; ~200–300 riders under Goštautas's, Astikas's and a Kęsgaila's banners; cry "Kęstučio sūnus! Kęstučio vardu!" cut off at the frost patch. **Švitrigaila** flees barefoot in his shirt on a bareback horse; it balks on the patch; he asks "Kęstučio sūnus tave atsiuntė?", Žiežirba: "Aš atėjau pats ... kad paliktume tau kelią." Švitrigaila: "Tokia šalta ugnis. Gal tiek mums visiems ir liko." The patch's silence swallows hoofbeats; pursuers lose him (gives Žygimantas's later "kažkas jam padeda", ch 19). Dawn: Žygimantas arrives with Mykolas and ten riders, goes to the ford; Žiežirba tells him of Kęstutis's passage; Žygimantas's question about suffering/"panaudoti" starts and is **not asked**; his hand goes to his throat; "Ačiū." He enters the inn afterwards (as ch 19 says). **First look into the pebble**: Žiežirba looked at Rasos (June 1432), saw the oath; seeds the lie "du davė savo žodį, vadinasi gali ir vienas" (ch 20).
**Ch 10.** 15 Oct 1432, Grodno Old Castle courtyard, open air. Zbignevas reads the privilege in Latin: grand duke under the Crown's protection, Vilnius–Radom union renewed, lords' rights confirmed, one sentence on succession readable two ways. Žygimantas (67), in a plain dark cloak, kneels and swears; Jogaila (deaf, old) stares at the empty place over Žygimantas's shoulder (Kęstutis) and then at Henė (in the crowd, one stone), looks away first; says "Tu turi tėvo akis"; begins a word "Kr..." and cannot finish. Žygimantas was 17 at Kęstutis's death and 19 when taken to Prussia. **Birutė is Žygimantas's mother** ("mano jauniausias"), per houses doc "saga treats all as Birutė's"; she first saw him as a man only now, through the pebble. Night: Žygimantas offers the vault, ring, guards and time; **Žiežirba: "Ne"** ("Jei uždegsiu ją tau, ji degs už tave ... kalbės juo"). Žygimantas: "Aš galiu juos maitinti. Duona už duoną"; "Aš nežinau, ką pasiūlytum, ko vėliau nepaprašytum atgal." Birutė: "Tai buvo pirmas kartas, kai jis pasakė ne kunigaikščiui. Ne paskutinis." The sea at Palanga "breathes both ways", then "Kitą rudenį miškas pradėjo atsiversti ant kito šono."

## End state entering ch 11 (autumn 1433)
- Žiežirba (42/43) on foot, in byres, with the horn lantern (lightning-fire, left hand numb as ch 2), silver wolf in a coat pocket, cracked pebble on a thong at the throat-scar. He has seen the oath in the pebble (June 1432). He refused to light the flame for Žygimantas (Oct 1432) but did the Ašmena road-work and remains loosely on terms (the letters of ch 11, the break only in ch 19).
- Wolves: fourteen (Pilkoji, Žiežirba, Uosis, Spyglys, Alksnis, Šiurpas, Kirmėlė + others). No new krikštas; the boy went home.
- Žygimantas: Grand Duke (Grodno), the chain not yet his, Henė whispering with one stone; Švitrigaila in Polotsk. Jogaila alive (d. 1 Jun 1434), sees Kęstutis and now speaks to him only in ch 12.

## Verify
1. Jogaila **present at Grodno, 15 Oct 1432**, and the ceremony in the courtyard: Saga staging; terms deliberately vague ("Vilniaus ir Radomo sąjunga atnaujinta", succession sentence). Verify before naming clauses.
2. Real participants of the Ašmena coup: I named Goštautas, Astikas and a Kęsgaila son (outline's council lords). Skirmantas Gaidys (ch 18) is not named in ch 9 but the "gate held open" fits. Rider numbers (200–300) invented.
3. Ašmena geography (inn on the south bank, Polotsk road north of the ford) is Saga; the gazetteer only says "same inn, same ford".
4. Ch 19 has Žiežirba say "Čia ėjo jūsų tėvas" at the ford as if new; ch 9 already told Žygimantas this in 1432 (and added the bread/horse details). Suggest ch 19 treat it as a recall, or leave.
5. Ch 19 says Kęstutis was tied "rankas surištas priešais" and nobody asked if he was cold: ch 9 is written to agree (bound hands, upright).
6. Žiežirba's first look: ch 5 says "pusantrų metų" after Kūčios 1430 (June 1432), ch 27 says "prieš septyniolika metų" (1431): ch 9 uses Rasos 1432; a one-year slip in ch 27 is not mine.
7. Jadvyga's death date (8 Dec 1431 per houses_and_lineages) deliberately not stated; Jogaila's age left as in ch 12.
8. Wolf count: outline says "a dozen" in ch 7; I used **fourteen** to match ch 11 (14) and ch 13 (12 + Pilkoji + Žiežirba).
9. Mykolas Žygimantaitis as Žygimantas's rower/companion in 1432 is invented (not attested).
10. Henė's stone: still one (Jogaila's) throughout; none of these chapters shows his stone falling.
