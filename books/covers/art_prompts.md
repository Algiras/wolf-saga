# Cover art prompts: Geležinio Vilko Saga

Human-readable version. The machine-readable source of truth is `books/covers/covers.yml` (`art_prompt`, `art_wide_prompt`, `art_negative`). If you edit a prompt, edit it there and mirror it here.

## Series style (same in all six books)

**Style block** (prefixed to every prompt):

> Painterly dark-realist historical fantasy illustration, oil-on-canvas look with visible brushwork and restrained, desaturated natural pigments, cinematic low-key lighting, grounded and gritty rather than glossy, high detail, atmospheric depth. Setting: the Grand Duchy of Lithuania and its Baltic borderlands, 14th-15th century, period-accurate clothing, weapons and architecture.

**Recurring motif** (every book): the Iron Wolf.

> Recurring series motif: the Iron Wolf, a colossal wolf's head and shoulders in strict profile facing right, vast as the sky, translucent and made of [book-specific material, see below], one single point of light for an eye, its outline dissolving at the edges into the atmosphere. It dominates the upper third of the frame and must read at thumbnail size.

The wolf always faces right, always sits in the upper third, always has a single point of light for an eye; only its material changes (storm cloud, dust and gold haze, forest mist, candle smoke, ash, embers). That is what makes six different paintings read as one series, and it is why the title block can sit in the same place on every cover.

**Negative prompt** (every book): `text, letters, words, numbers, runes, inscriptions, captions, subtitles, watermark, signature, logo, banner lettering, readable heraldry or coats of arms, modern objects, power lines, plastic, anachronistic plate armour, cartoon, anime, 3D render look, glossy CGI, extra fingers, deformed hands, duplicated figures, cluttered composition`

Short form for tools that take a one-line negative: `no text, no letters, no watermark`.

## Required formats

- **Portrait art:** aspect 2:3, at least **2048 x 3072 px**. The compositor crops it to the e-book cover (1600 x 2560, 5:8, so a few percent is trimmed from the sides) and to a centred square for the audiobook (3000 x 3000), so keep the subject in the middle band.
- **Wide art:** aspect 16:9, at least **3840 x 2160 px**, saved as `bookN_wide.png`. Used for the video background and the thumbnails. If it is missing, the compositor crops the portrait art instead (less sharp, and the title area is not guaranteed quiet).
- **Safe areas** (applies to both): top 14 percent and bottom 30 percent of the portrait calm; lower-left third of the wide image calm. Nothing important within the outer 6 percent.
- If your model cannot output these sizes natively, generate at the largest native size and upscale; the compositor adds grain that hides mild softness.

**Historical and canon cautions:**

- Trakai: the red-brick Island Castle was NOT finished in 1382. Kęstutis began it in the second half of the 14th century, the wars of 1377-84 interrupted it, and Vytautas completed it c. 1407-09 (gazetteer, no. 7; the saga's 1382 island is 'foundations and an abandoned building site, half-flooded'). The existing cover (books/1/cover.png) shows the finished towers, which is the iconic picture and acceptable as a deliberate icon, but the canon-accurate prompt above asks for the unfinished building site. The castle Kęstutis actually lived in is the Peninsula Castle on the neck between lakes Galvė and Luka. Decide once, and use the same choice on the 16:9 variant.
- Vytautas was about 32 in 1382 (born c. 1350). Mail, gambeson, fur; no plate harness. The amber is Birutė's pebble: raw, rounded, not a cut gem.
- books/1/cover_art.png (hooded figure, burning forest, rain) is textless and is used as interim art, but it is a different scene (the 1384 burnings of the Neman castles, not winter Trakai). Replace it with art generated from this prompt when available.
