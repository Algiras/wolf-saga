# Birutė (The Priestess-Queen)

**True Name**: Birutė
**Divine Origin**: Born **mortal**, the last of the **Palanga amber-priestess line**; the last **vessel** of the Amber Queen (canon D1)
**Archetype**: The Forbidden Love / The Hidden Goddess

## The Office she inherits
*   **The Amber Queen is an office, not a person**: the sea's grief and memory, first held by the goddess Jūratė after Perkūnas shattered her amber palace and killed Kastytis (`world_rules/cosmology.md` §3.4). It needs a living vessel to act on land.
*   **The Line**: Vessels are drawn from the Palanga amber priestesses, and each ends by giving her mortality back to the sea. The old priestess of the line placed Birutė in the Gediminid bed on purpose: the line wanted a duke's son with the sea in his blood.
*   **Memory-Keeper**: As the Queen she holds the "Deep Memory" of everything gods and men have destroyed on the shore.

## The Becoming (Book I)
1.  **Hearing (ch. 3)**: The Queen's voice speaks to her from amber as she binds Vytautas in the Sutartis.
2.  **Changing (ch. 11, 1382)**: Widowed, her eyes go sea-dark. Jogaila's men throw her into the sea with stones in her skirts; the sea throws her back, and her ring, three times. A debt not yet paid in full cannot be collected early.
3.  **Transformation (ch. 27, 1384)**: At the full moon after the reconciliation she walks into the water at Palanga, pays her son's surety in full and dissolves into light, leaving the **amber kneeling woman** on the shore.

## After 1384
*   She is the **last** vessel. The Queen can now act only through amber Birutė touched: Vytautas's pebble and the kneeling statue.
*   Her post-transformation voice narrates the series (`00_pradzia.qmd`: "I am the one they call the Amber Queen, though my true name was Birutė").
