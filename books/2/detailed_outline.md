## Premise

The Pact is dead and the land's spirits are going feral. Vytautas, twice baptised and holding only Grodno, must bow in his father's hall at Trakai to Skirgaila, the man who held the rope at Krėva, while he keeps the last sacred flame alive in secret in the flooded foundations of his father's unfinished island castle. Ambition drives him back to the Order a second time (1389–92), to besiege the city he once saved, and then to betray the Order again. His two hostage sons pay for it with their lives. Made Grand Duke at Ostrów (1392), he builds an empire from the Baltic toward the Black Sea, shelters a fallen khan, and accepts the Shadow-Seller's vision of a throne on the steppe. On the Vorskla (12 Aug 1399) the steppe eats his army. **This is the book the protagonist loses.**

## Magic and power state at the start (spring 1387; graph progressions)

- **Silent Pact:** *ended* (Šventaragis flame put out, Feb–Mar 1387). The remnant bought by Birutė in 1384, the **Hidden Groves** of Samogitia and the deep Dykra, still holds.
- **Unbinding level:** **2** (rises to **3** by 1399). First Unburned, Quenched and Voiceless appear in and around Vilnius; the Riders of the Reise may now cross onto Lithuanian soil.
- **Practitioners:** tier 4: **1** · tier 3: **25** · tier 2: **300** (by 1399: 1 / 18 / 220). The single tier-4 is **the Medvėgalis žynys** of the Hidden Groves *(Saga suggestion: canon gives no name; he dies at Grunwald in Book III, so the count reaches 0 by 1411)*.
- **The pebble:** **glowing** (I ch. 29). **No surety** since 1384: Vytautas pays the full price of every working himself.
- **Henė's stones:** **three** (Jogaila, Skirgaila, Vytautas); Skirgaila's wobbles (I ch. 30).
- **The Last Flame:** lit in secret on the night of the 1387 extinguishing, from the ember carried out of Šventaragis by **Kalvis** *(picked from the open options in `iron_wolves.md` §16.3 and `religion_and_clergy.md` §2.3)*.
- **Perkūnas:** his claim on Vytautas stands (I ch. 10); he keeps score of oaths.
- **Iron Wolves:** about **40** after the 1382 schism; by rule 9 the whole brotherhood now answers to the keeper of the only Gediminid flame, Vytautas.
- **Artifacts:** Kęstutis's sword and the golden chain with Skirgaila; the silver wolf with Vytautas; the kneeling amber woman at Palanga; the Iron Wolf's skull in the Dykra.

## Hands on to Book III

A humbled Vytautas, Form 5 broken, who has seen what empire-hunger costs; **Samogitia sold** (Salynas 1398) and burning to rise; the pebble **cold**; Henė with **two stones**; Skirgaila dead and Kęstutis's sword lost in his Kyiv estate; Jadvyga dead and Jogaila's Polish crown legally exposed; a boy rescued from the Unburned growing up beside the flame in the Trakai vault; the wolves estranged (they would not follow him into the steppe); the Order under Konrad von Jungingen eyeing the map of Samogitia.
