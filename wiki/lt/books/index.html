Knygos¶

Book

Years

Description

I · The Wolf’s Exile

1377–1387

The fall of Kęstutis, the break of the Silent Pact at Krėva, Vytautas’s flight to the Order, and the 1387 baptism of Lithuania.

II · The Golden Shadow

1387–1399

The civil war for Trakai and Vilnius, the Ostrów pact, the rise of the Golden Shadow, and the disaster on the Vorskla.

III · The Green Wood

1399–1411

The recovery from Vorskla, the secret alliances with Poland, the Samogitian uprisings, and the victory at Grunwald.

IV · The Invisible Crown

1411–1430

The zenith of Vytautas’s reign, the Congress of Lutsk, the blocked coronation project, and the deathbed of the Grand Duke.

V · The Wolf’s Legacy

1430–1450

The civil war between Švitrigaila and Žygimantas, the bloodbath of Pabaiskas, and the unravelling of the Iron Wolves.

VI · The Holy Forest (prequel)

1341–1377

Prequel: Gediminas’s reconstructed winter 1341–42 funeral, the winter 1344–45 coup against Jaunutis, the later November 1345 Silent Pact, and the thirty-year crusade defence.
