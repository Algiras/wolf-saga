# Vytautas: Timeline

## Era: The Civil War (Book I)
1.  **The Uneasy Summer (1381–82)**: Vytautas holds Grodno and stands by his father Kęstutis, sole surviving oath-taker since Algirdas's death (1377). In autumn 1381 Kęstutis seizes Vilnius and spares Jogaila.
2.  **The Betrayal (early Aug 1382)**: Comes with Kęstutis to parley at Jogaila's camp near Trakai under Skirgaila's safe-conduct. Seized and sent to Krėva. (The book stages the trap in Vilnius; acceptable dramatisation.)
3.  **The Dungeon**: Imprisoned at Krėva. His father is found strangled on 15 Aug 1382.
4.  **The Escape (autumn 1382)**: Leaves in the clothes of Ona's maid, who stays behind in his place. Birutė's fog covers him.
5.  **The Exile**: Flees through the Dykra, hunted by Skirgaila's men; Eglė's price. Reaches his sister Danutė in Masovia, then the Order in Prussia.
6.  **The Pact (1383–84)**: Baptised "Wigand" at Tapiau (21 Oct 1383) with a lying heart. Treaty of Königsberg (30 Jan 1384): cedes Samogitia and becomes the Order's vassal.
7.  **Traitor of Traitors (Jul 1384)**: Secretly reconciles with Jogaila and burns Marienwerder and Georgenburg.
8.  **The Division (1384)**: Receives Grodno, Brest and Podlasie; **Trakai goes to Skirgaila**. Birutė pays the surety at Palanga.
9.  **The Secret Flame (Feb–Mar 1387)**: Re-baptised Aleksandras (Kraków, 1386); the night the Šventaragis flame is put out, he lights one in secret.

## Later books (summary)
*   **Book II**: second defection (1389–90), the siege of Vilnius (1390), the sons poisoned (1392), Ostrów (4–5 Aug 1392, Grand Duke; Trakai at last), the Vorskla (12 Aug 1399).
*   **Book III**: reconciliation with Jogaila; Grunwald (15 Jul 1410).
*   **Book IV**: Ona dies (31 Jul 1418); Vytautas marries Julijona (13 Nov 1418); Lutsk (1429); the crown embassy blocked at the Polish border (Aug 1430); dies at Trakai, 27 Oct 1430.
