Grand Principality of Moscow¶

Overview¶

[Historical record] Rurikid princes of Moscow, rising as the Horde’s tribute-collectors. Dmitry Donskoy (1359-89) won Kulikovo (1380), but Tokhtamysh burned Moscow in Aug 1382. Vasily I (1389-1425) married Sofija, Vytautas’s daughter, in 1391; he stood off Vytautas on the Ugra in 1408 and made Vytautas guardian of his son in his 1423 testament. Vasily II (1425-62) was blinded in the civil war of 1446 and restored.

Patron Force¶

None canonical. [The saga’s suggestion] The Steppe-Hunger’s shadow, since Moscow learned rule as the Horde’s tributary.

Internal tensions¶

Dynastic rivalry between Vasily II and his uncle Yuri’s sons; Metropolitan Photius against the Kyiv metropolitan Gregory Tsamblak; Novgorod and Pskov playing Moscow off against Vilnius. The Kęstutaičiai blood survives only here: Sofija’s grandson, the blinded Vasily II (V ch. 29 coda).
