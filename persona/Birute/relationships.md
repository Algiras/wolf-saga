# Birutė: Relationships

## Kin
*   **Kęstutis (Husband)**: He "stole" her from the shore-fire c. 1350; the shore-women say she let herself be stolen. She loved him all his life. She outlives him (1382) by two years.
*   **Vytautas (Son)**: "Vyti". She binds him in the Sutartis, stands surety for him and is his moral compass. After 1384 he feels her only through the pebble.
*   **Ona (Daughter-in-law)**: Calls Birutė *anyta*. Allies in the escape.
*   **Her other children**: Žygimantas, Tautvilas, Danutė, Rimgailė (the saga treats all of Kęstutis's children as hers).

## Rivals
*   **Julijona of Tver**: Algirdas's Orthodox wife. The two represent competing futures for Lithuania.
*   **Jogaila**: Whose men try to drown her in 1382.

## Legacy
*   **The People**: They view her as a saint-like, goddess-like figure. Her hill at Palanga becomes a place of pilgrimage.
*   **Marquard von Salzbach**: The Order knight who insults her and is beheaded after Grunwald (Długosz; verify the occasion).
