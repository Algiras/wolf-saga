# Jogaila (The Shadow King)

**True Name**: Jogaila; baptised Władysław (Kraków, 15 Feb 1386)
**Title**: Grand Duke of Lithuania (1377–81, 1382–92, then overlord); King of Poland (1386–1434)
**Age**: Late 20s to about 30 in 1382 (born c. 1352–62; birth year uncertain)
**Archetype**: The Politician / The Modernizer
**Pronunciation**: jo-GAI-la · /joˈɡɐɪɫɐ/ · English sound-alike "yo-GUY-lah"
**Power**: **Tier 0** (Paprastas). No gift. **Bought** power only: his 1382 bargain with the Shadow-Seller (the "easy way" of the trap; the price is that his line inherits the crown and loses the land). His ambition also feeds the Gold-Glutton and draws the White Silence (`world_rules/magic_rules.md` §8; `cosmology.md` §3.3).

## Appearance
*   Slender and somewhat frail-looking, but with restless energy.
*   Dark, intelligent eyes that never settle.
*   Dresses in fine velvets and silks (Western style), contrasting with his cousins' furs and leather.
*   Clean-shaven, unlike the bearded pagan dukes.

## Personality
*   **Anxious and Calculating**: Every move is a chess piece, played by a young man who has lived in his father's shadow and fears losing everything.
*   **Paranoid**: He sees treachery everywhere, largely because he is treacherous himself.
*   **Visionary**: He believes the Old Ways are dying. To save Lithuania, he believes it must become Christian and Western, even if it means destroying its soul.
*   **Haunted**: He never stops seeing Krėva. He cannot look at the silver wolf (ch. 26).

## Motivation
*   **Power**: To unite Lithuania and Poland into a superpower.
*   **Legacy**: To found a new dynasty that rivals the Holy Roman Empire.
*   **Fear**: He fears the Teutonic Order and believes only conversion will stop them.
