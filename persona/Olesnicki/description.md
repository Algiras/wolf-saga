# Zbigniew Oleśnicki (The Cardinal-King)

**Title**: Royal secretary; Bishop of Kraków (1423–55); Cardinal (named 1439)
**Era**: Book IV (mortal antagonist: the crown embassy blocked, 1430) and Book V
**Archetype**: The Religious Oligarch / The Shadow Regent
**Pronunciation**: ZBIG-niew o-leś-NIC-ki · /ˈzbʲiɡɲɛf ɔlɛɕˈɲit͡skʲi/ · English sound-alike "ZBEEG-nyef oh-lesh-NEETS-kee"
**Power**: **Tier 0**. A statesman, not a sorcerer. The **Gold-Glutton** works through his ledgers and seals, and the golden chain from Krėva resurfaces on one of his envoys (`world_rules/power_systems.md` §5), but he serves neither knowingly.

## Appearance
*   Usually dressed in his clerical robes, but carries himself like a general.
*   Sharp, clean-shaven face with deep-set, intelligent eyes.
*   He is said to have "the cross in his hand and the sword in his heart."

## Personality
*   **Nationalist**: He believes Poland should be the dominant partner in the union. He deeply distrusts the Lithuanian "pagan" spirit.
*   **Inflexible**: Once he sets a course (like denying Vytautas the crown), gold and threats will not move him.
*   **Guardian**: He served Jogaila faithfully and effectively took over the government as the king aged.

## Motivation
*   **Polish Supremacy**: Bringing Lithuania and Ruthenia under the Polish crown.
*   **Catholic Orthodoxy**: Opposing the Hussites and ensuring the "New Faith" is the only law.
