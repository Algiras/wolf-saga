Uosis / Ash (elder wolf)¶

Summary¶

Uosis (Ash) is a white-muzzled elder wolf of the Iron Wolves, one of the few survivors of the brotherhood who lived through Pabaiskas into the late 1440s. He remains at Pilkoji’s side in the bogs of the Dykra until his death in the deep winter of 1448–1449.

Uosis / Ash (elder wolf)

Type

character

Years

c. 1375–1449

Status

The saga’s invention

Note

White-muzzled elder wolf of the Iron Wolves; accompanies Pilkoji in 1430; dies in the winter of 1448-49 (Book V ch. 28).

Appearance & tells¶

An ancient wolf with a muzzle frosted grey-white, stiff knees, and heavy grey wolf pelts pinned with bog-iron clasps. Speaks in a low, gravelly rasp and rarely moves unless necessary.

Want · Need · Lie · Truth¶

Want (external)

To endure beside Pilkoji until the last pact is fulfilled.

Need (internal)

To find rest without abandoning the dwindling circle of the brotherhood.

Lie they believe

The wolves must survive even after the sacred fire has gone out.

Truth they learn

When the hearth is cold and the land has moved on, the wolves must return quietly to the peat.

Arc by book¶

  • Book IV (1411–1430). Present among the elder wolves during the council in the bogs and the gathering of 1430.

  • Book V (1430–1449). Survives the Pabaiskas campaign and the winter of the Unburned (1435–1436); remains one of the final four wolves living in the wild; passes away in the winter of 1448–1449, remembered by Pilkoji before her own death (ch. 28).

Power & magic¶

A vilkatas of the Iron Wolves. Over forty years sworn; possesses the gift of wolf-shape (vilkavimas) and heightened animal senses. Bound to the sacred flame and the earth of the Dykra.

Record vs saga¶

  • [Historical record]: No record.

  • [The saga’s invention]: Fictional character representing the twilight of the Iron Wolves.

Pronunciation¶

UO-sis · IPA /ˈuə.sʲɪs/ · English sound-alike “WOH-sis” (Ash tree).

Appearances¶

Book IV chapters 6, 23; Book V chapters 7, 13, 16, 28.

Relations
