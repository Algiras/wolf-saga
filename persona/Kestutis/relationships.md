# Kęstutis: Relationships

## Kin
*   **Vytautas (Son)**: His pride and joy, "Vyti". He trained him to be a warrior, perhaps too well.
*   **Algirdas (Brother, deceased 1377)**: They ruled together for thirty-two years and swore the Silent Pact together. Kęstutis misses him daily.
*   **Birutė (Wife, living)**: The Palanga amber priestess he "abducted" c. 1350 (a staged theft, per the shore-women). She **outlives him**: Jogaila's men fail to drown her in 1382, and she gives herself to the sea by choice at Palanga in 1384 (D1, D3).
*   **Žygimantas, Tautvilas, Danutė, Rimgailė (Children)**: the rest of the Kęstutaičiai.
*   **Ona (Daughter-in-law)**: She calls him *uošvis*.

## Enemies
*   **Jogaila (Nephew, *sūnėnas*)**: The "Snake in the Grass". Kęstutis spared him once and never thought his brother's son would turn on him.
*   **Skirgaila (Nephew)**: Gave the safe-conduct he broke.
*   **The Grand Master**: The eternal enemy. Kęstutis has fought the Order for fifty years (and escaped Marienburg in 1361).

## Friends across the line
*   **Günther von Hohenstein**, commander of Osterode, who warned him of the Dovydiškės treaty in 1381.
*   **Jonas and the hidden nine**: his Iron Wolf guard, "the men who don't drink Jogaila's mead".
