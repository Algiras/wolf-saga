# Book V fact-check (Vilko Palikimas, 1430-1450, epilogue 1553)

Pass date: 2026-10-12. Rule: D3, history is the spine; myth explains why, never whether; invention only where the record is silent. Format per item: claim -> record (source) -> action. Chapter numbers are Book V. Binding Book IV decisions applied: Jogaila b. c. 1362 (about 68 in 1430, about 72 in 1434); Jogaila in the Trakai corridor on 27 Oct 1430; burial about eight days after death, St Michael's altar beside Ona; lords elected Švitrigaila, Jogaila approved on 7 Nov 1430; no "Jakub of Gorlice"; envoy Baltramiejus Rogala (not named in Book V text); "Zbignevas Olesnickis".

Sources most used: en.wikipedia Lithuanian_Civil_War_(1431-1435) and _(1432-1438); Battle_of_Wiłkomierz; Battle_of_Ashyiany; Treaty_of_Christmemel; Union_of_Grodno_(1432); VLE (vle.lt) articles on Švitrigaila, Žygimantas Kęstutaitis, Gardino sutartis, Skirsnemunės sutartis, Kristinas Astikas, Jonas Goštautas, Mykolas Žygimantaitis, Kazimieras Jogailaitis, Kazimiero privilegija, Bresto taika; pl.wikipedia Świdrygiełło, Kazimierz IV Jagiellończyk, Jadwiga Jagiellonka; pl.wikisource Litwa za Witolda/1430 (Kraszewski); zamki.name/kraje/litwa/swidrygiello.php (Długosz-based chronology).

## Items from the brief

1. **Vytautas buried in Vilnius cathedral; Jogaila at the funeral** -> died 27 Oct 1430 at Trakai, buried in Vilnius cathedral; Jogaila "brought the body and buried him with royal pomp", Kraszewski dates it 7 Nov (https://pl.wikisource.org/wiki/Litwa_za_Witolda/1430; https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witold_Kiejstutowicz "w obecności Jagiełły" at death). Book IV fixes it at about eight days after death, St Michael's altar by Ona -> **confirmed** (ch 2); added "prie Šv. Mykolo altoriaus, šalia Onos" in ch 2. Ch 1: Pilkoji's "no one to see them off" -> "Net kai yra kam juos palydėti"; light in the tower window now has Julijona, the bishop, the king. Ch 2: "Lordai iškėlė jį prieš dešimt dienų" -> "vos prieš kelias dienas" (election was after the death); "tris dešimtis metų" over the hearth -> "keturias dešimtis" (1387-1430).
2. **Švitrigaila's elevation** -> unilateral election by the lords (Oct 1430), Jogaila approved 7 Nov (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_Civil_War_(1431%E2%80%931435)); Długosz: during the funeral rites Švitrigaila seized Vilnius castle, then Trakai (zamki.name). Jogaila stayed in Vilnius as a virtual hostage until before Christmas 1430 (Kraszewski via kurierwilenski.lt). -> **corrected in ch 3**: the confirming letter is no longer "iš Krokuvos" but written by the king in the Vilnius castle where he still lodged "kaip svečias"; Švitrigaila goes to the king in person after the Podolia news, the king's warm "Aš sergu" letter arrives "jau išvažiavęs namo", three weeks later.
3. **Polish seizure of Podolia / Kamianets** -> Oct-Nov 1430, immediately after the death; Kamianets refused to hand over (lt.wikipedia Švitrigaila: "1430 m. spalio-lapkričio mėn."; zamki.name; Tęgowski). Your suspicion was right. -> **corrected in ch 3** (news arrives "lapkričio pabaigoje, kai keliai jau stingo", not March) and **ch 6** ("paėmė pavasarį" -> "dar lapkritį"; "per pusmetį" -> "per kelias savaites").
4. **1431 war** -> Polish invasion of Volhynia from 25 Jun; king crosses 21 Jul; Švitrigaila beaten on the Styr 31 Jul; Lutsk castle (Lubart's) besieged from about 1 Aug, assault repulsed 13 Aug; agreement 26 Aug; **truce of Czartorysk 1 Sep 1431** to 24 Jun 1433 (en.wikipedia Civil_War_(1431-1435); pl.wikipedia Świdrygiełło; zamki.name). Goštautas was captured defending Lutsk (VLE) -> **corrected in ch 3** (dateline "-1431 m. rugsėjis"; Poles "įžengė į Voluinę po Šv. Jono ... liepos pabaigoje ... apsupo"; truce "rugsėjo pradžioje").
5. **Christmemel, 19 Jun 1431** -> signed 19 Jun 1431 at Christmemel (Skirsnemunė); parties Švitrigaila, Grand Master Rusdorf, Livonian Master Zisse von Rutenberg; witnesses include Goštautas, Kristinas Astikas, Mykolas Kęsgaila, Žygimantas Kęstutaitis and many nobles; terms: mutual aid if attacked, war only by joint consent, shared spoils (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Christmemel; VLE Skirsnemunės sutartis). -> **corrected in ch 3**: Švitrigaila no longer comes "be Goštauto, be Astiko"; they and Žygimantas ride with him; Order's men named as acting for Rusdorf and Rutenberg; treaty summary changed from "bendras karas prieš lenkus" to the real terms. Who attended in person on the Order's side: record silent, left as invention. Note: en.wikipedia says Lithuania ceded Palanga here; VLE says Melno borders kept; Book V keeps Palanga Lithuanian (see canon conflicts).
6. **Jadwiga's death** -> Jadwiga Jagiellonka b. 8 Apr 1408, d. 8 Dec 1431 aged 23 (Kraków); Sophia accused of poisoning her, sources suggest frailty (https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jadwiga_Jagiellonka_(1408%E2%80%931431); en.wikipedia Jogaila). Sons Władysław b. 31 Oct 1424 (7), Casimir b. 30 Nov 1427 (4) -> **confirmed** (ch 6); the whispered "nuodai" is the real rumour.
7. **Ašmena coup** -> night of 31 Aug 1432 (1 Sep in some sources); conspirators incl. Goštautas, Petras Mangirdaitis, Semen Olshanski, backing Žygimantas Kęstutaitis; Švitrigaila escaped to Polotsk, his pregnant wife was seized (en.wikipedia Civil_War_(1432-1438); VLE Žygimantas) -> **confirmed** (ch 9; Polotsk flight correct). **Corrected in ch 8 and 9**: Mykolas Kęsgaila (Švitrigaila's man, imprisoned by Žygimantas after the coup, his brothers executed; VLE/en.wikipedia Mykolas_Kęsgaila) replaced by Petras Mangirdaitis (supported the coup; later Grand Marshal 1434-59).
8. **Grodno, 15 Oct 1432** -> VLE: 15 Oct 1432 (en.wikipedia: act of 25 Oct); a Polish delegation led by Bishop Oleśnicki proclaimed Žygimantas Grand Duke for life (30 Sep) and received his oath in Grodno; **Jogaila was not there** (he issued his own act at Lwów on 30 Oct 1432 and confirmed Grodno on 3 Jan 1433). Terms: Vilnius-Radom union of 1401 confirmed; Jogaila's supremacy; after Žygimantas's death Lithuania returns to Jogaila and his heirs; Trakai to Mykolas (VLE); Podolia to Poland, most of Volhynia kept by Lithuania until his death; no crown for Žygimantas. -> **corrected in ch 10**: Jogaila's appearance removed; empty throne with the king's seal, Zbignevas swears Žygimantas in the king's name and reads the king's letter, whose postscript in a shaking hand ("Tu turi tėvo akis", then a struck-out *Kr*) keeps the Krėva motif; the "two readings" sentence now states the real succession clause and Podolia; feast seat beside the bishop; closing "Kitą rudenį" -> "Tą patį rudenį".
9. **Battle near Ašmena** -> **8 Dec 1432**, not 1433 (VLE Ašmenos mūšis; en.wikipedia Battle_of_Ashmyany): Švitrigaila marched from Polotsk with Tatar help, took Minsk, Krėva, Ašmena; Žygimantas won; Długosz: about 10,000 dead. The outline's "second battle Dec 1433" does not exist. -> **corrected in ch 11 by re-dating the chapter to autumn-winter 1432**: dateline, lantern "dveji metai", keeper 42, envoy "prieš dvejus metus", Pilkoji "dvidešimt šešerius metus"; the field scene now "gruodžio aštuntąją ... laimėjo Žygimantas", dead "Tūkstantis, gal du, ir kai kurie ... su totoriais". Ripples: ch 15 "Nuo 1432-ųjų rudens"; ch 16 "Prieš trejus metus, Ašmenos lauke"; ch 28 "septyniolika metų" (x2). It also fits better: Švitrigaila's Polotsk envoy promising a return to Vilnius is a man who lost the throne in September.
10. **Jogaila's death, Gródek, nightingale, age** -> d. 1 Jun 1434 at Gródek near Lviv, "in the 48th year of his reign" (en.wikipedia Jogaila). Długosz: he caught a cold in a late-April frost at **Medyka** listening to nightingales at night, then fell ill and was taken to Gródek; the day before death he summoned loyal knights and entrusted his sons to them; Queen Sophia was in Kraków; Oleśnicki and the chancellor, bound for Basel, turned back at Poznań; body reached Kraków 11 Jun, funeral at Wawel 18 Jun (https://historia.org.pl/2024/10/11/jak-zmarl-wladyslaw-jagiello-smierc-i-pogrzeb-wladyslawa-jagielly/). 1 Jun 1434 was a Tuesday (Julian). Birth c. 1362 per Book IV -> c. 72. -> **corrected in ch 12**: dateline "balandžio pabaiga - birželio 1 d., Medyka ir Gródekas"; nightingale night in the Medyka forest in late April ("vėlyva šalna"), no "trečiadienis-ketvirtadienis"; Henė's time-left answer "iki birželio pirmosios"; Henė announces that Žygimantas "rengia raštą" (the Orthodox-rights privilege was issued 6 May 1434 at Trakai, so in late April it is not yet out); illness lasts weeks; Zbignevas removed from the deathbed (replaced by "vyriausias ponas"; news that he and the chancellor are returning from Basel); Sonka "Krokuvoje"; age "septyniasdešimt dveji ar daugiau, nes niekas tiksliai nežinojo"; Władysław "devynmetis" (was "dešimtmetis"). Kęstutis vision, Franciscan Anselm, last confession: invention.
11. **Pabaiskas, 1 Sep 1435** -> near Pabaiskas, about 9 km south of Ukmergė, by the Šventoji (hence "Battle of Šventoji"); Lithuanians under Mykolas Žygimantaitis with about 4,000 Poles under Jakub Kobylański defeated Švitrigaila's host; Livonian Master Franke Kerskorff killed with his marshal and several komturs; Žygimantas Kaributaitis wounded, captured, died days later; Švitrigaila fled to Polotsk with about 30 followers (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Wi%C5%82komierz) -> **confirmed**: river, place, Kerskorff, Mykolas in command, Poles present (ch 13, 14). **Corrected**: ch 13 Mykolas's banner "kurį lydėjo lenkų pulkas"; ch 13 and 15 Švitrigaila flees "su trisdešimčia raitelių" (was 200), ch 15 "Septyni jų ... nuėjo savais keliais" (was twenty). The 400 Livonian riders, brother Hermanas, rotmistras Sokalskis: invention.
12. **Peace of Brest (Brześć Kujawski), 31 Dec 1435** -> Order stops backing Švitrigaila, supports only dukes chosen jointly by Poland and Lithuania (en.wikipedia; VLE Bresto taika) -> **confirmed** (ch 15).
13. **Švitrigaila after 1435** -> fled to Polotsk, lost Polotsk, Vitebsk, Smolensk by 1437, went south to retake Lutsk and Kremenets (pl.wikipedia); 15 Aug 1437 in Kraków, agreement at Lviv 4 Sep 1437 (rejected by the Sieradz diet 15 Oct); end of 1438 driven from Kyiv and Lutsk to Moldavia; returned to Lutsk after Žygimantas's murder (1440); Casimir granted him Volhynia and Podolia in 1442; died Lutsk 10 Feb 1452 (VLE; zamki.name). "15 years left" in winter 1436-37 -> 1452 is 15 years, **numerically confirmed**, but he did not hold Lutsk continuously -> **corrected in ch 15**: "jis juos turės" -> "jis juos prarasinės ir susigrąžinos, ir mirs juose". Švitrigaila at Lutsk in 1429: no source (Book IV decision), already softened. Mother **Uliana (Julijona) of Tver**: d. 1391/92; founded the Holy Spirit monastery in Vitebsk and took the veil there (en.wikipedia Uliana_of_Tver); burial place unrecorded -> **corrected in ch 15** ("motinos įkurtas Šventosios Dvasios vienuolynas"); grave at Vitebsk left as invention. Name kept "Uliana" because the standard "Julijona" would collide with Vytautas's widow Julijona in the same book.
14. **Žygimantas's murder, 20 Mar 1440** -> killed at Trakai on 20 Mar 1440, which was **Palm Sunday** (Julian Easter 1440 = 27 Mar); by boyar conspirators led by Aleksandras and Jonas (Ivanas) Čartoriskis, supporters of Švitrigaila, who then returned Lutsk to him (VLE; en.wikipedia "possibly led by Alexander Czartoryski"; zamki.name) -> **confirmed** (ch 21, 22). Goštautas's complicity is flagged uncertain by VLE; ch 21's Goštautas harness-mark remains an insinuation, not a claim. The mean full moon fell about 18 Mar 1440, so "dviem dienom praėjęs pilnatį" in ch 22 holds.
15. **Casimir's elevation, 29 Jun 1440** -> proclaimed by the lords in Vilnius cathedral on 29 Jun 1440, against the wishes of the Polish lords; Jonas Goštautas coordinated and became de facto head of the council (en.wikipedia Goštautas, Casimir; VLE). Offices: Goštautas voivode of Trakai (1440/1433-43), then Vilnius voivode and chancellor 1443-58 (sources differ on start years); ch 23 names no office. Age: b. 30 Nov 1427 -> **12 years 7 months** (en.wikipedia says 13); ch 24 "13 by Jan 1441" correct. Casimir came to Vilnius in **May 1440** as his brother's viceroy with the Kraków castellan Jan of Czyżów and Paweł Chełmski (pl.wikipedia). -> **corrected in ch 23**: no longer a ten-day ride from Kraków ending on the day; "Pavasarį jis atvyko į Vilnių", the last ten days being the lords' progress; Sonka's gate words "pavasarį" (was "prieš dešimt naktų"). Kraków's resistance kept.
16. **Mykolas Žygimantaitis** -> after 1440 contested the throne against Casimir (Trakai voivodeship and Samogitia backed him 1440-41, Masovia until 1447); 1448-49 with the Crimean khan took Starodub, Novhorod-Siverskyi and Kyiv; captured, taken to Moscow, died there shortly before 10 Feb 1452, poisoning suspected, buried in Vilnius cathedral (VLE; en.wikipedia). -> **corrected in ch 23**: rumours now "Mozūrija / Maskvos kunigaikštis / slepiasi Žemaitijoje" (was Moldavia / swamp corner).
17. **Prussian Confederation, 14 Mar 1440** -> founding act at Marienwerder (Kwidzyn) 14 Mar 1440 (convention decision at Elbing 21 Feb) -> **confirmed** (ch 23, "prieš tris mėnesius" is about 3.5).
18. **Hanseatic office at Kaunas** -> Kaunas municipality: Hansa agreement 1441, office opened, active to 1532; VLE dates the office "mid-15th century" (no firmer date) -> **confirmed as "1441, about"** (ch 23 "kitais metais, gal už dvejų" fits; ch 17's Hansa merchant in 1437 is invention before the office).
19. **Kristinas Astikas alive in 1447?** -> No: died about 1442-43 (VLE) or 1442/44 (en.wikipedia). Alive in 1430-40 -> ch 3, 17, 23 fine; ch 26 (1447) does not mention him -> **confirmed, no edit**. Office: Vilnius castellan (not voivode); ch 17/23 name no office. Radvila Astikaitis a young son: fine.
20. **Mykolas Kęsgaila** -> Elder of Samogitia 1412-32, 1440-41, 1443-50; backed Švitrigaila 1430, imprisoned after 1432, restored 1440 (en.wikipedia) -> ch 3 (1430), 23 (1440), 26 (1447) fine; **ch 17 (Feb 1437) corrected**: his seat given to Petras Mangirdaitis, "didysis maršalka" (Grand Marshal from 1434), four mentions.
21. **Habsburg approach, Albert II** -> 1438-39 Žygimantas negotiated an anti-Polish coalition with Albert II (VLE Žygimantas Kęstutaitis); Albert elected 18 Mar 1438 -> **confirmed** (ch 18).
22. **Union of Florence, 6 Jul 1439; Isidore** -> decree Laetentur caeli 6 Jul 1439; Isidore of Kyiv at the council and a co-drafter; Moscow imprisoned him later (en.wikipedia Bull_of_Union_with_the_Greeks) -> **confirmed** (ch 20, news in Nov 1439 plausible; Isidore himself not shown).
23. **Varna, 10 Nov 1444** -> 10 Nov 1444; Władysław III's body never found (en.wikipedia Battle_of_Varna) -> **confirmed** (ch 25, 26).
24. **Vasily II blinded** -> Shemyaka took Moscow 12 Feb 1446; Vasily captured while on pilgrimage at the Trinity monastery; 16 Feb 1446 is the traditional date, place disputed (Moscow vs the monastery). Julian Easter 1446 = 17 Apr, Lent began 2 Mar, so the blinding fell **before Lent** (your suspicion right). Ch 25 only says the **news reached Palanga "per gavėnią"** (via a Riga merchant), which fits; **no edit**. Sofija d. 1453, so "jau sena" in 1450 is right; Vasily restored 1447 (ch 29 correct).
25. **Casimir's privilege and coronation** -> privilege issued in Vilnius on **2 May 1447** (pl.wikipedia Kazimierz IV: "Państwa ... nie uszczuplimy"); VLE gives 5 May. Content: offices only to locals, no noble arrested without trial (already in 1434), free travel abroad, no reduction of territory, landowners judge their peasants, townspeople's rights, nobles regardless of rite. Coronation **25 Jun 1447, Wawel cathedral, by Archbishop Wincenty Kot** (pl.wikipedia). 25 Jun 1447 was a Sunday. -> **ch 26 consistent**; one error corrected: "abu pasirašyti tą pačią savaitę" -> "per tuos du mėnesius". Ch 26's quoted clauses (no arrest or confiscation without trial, offices to locals, land kept) match.
26. **Casimir d. 1492; "penkiasdešimt du"** -> d. 7 Jun 1492, Old Grodno castle, aged 64. 1440 + 52 = 1492 -> **confirmed** (ch 23).
27. **Olaus Magnus** -> Archbishop of Uppsala (titular, last Catholic archbishop, held the title in exile until 1557), lived in the St Brigitta house in Rome; *Historia de gentibus septentrionalibus* printed in Rome in 1555; Book XVIII describes werewolves in Prussia, Livonia and Lithuania gathering at Christmas night and emptying beer and mead cellars, piling the casks in the middle of the cellar (Baring-Gould, Book of Werewolves ch. 5; Smithsonian blog on Olaus) -> **corrected in ch 30**: "kadaise Upsalos arkivyskupas" -> "Upsalos arkivyskupas be savo katedros, tremtinys" (he never ceased to be archbishop in title). Writing in Dec 1553, printed Jan 1555: fine.
28. **Second Peace of Toruń, 19 Oct 1466; First Statute 1529** -> Order's remaining (eastern) lands become a Polish fief; Statute 1529 confirmed -> **confirmed** (ch 30, only as the grandfather's hearsay and, for 1529, the scribe's reading).
29. **Uliana of Tver's burial** -> see item 13.
30. **Kretinga church** (found while checking ch 29/30) -> no church at Kretinga before 1602 (first documented consecration 1619); Palanga had a church c. 1540 that soon passed to the Lutherans (VLE Palangos istorija). -> **corrected**: ch 29 "Kunigas Kretingoje" -> "Kunigas toli"; ch 30 midnight Mass "į Kretingą" -> "į Palangą". Kretinga as a village and road remains.

The in-scene voice through the pebble in ch 29 stays italic as speech.
