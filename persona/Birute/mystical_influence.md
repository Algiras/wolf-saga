# Birutė: Mystical Influence

**Patron Force**: The Amber Queen (whose vessel she becomes)
**Connection**: Born gift, then vessel

## The Meddling
-   **The Living Oracle**: Birutė does not just hear the Whispers of the sea; by 1384 she *is* them. The Queen's whisper is surf, and it comes only through amber.
-   **The Shore-Flame**: She was sworn to the Palanga fire, and that flame is one of the Silent Pact's sacred fires.
-   **The Surety**: Part of every price Vytautas pays is paid by her, wherever she is: the Krėva fog costs her voice; later costs take her sight. In 1384 she pays the whole remaining debt at once.
-   **Distance**: Through the anchored pebble (her blood on the amber) she can work at Krėva from Palanga.
-   **After 1384**: She acts only through amber she touched. The kneeling statue calms storms on that shore; whoever moves it dies at sea.

## Interaction
She accepts the Forces as naturally as the wind. She saw her "abduction" by Kęstutis as a trick on the letter of her vow, and knows the spirit of it was written down and will come due.
