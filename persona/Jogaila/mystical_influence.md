# Jogaila: Mystical Influence

**Patron Force**: The Shadow-Seller (a shard of Velnias)
**Rival Force**: The Root-Mother
**Drawn to him**: The White Silence (it gathers wherever a baptism is being bought) and the Gold-Glutton

## The Meddling
-   **The Pragmatic Voice**: The Shadow-Seller whispers to Jogaila, in his own voice, that "betrayal is merely a higher form of loyalty to the future." It provides the logic for the Cross-marriage.
-   **The Silent Grove**: When Jogaila enters a sacred grove, he feels a crushing pressure on his chest. The Root-Mother views him as a tumour in her side.
-   **The Crow-Eyes**: Crows often follow Jogaila. He believes they are spies for his enemies, but they are the Shadow-Seller's "eyes", making sure he keeps to the deal.
-   **The Lot-Caster**: His court *burtininkas* has never once cast an unfavourable lot for him. Henė likes the man.

## Interaction
Jogaila knows he has bargained. He believes he is outsmarting the Shadow-Seller, but every victory pulls the thread tighter. The bargain's letter shows in his marriages: four wives, and sons only from the last.
