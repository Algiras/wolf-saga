Danila (Vytautas’s scribe)¶

Summary¶

Danila is a loyal chancery scribe and personal chamber servant to Grand Duke Vytautas throughout his later years in Vilnius and Trakai (Book IV). He handles correspondence, manages parchment and seals, and attends to the aging duke as his sight and hearing fade.

Type

character

Years

c. 1385–1435

Status

The saga’s invention

Note

Scribe and personal servant to Vytautas in Vilnius and Trakai (Book IV ch. 1, 16, 24).

Appearance & tells¶

A quiet, ink-stained clerk in modest wool robes, moving without sound between the archive chests and the duke’s table. Attentive and discreet, he knows when to read aloud, when to hold the parchment, and when to step into the shadow of the door.

Want · Need · Lie · Truth¶

Want (external)

To preserve the letters and records of the Grand Duchy with meticulous precision.

Need (internal)

To serve an aging titan with quiet loyalty without becoming entangled in court conspiracies.

Lie they believe

Parchment and royal seals outlast the whims of kings and princes.

Truth they learn

Crowns and charters turn to dust, but the true word spoken in private endures.

Arc by book¶

  • Book IV (1411–1430). Serves as Vytautas’s trusted chancery scribe. Present during diplomatic correspondence across Ruthenia and the West; reads letters into Vytautas’s right ear after the duke loses his reading sight; attends the duke during his final illness at Trakai.

Power & magic¶

Tier 0 (mortal). A skilled secular scribe and archivist. No supernatural gifts.

Record vs saga¶

  • [Historical record]: Historical clerks and notaries of Vytautas’s chancery are well-attested in the Lithuanian Metrica and diplomatic registers.

  • [The saga’s invention]: Danila as a specific named personal chamber scribe and reader is an invention.
