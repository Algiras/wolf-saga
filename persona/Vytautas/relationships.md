# Vytautas: Relationships

## Allies (Loyalists)
*   **Ona (Wife)**: His anchor and his moral mirror. She organised his escape from Krėva. He trusts her counsel above all others, until the sons die (1392) and she stops looking at him.
*   **Kęstutis (Father)**: Vytautas worshipped him. Kęstutis's murder is the driving force of his rage.
*   **Birutė (Mother)**: Binds him in the Sutartis and stands surety for him until her sacrifice (1384). Calls him "Vyti".
*   **Danutė (Sister)** and Janusz I of Masovia: his first refuge after Krėva (late 1382).
*   **Jonas and the Iron Wolves**: the remnant of his father's hidden guard; his band in ch. 25.
*   **The Teutonic Order (Uneasy Alliance)**: Vytautas uses them as a weapon, but he despises them. He knows they want to conquer his home, not liberate it.

## Enemies
*   **Jogaila (Cousin, *pusbrolis*)**: Once a childhood friend, now the kinslayer. Book III forces them back together.
*   **Skirgaila (Cousin, *pusbrolis*)**: Jogaila's brother and enforcer, who broke the safe-conduct and took Trakai. Vytautas wants his head, and is later suspected of his death (1397).
*   **Henė, the Shadow-Seller**: the voice that offers him every shortcut; refused at Lutsk (Book IV).

## Complex
*   **Eglė**: the swamp's voice who saves him in the Dykra and takes a price (ch. 16). He wrongly calls her an amber priestess (ch. 15); she corrects him.
*   **Konrad Zöllner von Rotenstein**: his sincere host, whom he deceives (ch. 18–19).
