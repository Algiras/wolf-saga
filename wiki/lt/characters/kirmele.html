Pastaba

Elder wolf of the Iron Wolves; carried five-year-old Spark from the burning village of Šermukšniai in winter 1395-96 (Book II ch. 14); dies at Pabaiskas in 1435, whispering his carried names to Pilkoji (Book V ch. 13).

Summary¶

Kirmėlė (Worm) is an elder wolf of the Iron Wolves, a veteran scout and tracker. In the deep winter of 1395–1396, he carried the five-year-old child Spark (Žiežirba) from the burning village of Šermukšniai to safety through the snow. He falls in battle at Pabaiskas in 1435, whispering his carried names to Grey (Pilkoji) as he dies.

Appearance & tells¶

An aged, grizzled vilkatas with a white-streaked beard, scarred forearms, and silent, watchful eyes. Smells of swamp water, cold rowan leaves, and woodsmoke. Moves without a rustle even through frozen reedbeds.

Want · Need · Lie · Truth¶

Want (external)

To protect the sacred packs and carry the names of the fallen until the flame no longer needs wolves.

Need (internal)

To pass on his carried memories before his strength breaks in the field.

Lie they believe

An old tracker can keep death at arm’s length if he stays in the shadow of the pines.

Truth they learn

Every wolf owes his bones to the bog; the only victory is giving your carried names to living hands.

Arc by book¶

  • Book II (1387–1399). Stands in Vytautas’s hidden guard at the island foundations (ch. 2); in winter 1395–1396 carries the orphaned child Spark through the burning woods of Šermukšniai (ch. 14).

  • Book III (1400–1411). Mentors the younger wolves and guards the borders of Samogitia and the Dykra; tells Grey the story of the rescue.

  • Book IV (1411–1430). Present among the elder wolves during the late gatherings; accompanies Grey and Spark during the vigil at Trakai.

  • Book V (1430–1435). Marches to Pabaiskas (1 Sep 1435); wounded fatally in the battle alongside Hazel, Alder, and Oak; whispers his carried names to Grey and is buried in the peat knoll (ch. 13, 28).

Power & magic¶

A vilkatas of the Iron Wolves. Over forty years sworn; possesses the gift of wolf-shape (vilkavimas) and deep tracking instincts. Bound to the Root-Mother and the keeper of the sacred flame.

Record vs saga¶

  • [Historical record]: No record.

  • [The saga’s invention]: Entirely fictional character representing the brotherhood’s endurance and oral tradition.
