# The Hostage Sons of Vytautas

**True Names**: Uncertain. The names usually given are **Ivan** (Iwan) and **Jurgis** (Georg) *(verify)*. Number, names and year all differ between sources (`world_rules/houses_and_lineages.md` §1.5)
**Title**: Sons of Vytautas and Ona; hostages of the Teutonic Order
**Era**: Book II, Part 1 climax (1392)
**Age**: Children (born after 1371; their exact ages are unknown)
**Archetype**: The Price
**Pronunciation**: (see pronunciation guide)
**Power**: **Tier 0**. Their deaths are **herb-lore** (poison), not sorcery: "the horror of the saga is that men need no magic for the worst things" (`world_rules/power_systems.md` §2.4).

## Record [Record, weak]
*   Vytautas had sons who died young and left him with no male heir; his blood survived only through his daughter **Sofija** in Moscow.
*   The story that they were **poisoned while hostages of the Order** after his 1392 defection is carried chiefly by the 16th-century Prussian chronicler **Simon Grunau**, whom modern historians treat as unreliable; some call the poisoning a rumour. When the record is this thin, D3 lets the saga choose, and say why in-world.
*   Vytautas did secure the release of many of his kin held by the Order before he changed sides in 1392.

## Saga canon [Saga invention]
*   **Two sons, poisoned in 1392** (`master_chronology.md`; `houses_and_lineages.md` §6 rule 4). Vytautas got Ona and most hostages out; the boys were the ones he could not reach in time.
*   **Šešiapirštis**, the *Vilkų Tėvas*, goes into Prussia to save them, finds them already dead, and comes back to lay himself in the bog (`iron_wolves.md` §12.3).
*   **The cost**: ends Vytautas's fourth form, "Traitor of Traitors" (`books/character_arcs.md` §1.2). **Ona stops looking at him.** The Iron Saint's revenge.

## Relationships
*   **Vytautas and Ona**: their parents.
*   **Sofija**: their elder sister, sent to Moscow as Vasily I's bride in 1391.
*   **Konrad von Wallenrode**: Grand Master in 1392, under whom the deed falls.
