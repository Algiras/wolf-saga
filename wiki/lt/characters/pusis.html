Pušis (old Kęstutis wolf)¶

Summary¶

Pušis („Pine“) is a grey old wolf with a split nose who served Kęstutis. At the exposure of Kirmėlė in the Trakai vault he is the one who says the Word: leave him to the bog, alive. In 1399 he leads the wolves who will not cross the Dnieper into the steppe; he does not follow Kalvis to the Vorskla. He stands in the Kūčios line when the wolves name Žiežirba in 1405.
