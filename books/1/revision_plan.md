## Phase 2: structural (Sonnet)
Write in **Lithuanian**, in the book's voice, but **leaner**: in passages you touch, cut stacked intensifiers ("mirtinai", "neįtikėtinai", "negailestingai", "tai buvo / tai nebuvo") roughly by half. Don't rewrite untouched passages for style. Every magic use must show its **cost** (`magic_rules.md` §4). Keep chapter titles and filenames. Target length: grow structurally changed chapters to roughly 1,200–2,000 words where new scenes are added.

### R1 (prologue, 00, 01–04)
- Prologue: Šventaragis (not Romuva); Eglė is the last vaidilutė; at the end she leaves the city and heads toward the Dykra (D5).
- Ch. 1: summer harvest; add **Kęstutis's 1381 mercy**, a short memory of taking Vilnius and *letting Jogaila go* (H3); acknowledge the Order and Jogaila's men threatening Trakai (N37); the chain is among the gifts.
- Ch. 2: Vytautas references "last year we spared him"; set up **Daumantas** (the friend who dies in ch. 21) as Vytautas's sworn companion; an Iron Wolves hint in Kęstutis's guard (`iron_wolves.md`).
- Ch. 3: the full **Senovės Sutartis rite** (blood, true name, *"Jo skola — mano skola"*, his promise *"Kol aš gyvas, paskutinė ugnis neužges"*); Birutė goes **blind** after the off-zenith vision and reads his face by touch (M1, M2, M11).
- Ch. 4: per Q7, the coin turns to stone (cosmology §5, "seen, not saved").
