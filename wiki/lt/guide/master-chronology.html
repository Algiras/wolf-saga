Master chronology¶

The saga’s timeline from the age of legends to 1450: the historical record beside the saga’s hidden history.

Labels. [Historical record] is attested in the sources or standard scholarship; [The saga’s invention] was made for this series.

Columns: Record is what the sources say; Hidden history is the saga’s mythic truth; Book shows where the event is told.

Sofija leaves in autumn 1390 via Danzig and Pskov and marries Vasily I of Moscow on 9 Jan 1391

Part 1

11 Sep – 7 Oct 1390

Siege of Vilnius: the Order and Vytautas take and burn the Crooked Castle (fell 16 Sep; the Lower and Upper Castles held); Henry Bolingbroke (future Henry IV of England) takes part; Skirgaila defends. Tautvilas, on the Order’s side, is killed by a crossbow bolt; Karigaila, Jogaila’s brother, dies in the fall

The bombards and siege the current Book I wrongly uses

Part 1 set piece

Summer 1392 (July)

The break: at festivities at Ritterswerder the Order’s most prominent guests are seized; Ritterswerder, Metenburg and Neugarten are destroyed (no source gives the day; the Rasos night is the saga’s). In May the Order had released Ona to travel to Lithuania. Afterwards, in the Order’s revenge, his two sons, hostages of the Order, are said to be poisoned

The worst price. Ona’s grief; the Iron Saint’s revenge
