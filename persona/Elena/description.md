# Elena (The Maid Who Stayed)

**True Name**: Elena *(name not confirmed in the sources; treat as Saga invention until verified)*
**Title**: Ona's maid, of her household
**Era**: Book I (ch. 12 implied, 13, 14)
**Age**: Young ("jauna tarnaitė", 13:25)
**Archetype**: The Quiet Sacrifice
**Pronunciation**: e-le-NA · /ɛlʲɛˈnɐ/ · English sound-alike "eh-leh-NAH" *(verify)*
**Power**: **Tier 0**. A secret follower of the Root-Mother ("Šaknų Motinos pasekėja", 13:25): she has no craft, only faith.

## Record or invention
*   **[Record]** The oldest Lithuanian annals say Ona persuaded one of her maids to change clothes with Vytautas; the maid stayed in the castle and he walked out. Other accounts differ (he wore his wife's clothes; he was lowered from a window in a basket).
*   **[Not found in the record]** The maid's **name** and her **execution**. `story_review.md` and `houses_and_lineages.md` call them historical; both are marked *(verify)*, and no source has been found. Until one is, the name "Elena" and her death are **Saga invention**.

## In the saga
*   She lies in Vytautas's bed in his old furs, face to the wall, while he walks out in her dress. When he looks back, her eyes hold no fear, only a calm, sacred offering: she believes her death is the seed of a free Lithuania.
*   Her fate is never shown on the page. Canon: **executed** at Krėva when the deception is found, on Skirgaila's order.

## Relationships
*   **Ona**: Her mistress, who chose her. Ona carries the guilt.
*   **Vytautas**: Owes her his life, and never learns her name in the book (a possible Book II beat: he asks).
*   **Skirgaila**: Her killer.
