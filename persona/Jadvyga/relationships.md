# Jadvyga: Relationships

## Kin (Political)
*   **Jogaila**: Her husband. Their relationship began in cold duty and grew into a respectful partnership.
*   **Elizabeth of Bosnia**: Her mother, a tragic figure herself. **Louis I of Hungary**: her father.
*   **Elżbieta Bonifacja**: Her daughter, born 22 Jun 1399, dead within weeks; Jadvyga follows her on 17 Jul.

## Rivals (Spiritual/Political)
*   **The Teutonic Order**: She exposes their hypocrisy; she is a "more Christian" figure than the knights themselves.
*   **Vytautas**: She respects him and sympathises with Lithuania, but defends her crown's claims (the 1398 tribute demand, which helped push the Lithuanian boyars to hail Vytautas as king; verify details). **There is no record that she mediated Ostrów (1392)**: Jogaila's envoy there was Henryk of Masovia, bishop-elect of Płock, who married Vytautas's sister Rimgailė.

## Lost Love
*   **Wilhelm of Habsburg (Austria)**: The childhood betrothed she was forced to send away.
    *   *Dramatic Moment*: Tradition (Długosz) says he reached Wawel and she tried to break the gate to go to him, then chose her crown.
