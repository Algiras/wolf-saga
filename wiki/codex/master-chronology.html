Master chronology

Source: world_rules/master_chronology.md

Canonical timeline (decision D3: the record is fixed and magic fills the gaps). Supersedes history_timeline.md, which is kept as a short overview.

Columns: Record is what the sources say; dates marked (verify) should be checked against a standard reference (e.g. Kiaupa/Kiaupienė/Kuncevičius The History of Lithuania before 1795; Rowell Lithuania Ascending; Nikžentaitis on Vytautas) before they go into prose. Hidden history is the saga’s mythic truth. Book shows where the event is or should be dramatised.

Book I season and date audit (summary)

The book currently runs: autumn arrest (Dagotuvės), winter at Krėva, a spring and summer 1383 campaign, a Vilnius siege dated 1383, the truce, early autumn at Trakai, and winter 1387 baptism. The record runs: August arrest, the death on 15 Aug, autumn escape, a 1383 campaign and baptism, the July 1384 defection, 1385 Krėva, 1386 Kraków, Feb 1387 Vilnius. The cleanest fix is to move ch. 1–9 to late July and August (harvest-season Žolinė instead of Dagotuvės; the death on the Assumption is a gift for irony), keep the escape in autumn, and give Part III date headers. Detail is in books/1/continuity_bible.md.
