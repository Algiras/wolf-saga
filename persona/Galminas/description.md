# Galminas (The Samogitian Oak)

**Title**: Elder of Samogitia, "the last of the old *žyniai*", Lord of the Forest [Saga invention]
**Era**: Book I (ch. 29, watching the baptism) and Book III (the uprisings; transforms at Grunwald). Becomes a grove
**Archetype**: The Indigenous Patriot / The Unyielding Pagan
**Pronunciation**: GAL-mi-nas · /ˈɡaːlʲmʲɪnɐs/ · English sound-alike "GAHL-mih-nahs" *(verify)*
**Power**: **Tier 5, vessel (host) of the Root-Mother** (`world_rules/power_systems.md` §4). Before his death he was a tier 3 *žynys*; the Root-Mother reanimated him, and she now acts through him in full.

## Appearance
*   Gnarled and weathered, like the trees of his home.
*   Wears minimal armour (leather and bone), preferring mobility in the dense swamps.
*   His face is tattooed with ancient Baltic signs of protection.

## Personality
*   **Fierce Independence**: He doesn't care about the "Grand Duchy" or "Poland." He only cares about his land and his groves.
*   **Suspicious**: He views Vytautas's political games with the Christians as a betrayal. "You can wash a wolf, but he remains a wolf; you can baptise a man, but the truth remains in his blood."
*   **Guerrilla Master**: He knows every path, every swamp and every trap in Samogitia.

## Motivation
*   **Survival of the Way**: Protecting the sacred groves from the knights' axes.
*   **Sovereignty**: Keeping Samogitia free from both the Order and the centralising power of Vilnius.
