# Jogaila: Timeline

## Era: The Civil War (Book I)
1.  **The Succession (1377)**: Inherits the grand-ducal title from his father, Algirdas; Kęstutis remains co-ruler.
2.  **The Secret Treaty (31 May 1380)**: Signs the Treaty of Dovydiškės with the Order, secretly agreeing not to aid Kęstutis.
3.  **The Mercy (autumn 1381)**: Kęstutis seizes Vilnius, captures him, reads the treaty and releases him to Krėva and Vitebsk.
4.  **The Return (spring–Jun 1382)**: Retakes Vilnius while Kęstutis campaigns against Kaributas; the merchant Hanulas opens the gates.
5.  **The Coup (early Aug 1382)**: Kęstutis and Vytautas, coming to parley at his camp near Trakai under Skirgaila's safe-conduct, are seized and sent to Krėva. Kęstutis dies there on 15 Aug; Jogaila claims suicide and gives him a full pagan cremation.
6.  **Dubysa (31 Oct 1382)**: Promises the Order baptism within four years (never ratified).
7.  **The Reconciliation (Jul 1384)**: Makes a secret truce with Vytautas; gives Trakai to Skirgaila.
8.  **The Union (14 Aug 1385)**: Signs the Union of Krėva, in the castle where Kęstutis died.
9.  **The Coronation (1386)**: Baptised Władysław (15 Feb), marries Jadvyga (18 Feb), crowned King of Poland (4 Mar) in Kraków.
10. **The Baptism of Lithuania (Feb–Mar 1387)**: Puts out the Šventaragis flame and fells the grove. The Silent Pact ends.

## Later books (summary)
*   **Book II**: the second civil war with Vytautas; Ostrów (1392). **Book III**: Grunwald beside Vytautas. **Book IV**: outlives Vytautas, still guilty. **Book V**: dies 1 Jun 1434 at Gródek, after hearing a nightingale (anecdote).
