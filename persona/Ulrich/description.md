# Ulrich von Jungingen (The Iron Sentinel)

**Title**: 26th Grand Master of the Teutonic Order (26 Jun 1407 – killed 15 Jul 1410)
**Era**: Book III (*Žaliasis Miškas*, The Green Wood)
**Archetype**: The Zealot / The Aggressor
**Pronunciation**: UL-rich fon JUN-gin-gen · /ˈʊlʁɪç fɔn ˈjʊŋɪŋən/ · English sound-alike "OOL-rikh fon YOONG-ing-en"
**Power**: **Tier 0**, with **the Iron Saint's gift** (granted): fearlessness and tireless marching, at the price of pity. At Grunwald the Iron Saint stands on the field in person behind him.

## Appearance
*   A mountain of a man, about fifty at Grunwald.
*   His white cloak with the black cross is always immaculate, even in battle.
*   Eyes that shine with a dangerous, religious fervour.

## Personality
*   **Arrogant**: He believes the Order is invincible and "favoured by the Virgin."
*   **Aggressive**: Unlike his cautious brother and predecessor Konrad, he seeks a final, decisive battle to crush the "pagan-Polish hydra."
*   **Honourable (in his own way)**: He fought to the last at Grunwald, refusing to flee when the battle was lost.

## Motivation
*   **Purity**: To finish the mission of the Order and claim the "Wild Lands" for Christ.
*   **Dominance**: To prove the superiority of the German knights over the "Eastern rabble."
