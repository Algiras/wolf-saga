# The Flame-Keeper: Timeline [Saga invention, on Record anchors]

1.  **Born (c. 1390)**.
2.  **The Rescue (Book II, 1390s)**: Saved by the wolves when his village's dead rise in the first Unbinding hunts.
3.  **The Vault (Books II–IV)**: Sworn young; guards the secret flame in the Trakai island castle's deepest vault.
4.  **The Lantern (Book III, 1410)**: An ash-bearer carries the flame on campaign; perhaps he is the bearer on the eve of Grunwald.
5.  **The Going-Out (27 Oct 1430)** [Record: Vytautas dies at Trakai]: The flame dies with Vytautas. The Iron Wolf dissolves into mist on Trakai island (Book V ch. 1).
6.  **Pabaiskas (1 Sep 1435)** [Record: the battle]: The last dozen wolves spend a winter burning the unburned dead.
7.  **Trakai (20 Mar 1440)** [Record: Žygimantas murdered]: The wolves, too few, fail Žygimantas; the Iron Wolf's last howl over the lake.
8.  **The Last Wolf (late 1440s)**: Pilkoji gives him Kęstutis's silver wolf and lies down in the oldest bog with the Iron Wolf's skull. He lets the gods become stories.
