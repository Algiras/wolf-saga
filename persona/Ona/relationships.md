# Ona: Relationships

## Allies
*   **Vytautas (Husband)**: A true partnership. He leads the army; she manages the shadows.
*   **Elena and her other maids**: Loyal women who carried out the prison break. Elena stays in Vytautas's bed at Krėva and is executed.
*   **Birutė (*anyta*)**: The mother-in-law whose fog covers the escape. They share one secret: what the Sutartis costs.
*   **Sofija (Daughter)**: Sent to Moscow as Vasily I's bride in 1391.

## Enemies
*   **Jogaila**: She despises him for his betrayal.
*   **Skirgaila**: She fears him. He is the brute force that could crush them.

## Complex
*   **The Teutonic Order**: Her gaolers as hostage (1383–84; again c. 1390), and her husband's allies.
*   **Julijona Alšėniškė**: the woman Vytautas marries in 1418, the year Ona dies.
