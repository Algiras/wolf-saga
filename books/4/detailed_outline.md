## Premise

Grunwald won the war and lost the gods. Samogitia is baptised (1413–17) and the Hidden Groves fall one by one, while Vytautas preaches the Creed by day and keeps the Last Flame by night: the one man in Christendom for whom the Order's charge that Lithuania's baptism is false is true. Ona dies in 1418, and with her the last person who knew him before Krėva. He becomes master of an empire from the Baltic to the Black Sea and needs only a crown to make it a kingdom that will not revert to Poland when he dies. At Lutsk (1429) Sigismund offers one. The Bishop of Kraków, Zbigniew Oleśnicki, a statesman sure that Lithuania must never be a kingdom, sets out to stop it. And the Shadow-Seller makes his last offer: **the crown will arrive if the flame is allowed to die.** Vytautas refuses. The crown embassy is blocked at the Polish border (Aug 1430) and the crown turned to leaves (saga myth; the record never has it seized); Vytautas dies uncrowned at Trakai on 27 Oct 1430, and the flame goes out with him. **The antagonist wins the crown; the protagonist wins himself.** That refusal is the secret meaning of the historical mystery.

## Magic and power state at the start (1411; graph progressions)

- **Silent Pact:** ended; the **Hidden Groves** remain in Samogitia, exhausted after Grunwald. They fall with the baptism, 1413–17 (graph: *groves_fallen*, 1417).
- **Unbinding level:** **3**, rising to **4** in 1417 and **5** at Vytautas's death (1430).
- **Practitioners:** tier 4: **0** · tier 3: **14** · tier 2: **160** (by 1430: 0 / 9 / 90).
- **The pebble:** **warm** (the Grunwald oath kept). At Vytautas's death it **cracks**.
- **Henė's stones:** **two** (Jogaila, Vytautas). Henė is Vytautas's jester until 1428–29, then Sigismund's.
- **The Last Flame:** in the Trakai island vault, kept by **Žiežirba** (sworn 1405). Every new wolf must still be named into it.
- **Perkūnas:** **silent** for the whole book (series_arc E). His storm is held back for the funeral (Book V).
- **The Root-Mother:** spent at Grunwald; Eglė fading.
- **Iron Wolves:** about 30 *(Saga estimate, falling toward the dozen of 1435 in `iron_wolves.md` §1)*; Pilkoji sworn since 1404; Žiežirba sworn since 1405.
- **Artifacts:** Kęstutis's sword and the silver wolf with Vytautas; the golden chain with Henė (graph 1397–1428), then on **Oleśnicki's envoy** (1429–30); the kneeling statue at Palanga; the Lutsk crown (which never arrives).

## Hands on to Book V

**The flame is out.** Its going-out is replayed the same night from the vault in Book V ch. 1, through the eyes of its keeper. There will be no more krikštas. The pebble is **cracked** and the silver wolf waits for the keeper; Henė holds **one stone** (Jogaila's); Unbinding **5**; the Lithuanian lords raise **Švitrigaila** while **Žygimantas Kęstutaitis** waits; Podolia is seized by Polish lords; Sofija rules Moscow as regent for Vytautas's grandson; Samogitia is Lithuanian for good, and Christian.
