Žolės (herb-lore)¶

What it is¶

Herb-lore: brews, smokes and poisons. It is power anyone can carry, with no gift and no Law of Balance, which is exactly why it is the most dangerous. It is mundane chemistry in a mythic world.

Note

Žolės: traditional herbal knowledge, brews, healing poultices, and subtle poisons.

Who can use it (path & tier)¶

Tier 1: the žolininkė, the herb-woman, the midwife; also anyone who can gather and measure: Ona, the Lagoon witch, the Order’s poisoners. Eglė practises it at tier 3.

How it works (medium, words, timing)¶

Herbs gathered at Rasos (Midsummer) before dawn, in silence, are double potency and keep a year. Effects: sleep, truth-loosening, fever-breaking, wound-cleaning, and the seer’s smoke (henbane and amber dust) that lets a Kalba seer see further.

Costs¶

There is no magical cost: the price is the moral one. The seer’s smoke costs a terrible headache. A poison needs no sorcery at all (Book II ch. 16: “no spell, no god, no pact; only henbane seeds and root, a human hand’s work”).

Limits & counters¶

It is mundane, so it can be undone by mundane means: knowledge, a physician, an antidote. In the saga’s moral design it is where the worst crimes are done, because the horror is how little magic the world needs. A charm plus a Rasos-herb poultice heals what Water cannot.

Folk basis [Historical record]¶

[Historical record]. Lithuanian folk medicine was rich in wormwood (kietis), St John’s wort (jonažolė), sweet flag (ajeras), mugwort, linden, chamomile, valerian, henbane (drignė) and poppy; herbs gathered on Joninės night were held strongest, and the Assumption (Žolinė, 15 August) is the herb-blessing feast.

Notable practitioners¶

Ona’s sleeping draught for the guards at Krėva (Book I ch. 12); Ona’s herb list “kietis, chamomile, jonažolė, and drignė, keep that one apart” (Book II ch. 4, 6); the slow poison in the hostage sons’ food, drignė, kietis and sugar (Book II ch. 9); Skirgaila’s death in 1397 (ch. 16). The historical poisoning of 1392 rests on weak evidence (Simon Grunau).
