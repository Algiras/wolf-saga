# Vytautas: Mystical Influence

**Patron Force**: The Amber Queen (The Memory of the Sea), through his mother Birutė and the amber pebble
**Tormentor Force**: Perkūno Rūstybė (Perkūnas's claim, from Krėva, ch. 10)
**Contesting Force**: The Root-Mother, who wants him but cannot take a man already owned twice

## The Meddling
-   **The Vision**: Vytautas receives visions of his father's spirit wandering the amber shores. This keeps his grief fresh and his rage focused.
-   **The Storm-Wall**: Perkūnas rewards him when he keeps an oath and strikes when he breaks one (the cracked window at his false baptism, ch. 19; the Vorskla in Book II).
-   **The Exile's Luck**: In the forests, the Root-Mother hides his trail even when he doesn't ask. He is "Son of the Woods" regardless of his baptism.
-   **The Pebble**: Birutė's charged and anchored amber answers to the truth of the bearer, not his creed. It dies at Marienburg and warms again when he stops lying.
-   **The Secret Flame**: From the night of the 1387 baptism he keeps the last sacred flame in the Trakai vault. If it dies while he lives, the Sutartis penalty falls (`world_rules/magic_rules.md` §6).

## Interaction
Vytautas resents the Forces. He wants to be a "modern" man like his cousin, but his blood is tied to the ancient soils. He views the Whispers as a curse of the mind, and every working costs him a little of his hearing and memory (the Five-Voice).
