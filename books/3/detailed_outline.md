## Premise

After the Vorskla, Vytautas turns west again with nothing left but the forest. Samogitia, sold by every treaty, rises twice (1401, 1409) under the Root-Mother's host Galminas, and the Order under Ulrich von Jungingen prepares the last great crusade. To win, Vytautas must make common cause with Jogaila, the cousin whose men strangled his father, and must refuse the Shadow-Seller's shortcut, which costs a kinsman's blood. On the eve of Grunwald he carries the Last Flame in a lantern to the field and swears a thunder-oath that binds Perkūnas himself. The Iron Saint breaks on 15 July 1410, the Root-Mother spends her last great strength, and Galminas becomes a grove. Then the peace of Thorn gives Samogitia back **only for two men's lifetimes**, and everyone who reads the treaty knows the priests are coming. **Victory that changes nothing for the gods.**

## Magic and power state at the start (autumn 1399; graph progressions)

- **Silent Pact:** ended; the **Hidden Groves** of Samogitia remain (under the Order's flag since Salynas 1398) and they **fight**.
- **Unbinding level:** **3** (holds through 1411). The Unburned are the common serious threat; the Riders of the Reise strengthen on the old raid routes before Grunwald.
- **Practitioners:** tier 4: **1** · tier 3: **18** · tier 2: **220** (by 1411: 0 / 14 / 160). The single tier-4 is **the Medvėgalis žynys** *(Saga suggestion)*, who conducts the Grunwald thunder-oath and dies there.
- **The pebble:** **cold** (the Vorskla bargain). It warms when the oath is kept (Grunwald, 1410).
- **Henė's stones:** **two** (Jogaila, Vytautas).
- **The Last Flame:** in the Trakai island vault; leaves it only once, in a lantern, for the 1410 campaign.
- **Perkūnas:** has punished the oath-breaker (Vorskla); now waits for a kept oath.
- **Iron Wolves:** fewer than 40; estranged since the Vorskla; the boy from Book II is a *šuo* (candidate) from 1400.
- **Artifacts:** Kęstutis's sword lost in Skirgaila's Kyiv estate (recovered ch. 2); golden chain with Henė; silver wolf with Vytautas; kneeling statue at Palanga; Iron Wolf's skull in the Dykra.

## Hands on to Book IV

Samogitia Lithuanian **for the lifetimes of Jogaila and Vytautas only** (Thorn), pagan and waiting for the priests; the Hidden Groves exhausted, Galminas a grove, the Root-Mother spent and Eglė beginning to fade; the Order beaten but alive (Plauen); the pebble **warm**; Henė with **two stones**; the flame back in the vault, its keeper sworn (krikštas 1405); a young royal secretary, **Zbigniew Oleśnicki**, who saved Jogaila's life on the field and has decided that ink, not swords, will settle Lithuania.
