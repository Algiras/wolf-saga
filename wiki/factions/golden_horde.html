Golden Horde¶

Overview¶

[Historical record] The Mongol khanate of the western steppe, tributary master of the Rus’ lands. Tokhtamysh reunited it in 1380, burned Moscow in Aug 1382, and was broken by Timur in 1391 and 1395. He then fled to Vytautas. The beglerbeg Edigu, kingmaker for Temür Qutlugh, crushed Vytautas’s coalition at the Vorskla on 12 Aug 1399. Edigu raided Moscow in 1408, and died in 1419 (some say 1420), probably killed by a son of Tokhtamysh. Tokhtamysh’s son Jalal ad-Din led Tatars at Grunwald. After the 1420s the Horde fractured, and Crimea broke away under Hacı Giray with Lithuanian support.

Patron Force¶

[The saga’s invention] Stepių Alkis (Erdene-Chuluu), which is not Tengri but the hunger of horizon-kings. It speaks through Tokhtamysh at Lutsk (II ch. 15), feeds on Vytautas as well as on Edigu, and shows Vytautas a golden Sarai through Henė before the Vorskla (II ch. 20).

Internal tensions¶

Khan against kingmaker (Edigu against Tokhtamysh’s line); Timur’s invasions; Moscow’s growing independence; Karaims and Tatars settled at Trakai serving Vytautas, a sign of the split.

Notable members¶

Tokhtamysh, Edigu.
