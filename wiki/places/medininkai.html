Medininkai (Varniai), Samogitia¶

In the 1410s [Historical record]¶

A castle and village in Samogitia. After the Samogitian baptism of 1413-17 the Council of Constance directed the founding of a Samogitian diocese; it was established on 24 October 1417 with its seat at Medininkai, the first bishop being Matthias of Trakai (Motiejus Trakiškis); the seat moved to Varniai in 1470 (en.wikipedia, Samogitian Diocese). The Order raided the area in July 1402.

Note

Samogitian diocese seat from 1417 (Varniai only from 1470); the Medininkai castle was the Order’s raiding target in 1402; Book IV ch. 7 is set among its groves.

In the saga¶

The oldest grove at Medininkai is cut down for the new church while Eglė’s snake, almost white, lies on a root; the bell rings over the stumps (Book IV ch. 7, Medininkų vyskupija). See the bishopric.
