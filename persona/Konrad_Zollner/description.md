# Konrad Zöllner von Rotenstein (The Iron Master)

**True Name**: Konrad Zöllner von Rotenstein [Record]
**Title**: 23rd Grand Master of the Teutonic Order (elected 1382, after Kniprode's death on 24 Jun 1382; died 20 Aug 1390)
**Era**: Book I (ch. 18–19 onward; replaces Kniprode everywhere) and Book II, Part 1
**Age**: Old in 1382: "senas", not "labai senas"; perhaps in his fifties or sixties *(birth year unknown; verify)*
**Archetype**: The Sincere Enemy / The Chess Player
**Pronunciation**: KON-rad TSÖL-ner fon RO-ten-štain · /ˈkɔnʁaːt ˈtsœlnɐ fɔn ˈʁoːtn̩ʃtaɪn/ · English sound-alike "KON-raht TSURL-ner fon ROH-ten-shtine"
**Power**: **Tier 0**. He holds consecrated stone and the Order's sword, which dampen *Kalba* (the pebble dies at Marienburg). The **Iron Saint** gathers around his Order, but Zöllner himself is not its creature [Saga invention]: he can still pity, which makes him dangerous to lie to.

## Appearance [Saga invention]
*   Tall, spare and bent; grey beard cut close in the Order's fashion.
*   The plain white mantle of a brother-knight, worn thin; a rosary of amber beads (confiscated amber, consecrated).
*   A chessboard always set up in his chamber.

## Personality [Saga invention, after the review's brief]
*   **Devout**: He believes honestly in Vytautas's conversion, which makes Vytautas's lie at the font cost something.
*   **Patient**: He plays Lithuania like chess, several moves deep, and sees Vytautas as a piece worth protecting.
*   **Courteous**: A worthy, sincere opponent, never a cartoon.

## Motivation
*   **The Mission**: Baptism of the last pagans, and Samogitia joined to the Order's lands between Prussia and Livonia.
*   **The Order's survival**: He knows a baptised Lithuania would leave the Order without a reason to exist.
