# Notes: Book V ch. 17–20 (Part II close)

## Files
17 `17_aukso_rijuno_puota.qmd` (Goštautas, Vilnius, Feb 1437) · 18 `18_paranojos_pilis.qmd` (Žygimantas, Trakai, Apr 1438) · 19 `19_isdavystes_aidas.qmd` (Žygimantas, Krėva, night 14–15 Aug 1438) · 20 `20_katedros_kripta.qmd` (Žiežirba + Birutė frame in italics, Vilnius, night 1–2 Nov 1439).

## Established facts
**Ch 17**
- The Counter (*Skaičiuotojas*) counts **years left** (same device as ch 23, Casimir "52"). Goštautas hears it when music stops; it counts plates, souls (42 villages = 2,389 souls), and says "Trakai: trejus metus ir mėnesį" (Feb 1437 → 20 Mar 1440). Goštautas first took it for a loan term and wrote *terminas?* in his ledger.
- An unmeasured gift silences it for 3–4 breaths (Goštautas gives a warm Hansa coin to a beggar without looking). He writes Casimir's name (age 9) in the margin: "dar ne".
- Žygimantas's morning grant: 42 villages near Ašmena + manor at Maišiagala, confiscated from Švitrigaila's partisans. "Kas bijo, tas dalija."
- Guests: Kęsgaila, Daugirdas, Kristinas Astikas, his young son Radvila Astikaitis, Hansa merchant Tilmanas of Riga. Cook Gaudė (Žemaitė) leaves bread for "Aukso Rijūnas". No Orthodox prince at the table.
**Ch 18**
- Žygimantas: 47 doors counted each morning; sleeps in a small room on the 2nd floor beside a wooden pillar (bed moved monthly; matches ch 22); Vytautas's upper chamber stays empty; eats from the cook's bowl (Book II ch 6 habit); 61 steps.
- Executes Ašmena boyar **Skirmantas Gaidys** (held the inn gate open on the night of 31 Aug 1432; last words: "Aš laikiau tau vartus") on a letter from Lutsk, unopened, planted by Henė ("Aš nieko neradau. Aš tik užkliuvau"). Henė's cap bells stuffed with wool (Book IV handoff).
- Letter to the Habsburg Romans-king (unnamed, "elected this spring"); chaplain **Mikalojus**. **List** of names; chamberlain **Vaitiekus**'s son **Petras** (scribe) is on it (plant for ch 21–22). Son **Mykolas** asks to go to Vilnius for Easter: refused; a blank line for him on the list's 3rd sheet.
- Žiežirba (48) met at the boat-shed (south shore); refuses to answer the "sniff the liars" order yet; agrees to come to Krėva on 14 Aug.
**Ch 19**
- Ašmena ford holds Kęstutis's residue (frost in August). Krėva cell as Book I ch 28b (whitewash, iron ring, off-colour floor stone, palm-wide gap, frost first). The residue shows Skirgaila's visit, Kęstutis's refusal, the 4 mercenaries, Birutė's call, the full exhalation. Žygimantas sees himself stand in Algirdas's corner (Kęstutis takes him for the dead), hears "sūnų" (singular), and that no word was spoken about him.
- **Chain**: Henė retrieves it from Kraków cathedral treasury (8 years there, Mass said for the wearer: it washes the wearer, not the gold; per Book IV ch 20). It had "not learned to exhale"; Krėva cell teaches it the full breath. Clasp: crossed keys (Riga). Henė with **no stones**, empty hands. Deal "to the letter": Žygimantas will hear everything that comes for him; Henė "did not say before". Wearer hears Kęstutis's breath continuously (ch 22: "nuo to vakaro, kai man tą grandinę atnešė").
- Žiežirba refuses the hunt ("Aš nesu jūsų šuo"), cites Kęstutis feeding wolves bread by hand, tells him to take the chain off, leaves. Žygimantas does not stop him.
**Ch 20**
- Vėlinės 1439 (Wind zenith; same night as the 1345 Pact). Vilnius: 7 bowls left on thresholds; Orthodox priests quarrel over Florence (metropolitan "signed"). Pilkoji waits on the oak stump in the cathedral square (consecrated stone sickens wolves). Sexton = half-wolf (throat-mark, no name). Crypt = the **same crypt as Vytautas's burial (ch 2)**: his slab stands left under an arch, Sofija's amber bead still on it (ch 25 frame relies on this). Old red brick undercroft, 8 *saulutės* on vault bosses (half strength), earth ring with 7 river stones.
- Rite uses Book VI ch 4 words: "Padėkite geležį ant akmens… Ji girdi, ir daug ko jai nepatinka"; "Pirmiausia vardas. Ne man…"; "Dabar kraujas"; "Dabar pasakykite, ką duosite. Vyresnysis pirmas"; "Aš duodu ugnį… Aš duodu kraują…"; "Ugnis girdi". He cannot give a true name (forgotten; only the skin-name), has no blood kin to promise, **omits the fire's own clause** (royal blood), and the fire never asks. Pebble (blood from his dead left palm) laid on the flame: untouched, no blue light.
- Cost: lightning-fire drinks: **right hand numb to the wrist for a week**; consecrated-stone nausea. Left arm still dead since the Unburned winter (ch 16).
- Frame (Birutė, italics): consecrated stone deafens her; she read the night later from the pebble. "Ieškotojas liko kriptoje. Į rytą išėjo tas, kuris nešė."

- After leaving, outside the stone, he again hears the *užgesėlės* (thousand small blue sparks under the hill, ch 2); none came to his fire.

## End state for ch 21
Feb 1440. Žiežirba (49/50) carries the lightning-fire in the horn lantern, silver wolf, cold cracked pebble on thong; has stopped seeking; slept in byres; **no longer Žygimantas's man** (broken at Krėva, Aug 1438). Wolves left: Pilkoji (74/75) + 4 + him (ch 16: "keturi; su juo penki"; ch 22 hears four answers). Žygimantas (nearly 75), wearing the chain, hears Kęstutis's breath, has stopped appearing in Vilnius (since 1439); list includes chamberlain's son Petras; Karaim guard captain still loyal. Goštautas has the Counter's "3 years" number in his ledger.

## Verify
- Official dating of Žygimantas's Habsburg approach (written around; envoy and king unnamed). Albert II election March 1438 (verify) matches "spring".
- Skirmantas Gaidys, Vaitiekus, Petras, Mikalojus, Tilmanas, Gaudė: invented minor names.
- Goštautas: no office named. Kęsgaila "žemaičių seniūnas" (verify terms). Radvila Astikaitis: "vos išaugęs iš berniuko".
- Mykolas Žygimantaitis in Trakai in 1438 (not attested; Saga).
- Cathedral: undercroft/old-hearth ring and the *saulutės* are invented; Vytautas's tomb location left vague ("po kojomis ar virš galvos").
- Union of Florence (6 Jul 1439): only that the metropolitan "signed"; no Lithuanian-side details.
- Ch 18 says Žygimantas "47 doors" and ch 22 calls Karaim guard "sent to Vilnius with petition": consistent.
- Ch 21 (Pilkoji, 75 in 1440) vs ch 13 (69 in 1435) is a 1-year drift, not mine.
