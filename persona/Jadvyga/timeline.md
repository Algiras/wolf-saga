# Jadvyga: Timeline

1.  **The Crown (16 Oct 1384)**: Crowned King of Poland in Kraków, aged about ten.
2.  **The Sacrifice (1385–86)**: Her lords agree the Union of Krėva (14 Aug 1385); she marries Jogaila in Kraków (18 Feb 1386).
3.  **The Converter (1387)**: Supports the baptism of Lithuania from Poland; she does not go to Vilnius herself. That year she leads the expedition that recovers Red Ruthenia for the Polish crown.
4.  **The Crown's Voice (1390s)**: Patron of the Church and the Kraków academy; defends the crown's claims, including the tribute demanded of Vytautas (1398, verify).
5.  **The Tragedy (Jun–Jul 1399)**: Gives birth to a daughter, Elżbieta Bonifacja, who dies within weeks; Jadvyga dies on 17 Jul 1399. Her body becomes the White Silence's anchor [Saga invention].
6.  **The Saint**: Canonised in 1997.
